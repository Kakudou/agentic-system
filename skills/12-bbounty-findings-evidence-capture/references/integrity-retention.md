# Integrity and Retention

## Purpose

Maintain a reviewable relationship between admitted artifacts, their integrity identifiers, access controls, and authorized retention period.

## Preconditions

- The approved store can preserve access restrictions and artifact metadata.
- A retention rule or escalation contact is known.

## Documentation Method

For each admitted artifact, record its stable ID, content identifier or reason unavailable, size where permitted, original/derivative relation, storage classification, and retention disposition. Verify that manifest entries and available identifiers match the artifacts being handed off. Use the [integrity and stop checklist](../assets/integrity-stop-checklist.md).

## Privacy and False-Claim Controls

An integrity identifier demonstrates consistency only for the identified bytes; it does not prove origin, authenticity, completeness, exploitability, or absence of alteration before admission. Do not retain sensitive originals past the governing rule or create broad copies for convenience. Stop and escalate if approved storage or retention is unavailable.

## Evidence and Handoff

Provide the manifest version, integrity-verification result or limitation, custody changes, access restriction, retention deadline or rule, and destruction or transfer instruction where required.
