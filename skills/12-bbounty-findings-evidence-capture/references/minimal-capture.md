# Minimal Capture and Reproducibility Boundaries

## Purpose

Capture only the evidence needed to support an observed claim while clearly stating what another reviewer can and cannot reproduce from it.

## Preconditions

- A claim is written as an observable statement, not an assumed impact conclusion.
- Scope permits retention of the proposed minimum artifacts.

## Documentation Method

For each claim, identify the direct artifact, the authorized context in which it was observed, and the smallest non-sensitive context needed to interpret it. Record prerequisites already known, excluded steps, variables not controlled, and whether independent reproduction is not attempted, partial, or supported by corroborating observations.

## Privacy and False-Claim Controls

Do not convert a capture outline into instructions to repeat an action. Do not claim reproducibility from a single observation, timing coincidence, cached content, or a redacted artifact. Omit payloads, credentials, customer data, and unnecessary state details; request restricted review if they are indispensable.

## Evidence and Handoff

State the claim boundary, direct evidence IDs, required reviewer context, known nondeterminism, and exact authorization needed for any additional validation. Mark unsupported portions `inconclusive`.
