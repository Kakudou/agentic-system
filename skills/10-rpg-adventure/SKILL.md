# Open RPG Adventure Design

`/10-rpg-adventure {seed-or-character-hook}`

Inputs: any imported PC background and goals, system/setting, scale (`short-run` or `campaign`), desired tone, established continuity, unresolved threads.

For a short run, prepare no more than a compelling hook, a few locations and NPCs, one central tension, optional escalation pressure, and plausible payoffs. For a campaign, add recurring factions, long arcs, offscreen agendas, and durable links between runs, but keep the next scene immediately playable. Solo PCs must remain competent protagonists; do not assume a four-person party or insert compulsory companions to fill tactical slots.

Prepare **situations, not outcomes**. Use NPC goals and realistic clocks to make the world move even if the player refuses the mission. Preserve multiple entrances, negotiation and withdrawal options. All opposition has knowledge limits and priorities. Record proposed twists as *GM possibilities* until confirmed by play; no automatic quest-rail enforcement.

Return a concise GM scene packet with public initial conditions and separate spoiler-protected information. Never reveal concealed plans in player-visible narration. Avoid detailed mechanical block design unless the scene actually needs it; use only the selected game's **local Markdown rules** for stats.
