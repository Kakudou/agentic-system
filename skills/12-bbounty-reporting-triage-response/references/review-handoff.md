# Review Handoff

## Purpose

Deliver a bounded, evidence-linked preparation packet to an authorized reviewer for a decision outside this skill.

## Preconditions

- Triage record, classifications, evidence mappings, and privacy review are complete or explicitly stopped.
- An authorized reviewer and approved evidence location are identified.

## Documentation Methodology

Package the report identifier, authorization reference, preserved triage request, classified points, source-linked factual material, confidence, limitations, redactions, and stop events. State one requested decision per unresolved item. Distinguish requested review from any platform outcome.

## Boundary

The handoff does not transmit a platform response, direct communication, negotiate a decision, or authorize retesting. It does not establish report status or acceptance.

## Evidence And Handoff

Use the static handoff template. Include only approved references or sanitized excerpts; identify withheld evidence and the conditions needed for further review.
