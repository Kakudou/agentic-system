# Factual Clarification Preparation

## Purpose

Prepare reviewer-facing factual content that can later inform an authorized response without drafting or transmitting a message.

## Preconditions

- Each point has an evidence mapping, confidence label, and applicable handling decision.
- A reviewer and approved handoff location are known.

## Documentation Methodology

For every requested point, write a neutral factual statement tied to its evidence reference. State conditions, observed result, material limitation, and unknowns. Use exact terminology from the supplied record where it prevents ambiguity. Phrase bounded inferences as inferences and preserve contradictory material. Identify questions that require program or owner interpretation.

## Boundary

This is not a communication template. Do not address a triager, make commitments, advise on timing, recommend negotiation or dispute tactics, or propose retesting. Do not claim acceptance, priority, severity, duplicate status, closure, or reward.

## Evidence And Handoff

Hand off factual points with their citations and confidence labels, plus a list of statements withheld because evidence or authorization is insufficient.
