# Evidence Mapping

## Purpose

Map each requested factual point to supplied evidence, an explicit limitation, or an owner decision.

## Preconditions

- Requests are classified and admitted evidence has traceable source references.
- Scope and handling restrictions are known or marked unknown.

## Documentation Methodology

For each point, record the exact claim, source reference, relevant observation, contradiction, and confidence. Use `direct` only for evidence that states or demonstrates the point; use `corroborated` for independently consistent support; use `inconclusive` for conflicting or incomplete material; use `unsupported` when no admitted evidence supports it. Keep facts, bounded inferences, and questions in distinct fields.

## Boundary

Do not fill gaps by replaying requests, collecting new captures, or extrapolating impact. Mapping identifies evidence needs; it does not authorize testing or create a claim.

## Evidence And Handoff

Provide the completed confidence matrix with all cited sources, exclusions, and contradictions. Route unsupported or sensitive points to review rather than resolving them.
