# Triage Record Interpretation And Evidence Admission

## Purpose

Create a stable record of supplied triage material without treating it as an instruction to act or a final finding decision.

## Preconditions

- A report identifier, authorization reference, and supplied triage material exist.
- The source, date or version when available, and handling restrictions can be recorded.

## Documentation Methodology

Capture the triager text verbatim or as a source locator with a faithful quote. Record speaker role only when supplied, alongside receipt time, report version, stated request, and explicit status language. Separate the triager's statements from the reporter's evidence and from analyst notes. Mark missing context, conflicting record versions, and unverified platform metadata.

## Boundary

Do not access a platform, infer that a status changed, send an acknowledgement, or perform active testing. A triage record is evidence for documentation, not authorization for a response or validation activity.

## Evidence And Handoff

Admit records only with a source reference and handling status. Exclude or flag unattributed excerpts. Hand off the preserved request, context gaps, and admission decision with the completed worksheet.
