# Differential Assessment Stop Checklist

Stop and route to the designated contact when any item applies:

- Written authorization, target boundary, or allowed observation method is unclear.
- The comparison crosses an unapproved account, tenant, host, third party, or time window.
- Evidence contains credentials, tokens, personal data, or other material outside handling rules.
- The next step would create state, affect availability, access another user’s data, or require unapproved interaction.
- Baseline and variation are materially incomparable or provenance is missing.
- A difference depends on unresolved deployment, identity, intermediary, or capture confounders.

Record the stop reason, retained evidence references, redaction status, and owner question. Do not imply confirmation.
