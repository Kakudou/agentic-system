# Bounded Variation Plan

## Purpose

Describe one authorized distinction whose observations may clarify a single security property.

## Preconditions

- A comparable baseline, explicit scope, and permitted observation boundary.
- A stated expected result, disconfirming result, and stop contact.

## Low-Impact Methodology

Name the baseline condition, the one documented distinction, the fields to compare, and the expected behavior. Hold other known conditions constant in the analysis record. Prefer already supplied, normal-use observations; this reference does not authorize new traffic or altered interaction.

## Interpretation And Uncertainty

A plan identifies what a difference could mean, not what it proves. If more than one material condition differs, classify the comparison as confounded or insufficient rather than selecting a favored cause.

## False-Positive And Scope Controls

Do not expand variation after results are known. Do not include state-changing, availability-affecting, cross-account, or third-party conditions unless separately authorized. Stop on unexpected sensitive data or unclear authorization.

## Evidence And Handoff

Preserve the plan, scope decision, expected and disconfirming outcomes, and excluded conditions in the baseline/variation worksheet. Handoff only the minimum question that remains.
