# Evidence And Validation Handoff

## Purpose

Prepare a bounded, reviewable handoff for a difference that remains security-relevant after confounder review.

## Preconditions

- Admitted evidence, scope decision, and an authorized review recipient or channel.
- A factual observed difference with limitations and alternative explanations.

## Low-Impact Methodology

Package only redacted evidence references, comparison conditions, field-level facts, expected behavior, uncertainty, and the minimum next question. State what was not assessed. This handoff does not authorize validation, exploitation, reporting, or disclosure.

## Interpretation And Uncertainty

Use conditional language for impact. A handoff can be appropriate at low confidence when the unresolved question is material and safely bounded; it is not a finding or a severity decision.

## False-Positive And Scope Controls

Do not include secrets, personal data, reusable session material, or third-party content. Do not request validation that exceeds the stated authorization. Mark stopped work and the reason rather than implying completion.

## Evidence And Handoff

Complete `assets/differential-handoff-template.md`, attach the worksheet and matrix references, and route unresolved scope or safety questions to the program-designated contact.
