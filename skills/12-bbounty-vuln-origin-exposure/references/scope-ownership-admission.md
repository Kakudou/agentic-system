# Scope And Ownership Admission

## Purpose And Preconditions

Use before assessing an origin-exposure concern. Require written authorization, an exact asset boundary, allowed sources and methods, exclusions, handling rules, and a stop contact.

## Methodology

Match each supplied observation to the approved asset. Record the authorization reference, source, collection time, collector, method, and secure evidence reference. Admit only the stated asset; provider names, routing labels, certificates, and related domains do not inherit scope or ownership.

## Interpretation And Controls

Publicly observable data does not establish permission or organizational control. Stop for ambiguous wildcard coverage, conflicting ownership information, sensitive data, an unapproved method, or a transition toward provider or tenant infrastructure. Request written clarification rather than collecting more target data.

## Evidence And Handoff

Record the admission decision, exclusions, and stop rationale in the checklist. Route unresolved scope or ownership questions to the program contact before further work.
