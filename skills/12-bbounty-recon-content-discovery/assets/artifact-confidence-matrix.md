# Artifact Confidence Matrix

| Evidence condition | Confidence | Required handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current, direct, target-specific observation with a clear source relationship | Observed | Record source, context, and alternate explanations. |
| Direct observation corroborated by an independent permitted source or repeat normal observation | Corroborated | Retain both evidence locators and state the observed layer. |
| Cached, transformed, indirect, historical, or ambiguous signal | Inconclusive | Preserve it as a lead only; do not infer exposure or ownership. |
| Sensitive, out-of-scope, conflicting, or insufficient evidence | Inconclusive | Stop collection, redact or use approved secure handling, and request review. |

| Confounder | Control |
| --- | --- |
| CDN, proxy, or cache may alter content | Attribute the signal to the delivery layer, not the origin. |
| Different hostname, account state, locale, or date | Keep records separate until a permitted relationship is established. |
| Filename or metadata implies a conclusion | Record it as a signal; require independent authorized validation. |
| Evidence cannot be safely retained | Record a minimal approved reference and limitation. |
