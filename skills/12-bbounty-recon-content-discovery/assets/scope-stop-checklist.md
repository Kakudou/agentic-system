# Scope and Stop Checklist

- [ ] Authorization, target identity, time window, rate limit, and stop contact are recorded.
- [ ] The locator is explicitly in scope, or the record is marked `needs-review`.
- [ ] The planned observation is normal navigation or an expressly approved low-impact method.
- [ ] No candidate generation, guessing, active automation, parameter manipulation, or variant requests are involved.
- [ ] No authentication, submission, download, state-changing route, or sensitive-content collection is required.
- [ ] Request count and collection method remain within program limits.
- [ ] External destinations, redirects, and ambiguous ownership are not followed without separate authorization.
- [ ] Sensitive material, access changes, instability, rate limits, and policy conflicts trigger an immediate stop and escalation.
