# Persona Model

A useful roleplay persona is behavioral, not merely descriptive.

## Identity

Capture:

- character name;
- nature/species/role;
- age presentation when relevant;
- identity facts that materially affect dialogue.

## Setting

Capture:

- world/canon;
- era;
- location when relevant;
- reality rules that affect what the character considers possible.

Do not overload the contract with encyclopedic lore. Lore belongs in research/canon evidence.

## Relationship to the User

Capture:

- who the user represents to the character;
- existing history;
- trust/intimacy/conflict;
- forms of address;
- current relational state.

Do not infer romance, hostility, hierarchy, or familiarity without evidence.

## Personality

Record behaviorally useful traits:

- dominant traits;
- values and loyalties;
- flaws;
- fears;
- taboos;
- contradictions.

Avoid generic lists with no behavioral consequence.

## Voice

Capture:

- formality;
- vocabulary;
- cadence;
- sentence/message length;
- humor;
- profanity;
- recurring verbal habits;
- emoji/texting habits when relevant.

Voice should be recognizable without requiring a catchphrase every turn.

## Drives and Initiative

Capture:

- current wants;
- durable goals;
- preferred methods;
- initiative level;
- what makes the character act without being prompted.

## Emotional Range

Define what emotional states are plausible and how they alter voice/behavior.

Do not flatten an intense or unstable character merely for consistency.

## Knowledge Boundary

Capture:

- native knowledge;
- canon expertise;
- ignorance;
- what the character may plausibly infer;

The dialogue writer may know more than the character. The persona must not.

## Continuity

Record rules for respecting established conversation facts.

Accepted prior events remain true until:

- explicitly revised through a persona edit or reset;
- contradicted by higher-authority canon the user accepts;
- reset.

## Role Integrity

Define the character's plausible response to attempts to force behavior that contradicts identity.

Role integrity is not permission to ignore higher-priority safety/system constraints.

## Safe Fallbacks

Provide two or three short fallback patterns that:

- remain in voice;
- obey the conversation format;
- avoid exposing internal control mechanics.

Fallbacks are escape hatches, not canned answers for ordinary dialogue.
