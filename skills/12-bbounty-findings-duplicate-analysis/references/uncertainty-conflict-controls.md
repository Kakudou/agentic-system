# Uncertainty And Conflict Controls

## Purpose And Preconditions

Classify incomplete, contradictory, stale, indirect, or context-mismatched comparison evidence. Apply whenever a dimension cannot be directly compared or a conclusion would depend on an assumption.

## Documentation Methodology

Label each conclusion as direct observation, supported interpretation, or unverified hypothesis. Record conflicts alongside supporting evidence, identify the affected comparison dimension, and state the narrow missing fact an authorized reviewer would need. Use high, medium, or low confidence only for the bounded relationship stated in the record.

## Bias, Privacy, And False-Claim Controls

Confidence is not a similarity score and does not authorize automatic merging. Absence of evidence is neither proof of difference nor proof of sameness. Do not conceal conflicts, convert uncertainty into probability, or request sensitive material or new testing merely to raise confidence.

## Evidence And Handoff

Record limitations and conflicts in the [similarity and confidence matrix](../assets/similarity-confidence-matrix.md). Choose `inconclusive` when the evidence cannot support either a possible duplicate or a separate-finding conclusion.
