# Evidence And Handoff

## Purpose And Preconditions

Transfer a bounded comparison packet to an authorized recipient without deciding duplicate status, severity, bounty eligibility, remediation, or public disclosure. Complete admission, comparison, uncertainty, and privacy records first.

## Documentation Methodology

Use the [handoff template](../assets/handoff-template.md). Identify the compared findings, scope basis, evidence locators, per-dimension results, same-symptom versus shared-condition boundary, conflicts, and one outcome: possible duplicate for review, keep separate, or inconclusive. State the authorized owner of each unanswered question.

## Bias, Privacy, And False-Claim Controls

Do not reveal other reporters' identities, private report contents, program-internal status, credentials, or restricted artifacts. A handoff is not acceptance or a merge. Do not include unsupported root-cause, exploitation, impact, or priority claims.

## Evidence And Handoff

Send sensitive originals only through the approved channel and reference them elsewhere. Record delivery or receipt only when actually evidenced; no receipt is unknown, not rejection.
