# Evidence Admission And Privacy

## Purpose And Preconditions

Admit only the minimum already captured material needed to compare named finding records. Require written authorization, current program rules, finding identifiers, traceable source locators, and an approved reviewer channel.

## Documentation Methodology

For each artifact, record its source finding, observation window, collection context, scope basis, handling class, and whether it is original, redacted, or metadata-only. Compare redacted identifiers or stable references where they suffice; retain raw material only in its approved restricted store.

## Bias, Privacy, And False-Claim Controls

Do not use another reporter's title, report status, private narrative, or restricted evidence as a shortcut to a duplicate conclusion. Do not copy credentials, tokens, personal data, customer content, or exploit material into the comparison packet. Missing provenance, scope, or handling authority makes the item inadmissible, not corroborating.

## Evidence And Handoff

List excluded evidence and why, preserve approved locators for admitted evidence, and request recipient-side comparison when needed material cannot be safely shared. Stop rather than broaden collection or access.
