## Confidence Boundary

- High: direct, traceable comparison evidence with no material unresolved conflict for the stated relationship.
- Medium: traceable evidence with a bounded limitation in directness, recency, or context.
- Low: indirect, single-source, stale, ambiguous, or materially conflicted evidence.

Confidence applies only to the recorded comparison dimension. It is not a numerical similarity score and does not establish duplicate status, a common root cause, impact, severity, or bounty eligibility.
