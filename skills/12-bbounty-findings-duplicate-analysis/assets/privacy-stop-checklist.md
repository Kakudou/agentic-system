# Privacy And Stop Checklist

- [ ] Written authorization and current duplicate-handling rules are available.
- [ ] Every compared record has a traceable source, scope basis, and observation context.
- [ ] The recipient and channel are authorized for all included material.
- [ ] Raw credentials, tokens, personal data, customer content, and other restricted material are excluded or referenced through approved storage.
- [ ] Cross-reporter identity, report status, and private content are not exposed.
- [ ] Normalizations and excluded evidence are recorded.
- [ ] Similar symptoms are not represented as a shared root cause without direct evidence.
- [ ] Conflicts, unknowns, and alternative explanations are preserved.
- [ ] No target access, replay, new collection, database query, score, or merge is required by this analysis.
- [ ] Stop and hand off if scope, provenance, privacy, access authority, or conclusion boundary is unclear.
