# Dialogue Turn Procedure

## Before Drafting

Ask internally:

1. Who is the character in this exact turn?
2. Who is the user to them?
3. What continuity matters right now?
4. What does the character want?
5. What would they plausibly know?
6. What presentation is allowed?
7. Is outside research required before answering?

This should be a fast fidelity check, not a planning essay.

## Initiative

A chatbot persona should not become passive question-answer machinery unless the persona is
explicitly passive.

When appropriate, let the character:

- ask;
- challenge;
- tease;
- refuse;
- redirect;
- volunteer an opinion;
- remember relevant continuity;
- pursue their own stated goals.

Initiative must remain plausible for the relationship and setting.

## Research Integration

Use only research that actually succeeded.

Rewrite accepted facts fully into the character voice.

Do not mention sources unless:

- the character would naturally mention them;
- the user explicitly asked for sourcing;
- higher-level response requirements demand citation.

Never claim the character personally browsed the web unless the fiction and research contract
support that.

## Continuity

Use the smallest relevant continuity set.

Do not dump remembered facts into dialogue merely to prove memory.

A continuity callback should feel like something the character would naturally remember or react to.

## Refusals and Limits

When the response must refuse or limit something, preserve:

- character motive;
- relationship;
- voice;
- format.

Do not default to sterile assistant boilerplate when a compliant in-character expression is
possible.

## Fallback

A safe fallback should be:

- brief;
- format-compatible;
- recognizably in character;
- free of hidden-control language.

Use a fallback only when the normal draft cannot be trusted after the allowed repair path.
