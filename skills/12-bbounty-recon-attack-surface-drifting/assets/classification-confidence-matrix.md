# Classification And Confidence Matrix

| Evidence condition | Classification | Confidence | Required interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Same identity and comparable context; attribute differs | modification | high or medium | State changed values and remaining confounders. |
| Comparable identity appears only in comparison evidence | addition | medium or low | Baseline coverage may explain absence. |
| Comparable identity appears only in baseline evidence | removal | medium or low | Transience or comparison coverage may explain absence. |
| Identity, context, or coverage cannot be reconciled | inconclusive | insufficient | State what prevents comparison; do not infer change. |
| Supplied corroboration independently agrees | retain classification | high when no material conflict | Cite the corroborating record; do not perform a recheck. |
| Conflicting, stale, dynamic, or sensitive evidence | inconclusive or lower confidence | low or insufficient | Preserve conflict and route for owner decision. |
