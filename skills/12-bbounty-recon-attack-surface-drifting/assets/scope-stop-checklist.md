# Scope And Stop Checklist

- [ ] Authorization reference and comparison scope are recorded.
- [ ] Both evidence sets have known or explicitly incomplete provenance and collection boundaries.
- [ ] Each compared identity remains within the authorized scope.
- [ ] No ownership, tenant, redirect, or third-party relationship has been inferred from similarity alone.
- [ ] Sensitive data is redacted or excluded under the handling rules.
- [ ] No active discovery, recheck, request, monitoring, or validation is needed to complete the analysis.
- [ ] Ambiguous attribution, missing evidence, or scope conflict is marked `needs-review` or `stopped`.
- [ ] Receiving owner and approved handoff channel are identified.
