# Scope and Stop Checklist

## Before Collection

- [ ] Exact target hostname and allowed paths are confirmed.
- [ ] Program authorization covers the planned passive or normal-visit method.
- [ ] Request, time, rate, and retention limits are known.
- [ ] A stop contact or escalation route is available.

## Stop Immediately When

- [ ] Authorization, target ownership, or permitted method is unclear.
- [ ] Authentication, a challenge, a block page, or a rate limit appears unexpectedly.
- [ ] Evidence contains credentials, tokens, personal data, or other sensitive material.
- [ ] The next action would alter inputs, enumerate, probe, or test a control without explicit authorization.
- [ ] Signals materially conflict and passive evidence cannot resolve the conflict.

## At Handoff

- [ ] Evidence is minimized, redacted, and stored only in an approved location.
- [ ] Each claim includes confidence, alternatives, and limits.
- [ ] The recipient is told that active validation requires separate authorization.
