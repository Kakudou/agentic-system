# Scope And Stop Checklist

- [ ] Written authorization, target boundaries, exclusions, and allowed evidence types are present.
- [ ] Evidence handling rules, recipient, and stop contact are identified.
- [ ] Every retained record has an evidence ID, locator, collection time, and scope disposition.
- [ ] Ownership, correlation, and lifecycle/status claims are labeled with confidence and limitations.
- [ ] Out-of-scope, sensitive, and scope-unclear material is withheld or minimized.
- [ ] No active discovery, validation, contact, testing, or exploitation was performed under this skill.
- [ ] Scope ambiguity, provenance failure, or unexpected sensitive content was stopped and escalated.
