# Privacy And Scope Controls

## Purpose

Keep evidence synthesis within written authorization, least disclosure, and recipient handling boundaries.

## Preconditions

- Scope, exclusions, handling rules, and stop contact are available.
- Intended recipients and permitted use are known.

## Method

1. Apply a disposition to every record: `in-scope`, `out-of-scope`, `scope-unclear`, or `withheld`.
2. Redact or omit credentials, tokens, personal data, unrelated tenant information, and unnecessary sensitive infrastructure details.
3. Keep withheld or out-of-scope material out of the inventory; record a minimal reason and escalation path.
4. Stop work and request clarification for authorization conflicts, third-party data, sensitive exposure, or unclear recipients.

## Interpretation And Controls

Public availability is not authorization. Related organizations, brands, cloud providers, subsidiaries, vendors, and shared infrastructure do not enlarge scope. Do not use evidence synthesis as a pretext for new collection, target expansion, contact, testing, or exploitation.

## Privacy, Scope, And Handoff

Use the least sensitive form that supports review. Label the package with its permitted recipient and handling restrictions. Handoff must identify exclusions, redactions, withheld records, stop events, and unresolved scope questions.
