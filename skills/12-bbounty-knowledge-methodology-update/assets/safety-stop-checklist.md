# Safety And Stop Checklist

- [ ] Proposal names an accountable methodology owner and approval recipient.
- [ ] Evidence provenance and source locators are recorded.
- [ ] Proposed wording does not expand authorization or imply target activity.
- [ ] Applicability boundaries and exclusions are explicit.
- [ ] Conflicts with current safeguards, scope, or reporting obligations are recorded.
- [ ] A rollback owner and prior approved reference are known, or the proposal is deferred.
- [ ] Sensitive details are handled within their authorized access boundary.
- [ ] Stop and escalate if authorization, provenance, safety ownership, approval authority, or rollback information is missing.
