# Observed Lesson And Applicability Boundaries

## Purpose

Describe what was observed and where that observation may or may not inform documentation. The goal is calibrated reuse, not a claim of general effectiveness.

## Preconditions

- An admitted evidence record with a known context and source locator.
- A proposed lesson stated separately from the raw observation.

## Documentation Methodology

1. Write the observation as a bounded fact: what happened, in which context, and when.
2. State the proposed lesson in one sentence, using conditional language where support is limited.
3. Define applicability by environment, authorization model, asset or program type, assumptions, and known dependencies.
4. List exclusions, counterexamples, and information that would change the conclusion.
5. Assign a confidence level with a short basis, such as repeated internal observations or a single retrospective.

## Uncertainty And Safety Controls

- Do not convert absence of evidence into evidence of absence.
- Do not generalize across programs, asset classes, or authorization boundaries without explicit support.
- Preserve dissenting or conflicting evidence in the packet.
- If the lesson could weaken safeguards or expand scope, mark it for owner escalation rather than recommending it directly.

## Evidence And Handoff

Complete one matrix row per lesson in the [evidence/applicability matrix](../assets/evidence-applicability-matrix.md). Give reviewers the observation, proposed lesson, boundaries, confidence basis, conflicts, and remaining unknowns.

## Authoritative Sources

- Admitted primary evidence recorded in the proposal
- The applicable program authorization and current methodology scope statement
