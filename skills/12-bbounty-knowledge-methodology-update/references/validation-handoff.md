# Validation Record And Handoff

## Purpose

Package a methodology-change decision so the designated approval/review recipient can verify documentation completeness, traceability, and outstanding constraints without interpreting it as an instruction to test or deploy.

## Preconditions

- Proposal, evidence matrix, applicability boundaries, and safety review are complete or explicitly marked incomplete.
- Approval status and recipient are known.

## Documentation Methodology

1. Check that every proposed claim links to admitted evidence or is labeled unsupported.
2. Check that applicability limits, conflicts, and stop conditions are present and readable.
3. Confirm the approval status, version status, rollback reference, and recipient match the approval record.
4. List missing records and unresolved questions prominently.
5. Transfer the packet with the [approved-change handoff template](../assets/approved-change-handoff-template.md); request acknowledgement of receipt, not implicit acceptance.

## Uncertainty And Safety Controls

- Documentation completeness does not demonstrate effectiveness or authorize execution.
- A missing acknowledgement leaves the handoff pending.
- Do not mark a handoff complete when approval is deferred, safety review is blocked, or a required record is absent.
- Keep sensitive source material within its authorized access boundary.

## Evidence And Handoff

The final packet contains the proposal, evidence/applicability matrix, safety checklist, approval/version/rollback record, and handoff acknowledgement status. Any subsequent controlled action requires its own authorized decision.

## Authoritative Sources

- Completed packet records and owner approval record
- Current approved methodology version and controlled history
