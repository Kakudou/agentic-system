# Shadowrun Sixth World — offline lore

Read [Sixth World lore primer](references/sixth-world.md) for fast lookup; when a detailed published fact or historical date matters read [the full source text](../11-shadowrun-rules/references/offline-source-index.md), particularly printed pp. 14–33 (intro/history), 53–57 (metatypes/lifestyles), 170–201 (Matrix/rigging), 202–228 (critters/NPCs), 230–243 (the streets and security).

Classify each statement as **published canon**, **previously established campaign fact**, **GM-private fact**, **in-world rumor**, **NPC false belief**, or **unknown**. Do not silently promote a rumor, future plot idea, or alternate-edition fact into canon. Bound each NPC's knowledge; a corporate office clerk does not know every conspiracy. An original local location, gang or corporation employee is fine as **campaign-created** fiction, not a quote from the book.

Keep descriptions vivid and character-visible; avoid exposition dumps. No internet, new PDF, or external wiki is required for routine offline play.
