# Shadowrun: Sixth World — local lore and running vocabulary

This is **Shadowrun Sixth World 6E, 2019 core setting**, a dystopian near-future Earth where megacorporations dominate commerce and politics, magic returned, metahuman identities coexist amid inequality, and the Matrix permeates daily life. Major power blocs include corporations, organized crime, law enforcement/security organizations, magical groups, states, dragons and other actors. The PC is typically a shadowrunner operating in the contested spaces between those forces.

## Essential grounded vocabulary

- **Mr. Johnson**: an often-anonymous client representative commissioning a job. Not every job starts with a Johnson.
- **Nuyen (¥)**: common currency. **SIN**: System Identification Number; being **SINless** shapes access, documentation and social standing. Fake SINs/IDs are important tools and risks.
- **The Matrix**: an always-present data/network environment; augmented reality, virtual reality, hosts, ICE/IC, decks, cyberware, drones and technomancy interact with it. **Decker** uses a cyberdeck; **technomancer** accesses the Matrix by Resonance.
- **Awakened** individuals wield or sense magic. **Essence** interacts with augmentation and magical capability. **Astral** phenomena cannot be dismissed as ordinary camera data.
- **Metatypes** include humans, elves, dwarfs, orks and trolls. Treat people as individuals, not fixed personality stereotypes.
- **Street terms** include chummer (friend), drek (mess/excrement), cred (money/reputation), chrome (cyberware), wetwork (assassination), fixer (connector), paydata (sellable information), wageslave (corporate employee), soykaf (soy coffee).
- **Heat**, **Reputation** and **contacts** connect what a runner does to how the Sixth World reacts over time.

## Making lore playable

A location has public look/feel, local power and security, things hidden by architecture, NPC relationships, credible paths and temporal constraints. Corporate and criminal players act according to their actual interests; not all institutions know everything another group knows. Make technology, surveillance, class and magic materially affect choices without revealing private canon as narration.
