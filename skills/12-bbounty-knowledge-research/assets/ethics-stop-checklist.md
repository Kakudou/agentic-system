# Ethics And Stop Checklist

- [ ] Written authorization, scope, permitted source classes, recipient, storage, and retention rule are recorded.
- [ ] The work is documentation-only and does not require target interaction, authentication, or active data retrieval.
- [ ] Each retained source has attribution, locator, access date, and a handling decision.
- [ ] No credentials, tokens, personal data, private content, or confidential material are retained.
- [ ] Copyright, licensing, and source-use restrictions permit the minimal retained material.
- [ ] Claims preserve uncertainty, contradictions, and validation boundaries.
- [ ] Stop and escalate unresolved scope, ownership, permissions, handling, or sensitive-data concerns.
