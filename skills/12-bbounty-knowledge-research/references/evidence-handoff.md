# Evidence And Handoff

## Purpose

Prepare a minimal, reviewable research package that preserves source attribution, uncertainty, and handling limits.

## Preconditions

- Question, authorization, scope, source records, claim records, and confidence decisions are complete.
- The recipient and approved reporting channel are known.

## Documentation Methodology

Review every claim against its cited source and locator. Package the research question, permitted source metadata, claim matrix, direct support, counter-evidence, limitations, and validation questions. Mark the package ready only when scope and handling controls are satisfied; otherwise mark it blocked or escalated.

## Provenance And Privacy Controls

Share only the minimum permitted excerpts and metadata. Do not include raw sensitive data, credentials, confidential content, non-permitted copyrighted text, or operational instructions. Preserve source attribution, handling labels, retention instructions, and approved access restrictions.

## Evidence And Handoff

Use the [knowledge handoff template](../assets/knowledge-handoff-template.md). State that the package is research context, not validation, authorization, or a claim that a target is vulnerable.
