# Uncertainty And Validation

## Purpose

Make confidence, limits, and validation ownership explicit so research context is not represented as a confirmed security finding.

## Preconditions

- Claim records include source locators and documented limitations.
- An authorized reviewer or validation path is identified for claims needing more support.

## Documentation Methodology

Rate confidence only in relation to documented source support: high for specific, current, authoritative corroboration; medium for credible but incomplete or single-source support; low for indirect or stale support; unresolved when provenance or applicability is missing. Record counter-evidence and one minimal validation question. Validation is a separate, explicitly authorized activity and is not performed by this skill.

## Provenance And Privacy Controls

Never convert confidence into proof. Do not assign CVSS, CWE, severity, reward, exploitability, target applicability, or disclosure status from research alone. Keep reviewer identities, restricted sources, and sensitive context in approved channels only.

## Evidence And Handoff

Provide confidence rationale, conflicts, limitations, validation owner or requested authorization, and the blocked status when no safe validation path exists.
