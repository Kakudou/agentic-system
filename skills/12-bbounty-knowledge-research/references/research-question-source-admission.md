# Research Question And Source Admission

## Purpose

Bound research to an authorized question and admit only attributable sources suitable for documentation.

## Preconditions

- Written authorization, current scope or disclosure policy, and an approved research recipient exist.
- The question identifies the decision it will inform without requiring target interaction.

## Documentation Methodology

State one question, its allowed topic and time boundary, and the facts needed to answer it. For each proposed source, record publisher, title, stable locator, publication or version date, access date, and why it is relevant. Admit the source only if its origin and permitted use are clear; otherwise mark it unresolved or exclude it.

## Provenance And Privacy Controls

Scope silence is not permission. Do not obtain material through authentication, target interaction, circumvention, or collection of personal, credential, or confidential data. Respect access terms, copyright, licensing, retention, and disclosure constraints.

## Evidence And Handoff

Provide the question, authorization and scope citations, source-admission decisions, exclusions, and unresolved permissions. Admission supports documentation only, not testing or a security conclusion.
