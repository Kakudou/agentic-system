# Claim Extraction And Uncertainty

## Purpose

Convert admitted source material into atomic, attributable claims without overstating what the sources establish.

## Preconditions

- Each source has a provenance record and an exact locator.
- The research question and permitted handling boundary are recorded.

## Documentation Methodology

Write one claim per record in neutral language. Link each claim to its source IDs and exact locator, then separately record direct support, assumptions, conflicting sources, missing context, and the smallest unanswered validation question. Quote only the minimum necessary text. Label a claim `supported`, `corroborated`, `uncertain`, or `contradicted`; do not silently merge incompatible statements.

## Provenance And Privacy Controls

Do not infer target ownership, configuration, impact, severity, or root cause from general research. Exclude operational instructions, personal data, credentials, confidential material, and non-permitted copyrighted content. Preserve attribution for every retained excerpt.

## Evidence And Handoff

Attach completed claim entries and the confidence matrix. Clearly separate source facts from analyst interpretation and list contradictions and validation needs for the reviewer.
