# Sensitive-Data, Copyright, And Scope Controls

## Purpose

Prevent research documentation from exceeding authorization, exposing sensitive material, or violating source-use restrictions.

## Preconditions

- Authorization, source-use terms, approved storage, recipient, and retention rule are known.
- The researcher can stop and escalate to the authorized owner.

## Documentation Methodology

Before retaining a source or claim, classify it as shareable, restricted, or do-not-retain. Record only the minimum metadata or excerpt needed for review. Stop when material indicates credentials, tokens, personal data, private content, confidential program information, ambiguous ownership, or a source that requires new access or interaction. Escalate the fact of the issue through the approved channel without copying restricted content.

## Provenance And Privacy Controls

Respect program scope, safe-harbor terms, law, copyright, licensing, robots or access restrictions, and retention policy. Do not bypass access controls, authenticate, retrieve material in bulk, or reproduce operational content. A public locator does not remove confidentiality, attribution, or handling obligations.

## Evidence And Handoff

Record the handling decision, stop reason, scope citation, minimal safe metadata, escalation recipient, and retention disposition. A handoff does not authorize follow-up collection or testing.
