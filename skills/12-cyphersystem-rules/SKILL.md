# Cypher System 2025 — complete locally embedded SRD

**The full 2025 Cypher SRD Markdown snapshot from the user's `csrd-main.zip` is embedded in this skill's `references/csrd-2025/content.en/`.** Source integrity and coverage are recorded in the [manifest](references/csrd-2025/CATALOG-MANIFEST.md) and [complete navigation](references/csrd-2025/CONTENT-INDEX.md). For any exact named option, invoke [`12-cyphersystem-catalog`](../12-cyphersystem-catalog/SKILL.md) and read the original local entry. These sources are authoritative over earlier condensed play notes. No rules lookup needs a network connection.

For a quick turn, focused local procedures remain available: [rules engine](references/rules-engine.md), [combat/recovery](references/combat-recovery.md), [XP/intrusions](references/experience-intrusions.md), [character management](references/characters-abilities.md), [gear and cyphers](references/cyphers-gear.md), [GM principles](references/game-mastering.md). These are **brief aids, not substitutes for the source text**.

**Turn:** player declares narrative intention. GM determines when uncertainty requires a check; consult relevant stat Pool, training/specialization, assets, Effort and Edge from the exact canon. Account for pre-roll player decisions and costs. Resolve d20 through player-provided roll or authorized native `rpg_dice` tool, including special rolls and GM intrusions. Apply outcomes to the Markdown event log and sheet through Fuhyō; Ōshō tells the story. Never generate a fictitious die face, insert Shadowrun d6 mechanics, or force a player into an unrequested numerical menu.

**Offline:** all listed catalog families and optional genre modules are local. Only activate genre modules declared for the campaign; do not quietly combine incompatible variants. Character creation, advancement, actions, combat, armor, recovery, worldbuilding, artifacts/cyphers, creature/NPC reference and Cypher Shorts are available in the original Markdown. The full ZIP source is not a claim about other commercial supplements not licensed in the SRD.

**Source limitation:** original Markdown may retain layout shortcodes and any source transcription errors. For an ambiguous field use the [readable source mirror](references/csrd-2025/readable.en/_index.md) and re-check the original Markdown. Do not manufacture rule certainty.
