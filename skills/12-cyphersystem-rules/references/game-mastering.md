# Cypher — GM procedures and genres (offline)

Cypher is a rules **engine**, not one canonical world. At session start choose setting and whether the tone emphasizes exploration, wonder, social drama, intrigue, horror, superheroes, science fiction, fantasy, historical, modern, cyberpunk, Weird West, post-apocalyptic or mixed play. Avoid treating fantasy as the default if player brings a cyberpunk solo PC.

## Constructing a short run

- Take a live PC desire or relationship; create an inciting situation and opposing agenda, two or three meaningful locations, credible NPC wants, a discovery worth pursuing and 1–3 world clocks. Keep options open, including delay, compromise, lie and retreat.
- Set NPC levels and modifications appropriate to the **world**; a creature typically uses its level for attacks, defense and related skills except listed level adjustments. The PC's tier does not magically increase every opponent's level.
- Use **GM intrusion** as an accepted complication with proper XP/refusal, not an unearned inevitability. Keep revelations, hidden motives and player knowledge separate.
- Let successes change the situation materially. Failure can cause cost/time/attention instead of instantly stopping the story. Do not overwrite the official d20 result unless the player explicitly accepted another resolution policy.

## Genres and mechanically justified changes

- **Fantasy**: magic, artifacts, mystical creatures, politics; magic abilities remain the listed type/focus abilities or approved homebrew.
- **Science fiction**: technology ratings, devices, vehicles and altered environments. Advanced tech is not automatically available to every character.
- **Modern and cyberpunk**: social surveillance, networks, equipment and corporations; Cypher rules remain d20 and Pools, not Shadowrun's rules simply because the scene has hackers.
- **Horror**: dread, uncertainty, stress and limited knowledge; ask player boundaries before introducing extreme themes and do not use mechanical horror modules without their exact write-ups.
- **Superheroes or post-apocalypse**: power scale, supplies and community consequences; anchor high-impact abilities in specific character records.
- **Other genres**: same core difficulty/action engine, setting-specific content created transparently at the table.

## Solo session behavior

No mandatory sidekick. Social scenes work through NPC agency rather than scripted branching dialogue. Character progression is based on documented choice and XP earned. Solo XP-sharing intrusion adaptation must be chosen once and written to the session contract. If actual random dice cannot be supplied, the assistant must not impersonate a roller.
