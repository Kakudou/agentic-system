# Cypher — characters, types, descriptors and focuses

## Shape of a PC

A PC description combines **type** (mechanical approach), **descriptor** (identity and traits) and **focus** (distinctive signature action) in the standard Cypher wording, such as “a [descriptor] [type] who [focus].” The setting may also use flavor, species, background and equipment. At creation a character has **tier 1** and three stat Pools (**Might, Speed, Intellect**) each with current/max values and corresponding **Edge**. Track **Effort rating**, skills/inabilities, character abilities with exact activation costs/limits, Armor, health states, cypher limit and cypher inventory, equipment, recovery rolls and XP.

## Type archetypes

The SRD's broad types include **Warrior** (physical action), **Adept** (special powers), **Explorer** (discovery and versatility), and **Speaker** (social influence). Many campaigns reskin these through flavors or genre modules. Starting Pools, Edge, skills, abilities, cypher limits and tiers depend on exact options: **do not claim an official character creation is complete if only a name and type are known**.

## Skills and actions

Skills are defined by what they reasonably cover; **trained** eases one step, **specialized** eases two. An **inability** hinders one; trained and inability typically cancel to neutral. Not all weapon types are automatically practiced; follow type/ability restrictions. Choose the action's stat according to how it is attempted, not only its name: forcing a door may be Might, agile bypass Speed, reading its circuit Intellect.

## Creating or importing

1. Prefer an imported complete sheet; reproduce **exact fields** in the session copy. Preserve unknown values explicitly.
2. If starting fresh, jointly choose genre, type, descriptor, focus and any flavor/abilities **supported by the local catalog or user-provided sheet**. If an official ability is absent from this condensed pack, avoid inventing a verbatim SRD ability description.
3. Record all current/max Pools, Edge, Effort, armor, damage track, recovery, skills, inventory, number/levels of cyphers, XP, arc and connections.
4. Character progression requires paid XP, valid tier/options and player's decision.
5. An existing Cypher PC from a real group remains source-authoritative; changes in a private solo run stay in its forked session copy unless explicitly adopted back into the original campaign.

**Scope:** a play-focused guide rather than the complete multi-hundred-entry official ability, focus and descriptor compendium. Original homebrew can be supplied by a table, recorded and played offline; it must be labeled homebrew.
