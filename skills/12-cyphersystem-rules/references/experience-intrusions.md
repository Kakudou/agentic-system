# Cypher — XP, GM intrusions and advancement

## GM intrusions

An intrusion is a **proposed complication** or surprising development that tests player agency; it is not automatic punishment for a plausible player action.

- A regular GM intrusion awards **2 XP**; the player keeps **1 XP** and **gives 1 XP to another player**, explaining the gift. If the player has at least 1 XP, they may **spend 1 XP to refuse**; then they do not receive the 2 XP. No pretense that a second human player exists in a solo session.
- In a **solo campaign**, decide at session setup how to handle the 'give 1 XP to another player' rule. Recommended explicit house option: solo PC receives 1 XP and the second XP is not created; alternatively bank the second XP as a communal resource agreed by the player. Label this a **solo adaptation**, not official RAW.
- On a **natural d20 roll of 1**, the GM can intrude with **no awarded XP**. Clearly distinguish it from a regular XP-awarding offer.
- An intrusion is not an excuse to negate a previous choice unfairly, enforce a railroad, reveal hidden GM material, or force a moral choice. Give a player enough information to understand what is being accepted/refused within the fiction.

## XP earned through character arcs and discoveries

- XP can reflect discoveries, milestones and character arcs; the PC chooses personal arcs and their meaning. An arc can reward progress without ultimate success. A typical arc may grant **2 XP** for each step, **4 XP** for a successful climax (**2** for unsuccessful climax), and **1 XP** for resolution, when that structure applies.
- Advance only using earned XP and player-approved choice. XP also supports certain immediate or short-term benefits; do not spend it silently on a reroll or fictional convenience.

## Character advancement to tier 2–6

A player purchases **four different advancement categories at 4 XP each** during a tier; each only once before reaching the next tier. Standard categories:

1. **Increase Pools** by **4 total points**, distributed at player's choice.
2. **Improve one Edge** (Might/Speed/Intellect) by **1**.
3. **Increase Effort rating** by **1**.
4. **Add/improve one non-attack, non-defense skill** (trained, then specialized).

Permitted type/focus/special alternatives may replace a category when their prerequisites are met. Completing all four categories reaches the next tier and grants appropriate type/focus tier benefits; do not invent a missing ability's text. Maximum standard tier 6; handling beyond it is a table-specific question.

**Example ledger entry**: `E-0042: Regular intrusion offered; player accepted; +1 XP retained (solo adaptation declared in session contract); no other player's XP created; narration consequence: ...`
