## Coverage

The package includes **all 36 Markdown files from `csrd-main.zip/content.en/`**, including complete catalog chapters for character types, flavors, descriptors, foci, abilities, equipment, full 2025 genre rules, creatures, NPCs, cyphers, game mastering, supplementary rules, and Cypher Shorts. They are original copied files under [`csrd-2025/content.en/`](csrd-2025/content.en/_index.md), with generated indexes and a readable mirror. File-by-file SHA-256 and byte counts are in [`CATALOG-MANIFEST.md`](csrd-2025/CATALOG-MANIFEST.md).

Completeness means **every source Markdown file in the uploaded repository ZIP**, not every paid Cypher sourcebook or a independently verified word-for-word equivalence with the original published 2025 DOCX. An ambiguous or garbled original section is a point for GM review, not a blank check to invent a rule.
