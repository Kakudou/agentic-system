# Cypher — cyphers, artifacts and equipment

**Cyphers** are single-use, scene-changing resources: devices, curiosities, supernatural tricks or other one-off effects depending on genre. A character's **cypher limit** is determined by the sheet; track item name, level, effect, activation, target/range, charges (usually single use), whether it is subtle/manifest as appropriate, and source of acquisition. Using a cypher generally consumes it; do not quietly reappear it on the next scene. Cypher levels and item descriptions may have specific ranges or exceptions; the local sheet's explicit entry governs when present.

**Artifacts** typically have continuing effects and a possible depletion check after use; depletion rates are not universal and must be read from the artifact. **Equipment** includes armor, weapons, tools, supplies, devices, currency/valuables and genre-specific resources. Mechanical effects are only granted to named capabilities when their item write-up supports them. Track quantities, worn/held/accessibility, encumbrance where relevant, and currency deltas.

## Safe immediate-use illustrative original cyphers (homebrew, not claims of canonical SRD entries)

- **Echo Lens** (level 3): for one scene, let the character hear faint acoustic events from the last several minutes near a touched surface; cannot recover speech behind soundproof barriers. This is a one-use in-world investigative clue, not an assurance the player will discover every secret.
- **Graveline Thread** (level 4): create a taut, short-lived line between two visible nearby anchor points for a scene; its strength is level 4 for relevant climbing or breaking attempts.
- **Mnemonic Paper** (level 2): remembers one legible page laid against it and reveals it to the user when unfolded; doesn't penetrate encrypted screens or hidden knowledge.

Use a homebrew cypher only when player/table accepts original content. Never pass it as an official listed SRD item. For exact canonical entries not included locally, use a sheet the player supplies or avoid the unsupported catalogue name.
