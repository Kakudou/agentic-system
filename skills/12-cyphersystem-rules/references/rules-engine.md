# Cypher System — offline core rules

## Rule of play

1. The player describes what the PC wants to do and how. The GM decides whether the task is routine or if meaningful uncertainty exists.
2. For uncertainty, set a **base difficulty** based on the world and task, independent of PC tier, skill and equipment. Difficulty 0 is automatic. Determine **Might** (strength, endurance), **Speed** (agility, reflex, ranged attacks), or **Intellect** (knowledge, social interaction, mental powers).
3. Apply player inability (hinders by one), skill **trained** (eases one) or **specialized** (eases two, total maximum from skills is two), and other situational steps. **Assets** can ease at most two steps. Then the player may elect **Effort** (typically one difficulty step eased per level; Effort ease cap six steps, subject to maximum Effort score and abilities). Easing total from skill + assets + Effort never exceeds ten steps.
4. Effort costs **3 Pool points** for the first level, **+2** per additional level: 1=3, 2=5, 3=7, 4=9, 5=11, 6=13. For an appropriate stat Pool, subtract that stat's **Edge once per action** across relevant cost(s); never create Pool points by having cost become negative. Some abilities grant a free Effort level, as specified on the sheet. An impaired character pays an additional 1 point per level of Effort. The action may also have an initial Pool cost and/or ability activation cost, all adjudicated under the 'Edge once per action' rule. Record choices before roll.
5. Effective difficulty ≤0: no roll, success (except certain GM intrusions). Otherwise target number = difficulty × 3; player rolls d20 and succeeds if result meets target. A task with target above 20 is impossible *without further easing or applicable roll modifiers*; natural 20 is not a universal automatic success against difficulty 7–10.
6. Record outcome, costs, special roll, harm and fictional changes. Player controls subsequent choices.

## Natural-roll special results

- **Natural 1:** free GM intrusion (no 2-XP award); do not invent another random roll.
- **17:** +1 damage on a successful damage-dealing attack.
- **18:** +2 damage on a successful damage-dealing attack.
- **19:** minor effect OR +3 attack damage; minor effect should meaningfully benefit PC and be mutually agreed.
- **20:** major effect OR +4 attack damage; if Pool points were spent for the action, refund them according to the rule (no net Pool cost for that action). Describe the effect with player input.
- Special results are based on the **natural die face**, not a modified total. When impaired, 17–20 additional attack damage is reduced and the PC ignores minor/major effects as described in `combat-recovery.md`.

## Action economy and repeat attempts

- A round spans roughly 5–10 seconds. A character generally takes **one action per turn**; movement interacts with the action (consult specific ability). An action may be an attack, an activation, moving a significant distance, assisting, defending as appropriate, or another significant attempt.
- After failure, **retrying the same task** generally requires applying at least one Effort level; there must be a feasible new attempt and time/fiction may change. A new approach can meaningfully change the circumstances. Do not force meaningless rolls for repeated idle actions.
- The GM can assign an **initial cost** to attempting strenuous actions; it is not an Effort discount. Edge may reduce costs, but **once per action**.
- GM chooses routine versus uncertain; this is not a universal 'roll for lockpicking' rule. Example: a competent locksmith may open an ordinary lock with time, good tools and no urgent pressure without rolling.

## Player-facing text style

Good: “The office door is sealed with a surprisingly advanced mechanical lock. You can feel a false set in the pins; it may take time, and the patrol is returning.”

Not the default: “Roll Speed DC 15 to lockpick.”

When Effort would be possible: “You can take your time or push yourself for a better shot—do you want to spend some Speed?” Show the exact Pool cost when a decision is required; don't conceal resource loss.
