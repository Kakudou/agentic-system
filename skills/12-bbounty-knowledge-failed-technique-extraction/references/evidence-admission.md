# Approved Evidence Admission

## Purpose

Admit only already-observed material that may be retained and discussed under the engagement's written authorization. Admission establishes documentation eligibility, not correctness or causation.

## Preconditions

- A current scope and authorization reference.
- A source reference and observation time or an explicit reason either is unavailable.
- A known handling classification and authorized storage and recipient.

## Documentation Method

For each candidate record, assign an identifier and record its source, observer or system context, observation interval, scope relation, and handling class. Preserve the smallest excerpt or metadata needed to support a later statement. Mark excluded material and why it was excluded.

## False-Claim and Privacy Controls

Do not admit reconstructed recollections as direct observations. Do not infer omitted request content, target state, authorization, or a result from a label such as "failed." Do not include credentials, session material, personal data, private content, or operational details unless their handling is explicitly approved; prefer redacted summaries or metadata-only references. Stop when source, scope, or handling is unclear.

## Evidence and Handoff

Carry the authorization reference, source identifier, observation context, handling decision, and exclusion notes into the learning record. Flag missing provenance or restricted material for the authorized owner rather than resolving it locally.
