# Constraint and Confounder Analysis

## Purpose

Bound learning claims by documenting conditions that may have influenced an observed discrepancy, including missing evidence and credible alternative explanations.

## Preconditions

- A completed expected-versus-observed comparison.
- Available context about authorization, environment, timing, identities, instrumentation, or records, or an explicit statement that it is unavailable.

## Documentation Method

List constraints as facts about the record, such as an incomplete observation window or unavailable authoritative state. List confounders as plausible influences, not findings. For each, state the supporting evidence, the affected claim, and whether it reduces confidence, creates an exclusion, or requires authorized review. Record alternatives alongside, rather than beneath, a preferred explanation.

## False-Claim and Privacy Controls

Do not use root-cause language unless a separately authorized owner provides evidence sufficient for it. Do not attribute behavior to a person, team, control, product, or target condition from a single ambiguous record. Avoid adding target-specific operational details. Stop if resolving a limitation would require new testing or access.

## Evidence and Handoff

Transfer constraints, confounders, alternatives, and evidence gaps with the affected learning statement. Identify the owner or authorization needed for any follow-up; no follow-up action is implied by the handoff.
