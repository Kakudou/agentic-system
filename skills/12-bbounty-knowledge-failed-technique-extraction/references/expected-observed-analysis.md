# Expected Versus Observed Analysis

## Purpose

Create a reviewable comparison between the documented expectation and the actual recorded observation. The comparison describes a discrepancy; it does not prove why it occurred.

## Preconditions

- Admitted evidence with a source reference.
- A stated expected condition derived from an approved plan, requirement, or prior documented assumption.
- A stated observation tied to an identified record and time context.

## Documentation Method

Quote or paraphrase the expectation and observation separately, each with its source reference. State the comparison in neutral terms, such as "the recorded response did not match the documented expectation." Complete the [expected/observed confidence matrix](../assets/expected-observed-confidence-matrix.md) with the evidence coverage and confidence rationale.

## False-Claim and Privacy Controls

Never rewrite an expectation after seeing the observation. Do not label an observation a success, failure, block, mitigation, or defect unless the admitted record directly supports that label. Preserve unknowns such as incomplete logs, timing ambiguity, or unavailable context. Redact identifiers and sensitive values not required to explain the comparison.

## Evidence and Handoff

Include both statements, their citations, the comparison boundary, and confidence rationale in the learning record. Hand off a discrepancy as a review question when evidence does not support a stronger conclusion.
