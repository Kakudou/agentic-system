# Validation and Knowledge Handoff

## Purpose

Perform a documentation review and transfer a bounded learning record to an authorized recipient. Validation here checks that statements are supported and properly bounded; it is not retesting or confirmation of an approach.

## Preconditions

- A completed static learning record with source references.
- An approved recipient, channel, and handling restriction.
- No unresolved stop condition.

## Documentation Method

Review every factual statement against an admitted source. Confirm expected and observed statements remain separate; that confidence, confounders, alternatives, exclusions, and privacy decisions are present; and that the record contains no operational instructions. Mark the result as `reviewed`, `needs-clarification`, or `stopped`. Transfer with the [knowledge handoff template](../assets/knowledge-handoff-template.md).

## False-Claim and Privacy Controls

Do not treat review as proof of technical cause, exploitability, impact, or general applicability. Do not ask the recipient to replay, retest, or extend the approach through this record. Share only the permitted redacted record and identify restricted supporting material by reference.

## Evidence and Handoff

Include the review date, reviewer role, disposition, unresolved questions, source references, handling restrictions, and recipient. A recipient may accept the documentation while leaving the learning claim qualified or unresolved.
