# Expected/Observed Confidence Matrix

| Element | Record | Evidence status | Confidence effect | Required note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Expected condition | Source and exact statement | Directly sourced, partially sourced, or unavailable | Missing source lowers confidence | Why this expectation was reasonable |
| Observed condition | Source, time context, and statement | Directly observed, summarized, or unavailable | Indirect record lowers confidence | Observation boundary |
| Comparison | Neutral difference statement | Supported, ambiguous, or unsupported | Ambiguity prevents strong learning | What is and is not compared |
| Constraints | Known record limits | Present or unknown | Limits reduce confidence | Affected statement |
| Alternatives | Plausible explanations | Considered or unavailable | Unconsidered alternatives cap confidence | Why unresolved |

Use `high` only for directly sourced, sufficiently contextualized comparisons with no material unresolved limitation. Use `medium` when the comparison is supported but materially bounded. Use `low` when evidence is indirect, incomplete, or has plausible alternatives. Use `unknown` when the record cannot support a confidence judgment. These labels do not establish causation, reproducibility, or exploitability.
