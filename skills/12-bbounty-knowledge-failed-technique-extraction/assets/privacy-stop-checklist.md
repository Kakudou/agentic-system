# Privacy and Stop Checklist

- Confirm authorization, scope, approved storage, recipient, and retention rule.
- Confirm every statement has an admitted source reference or is labeled unknown.
- Remove credentials, tokens, session data, personal data, private content, and unnecessary target identifiers.
- Replace restricted evidence with a redacted summary or metadata-only reference.
- State uncertainty, constraints, confounders, alternatives, and exclusions.
- Stop and escalate if scope, provenance, handling, consent, recipient authorization, or retention is unclear.
- Stop if completing the record would require replay, retesting, new access, or a new observation.
