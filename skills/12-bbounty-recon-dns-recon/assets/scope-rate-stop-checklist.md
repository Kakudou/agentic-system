# Scope, Rate, and Stop Checklist

- [ ] Authorization reference identifies the assessor and engagement.
- [ ] Exact zones and individually permitted names are recorded.
- [ ] Permitted methods are limited to passive sources and explicitly authorized low-impact observations.
- [ ] Rate cap, observation window, and evidence location are recorded.
- [ ] Out-of-scope provider domains and forbidden actions are recorded.
- [ ] Stop contact and escalation channel are recorded.
- [ ] Stop on unclear scope, rate-limit signal, sensitive data, instability, provider boundary, or owner instruction.
- [ ] No guessing, brute force, transfer requests, response-pattern evasion, service interaction, or account actions occur.
