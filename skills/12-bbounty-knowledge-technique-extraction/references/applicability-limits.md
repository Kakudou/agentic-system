# Applicability And Assumption Boundaries

## Purpose And Preconditions

Use after a neutral technique statement exists. Require evidence that identifies the relevant environment, configuration, identity or role boundary where applicable, and observation limits. The goal is to prevent overgeneralization.

## Documentation Methodology

For each claim, list supported conditions, necessary assumptions, known exclusions, counterexamples, and unknowns. Distinguish a source's environment from environments merely thought to be similar. Use the evidence/applicability matrix to map each condition and limit to a cited observation.

## False-Claim And Privacy Controls

Absence of contradictory evidence is not proof of broad applicability. Do not infer ownership, production equivalence, version equivalence, or impact from names, banners, or generic technology labels. Generalize identifiers only when doing so does not obscure a material boundary; otherwise redact and state the limitation.

## Evidence And Handoff

Mark applicability as supported, conditional, unsupported, or unknown. A record with material unknowns remains useful only when those unknowns are prominent in the handoff. Route questions about scope or environment to the evidence owner rather than filling gaps from external assumptions.
