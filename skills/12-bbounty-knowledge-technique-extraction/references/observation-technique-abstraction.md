# Observation-To-Technique Abstraction

## Purpose And Preconditions

Use only after evidence admission. The purpose is to describe a defensive assessment concept, not a sequence of actions. The source set must contain enough context to distinguish an observed condition from an inference.

## Documentation Methodology

Write three separate fields: observed fact, interpretation, and neutral technique statement. The statement names the condition or control relationship being assessed and the evidence category that could support it. Describe inputs and outcomes at a conceptual level only. Link every component to its source evidence and retain contradictory observations.

## False-Claim And Privacy Controls

Do not include commands, payloads, bypasses, automation, target-specific paths, or procedural instructions. Do not label a concept as proven because one observation is consistent with it. Avoid absolute terms such as "always," "guarantees," or "exploitable" unless the admitted evidence explicitly establishes that limited conclusion.

## Evidence And Handoff

Assign confidence based on evidence quality, independence, and completeness, not on anecdote count. Hand off the abstraction with its source references, counterevidence, and unresolved questions so reviewers can audit the boundary between fact and interpretation.
