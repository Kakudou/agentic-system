# Approved Evidence Admission

## Purpose And Preconditions

Use before extracting any knowledge claim. Require written authorization, defined scope, permitted source types, handling restrictions, and a named escalation contact. Each candidate observation must identify its source, collection context, and relationship to the authorized work.

## Documentation Methodology

Record the observation verbatim or as a clearly labeled minimal paraphrase. Attach a stable evidence reference, collection time, collector or originating artifact, relevant asset or environment, and any transformation made during redaction. Admit observations individually; related systems, historical artifacts, and third-party material do not inherit authorization.

## False-Claim And Privacy Controls

Do not admit summaries without traceable source support. Do not convert an observation into a claim of vulnerability, impact, ownership, or reproducibility. Exclude secrets, credentials, personal data, customer content, and unneeded target identifiers. Stop for uncertain scope, conflicting provenance, restricted material, or an unclear retention right.

## Evidence And Handoff

Mark each item as admitted, excluded, or awaiting clarification and preserve the reason. Hand unresolved admission decisions to the authorization or program contact; no technique record is completed from excluded evidence.
