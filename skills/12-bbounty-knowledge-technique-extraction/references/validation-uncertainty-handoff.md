# Validation, Uncertainty, And Knowledge Handoff

## Purpose And Preconditions

Use to review a completed documentation record and transfer it to an authorized knowledge owner. Require a complete record, cited admitted evidence, stated applicability limits, privacy review, and a named recipient. This is a documentation review, not live-target validation.

## Documentation Methodology

Check that every factual statement has an evidence reference and that inferences are marked as such. Review whether the technique statement is neutral, applicability conditions are complete, limitations are visible, and the confidence rating reflects the evidence. Record reviewer questions and the final disposition: documented, reviewed, needs clarification, or stopped.

## False-Claim And Privacy Controls

Do not upgrade confidence based on plausibility, popularity, or similarity to another report. Do not represent a review as confirmation that a technique works in any new environment. Recheck redactions and recipient authorization before transfer; stop if a claim depends on unavailable, conflicting, or restricted evidence.

## Evidence And Handoff

Send the static technique record, evidence/applicability matrix, redaction status, limitations, and unresolved questions using the knowledge handoff template. The recipient decides whether to retain, revise, or reject the knowledge; the handoff itself establishes no new fact.
