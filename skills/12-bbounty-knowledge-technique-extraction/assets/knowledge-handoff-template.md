# Knowledge Handoff Template

```markdown
# Technique Knowledge Handoff: <title>

## Recipient And Authorization

- Recipient: <authorized knowledge owner>
- Authorization reference: <reference>
- Record ID: <record_id>

## Bounded Technique Statement

<neutral, non-operational statement>

## Evidence And Confidence

- Evidence references: <references>
- Confidence: <low | medium | high>
- Review status: <documented | reviewed | needs-clarification | stopped>

## Applicability And Limits

- Supported conditions: <conditions>
- Assumptions: <assumptions>
- Exclusions and unknowns: <limits>

## Privacy And Handling

- Redaction status: <none | redacted | restricted>
- Handling caveats: <caveats>

## Recipient Action

- Retain, revise, reject, or request clarification: <decision>
- Unresolved questions: <questions>
```

The handoff transfers documentation for review. It does not assert new facts or authorize testing.
