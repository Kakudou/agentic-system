# Privacy And Stop Checklist

- [ ] Written authorization, allowed evidence sources, and handling restrictions are recorded.
- [ ] Every retained observation has provenance and a necessary documentation purpose.
- [ ] Secrets, credentials, session material, personal data, customer content, and unnecessary target identifiers are removed or referenced only through an approved restricted channel.
- [ ] Facts, interpretations, assumptions, and unknowns are visibly distinct.
- [ ] No payloads, commands, bypasses, automation, or reusable attack procedures are included.
- [ ] Work stops for unclear scope, conflicting evidence, uncertain classification, unexpected sensitive material, or an unauthorized recipient.
- [ ] The stop reason and escalation contact are recorded.
