# Stop-Condition Checklist

Before validation, confirm each item has a named recipient and immediate cease-work action.

- [ ] Scope, asset identity, authorization window, or permitted method is unclear or changes.
- [ ] Unexpected personal data, credentials, secrets, or customer content becomes visible.
- [ ] Service degradation, instability, error increase, or availability concern appears.
- [ ] Authentication, authorization, or account state behaves unexpectedly.
- [ ] A third-party service, asset, person, or data path would be involved.
- [ ] The planned volume, concurrency, account, data handling, or condition would exceed authorization.
- [ ] Evidence cannot be captured minimally and safely.
- [ ] Any observation requires an unapproved escalation to interpret or reproduce.

On any checked condition: stop, record minimal context, and notify the approved contact. Do not continue to classify the behavior.
