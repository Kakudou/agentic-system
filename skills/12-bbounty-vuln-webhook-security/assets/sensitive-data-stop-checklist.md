# Sensitive-Data And Stop Checklist

- Confirm written scope, ownership, allowed evidence sources, retention rules, and stop contact.
- Record metadata and redacted locators only.
- Stop if secrets, tokens, personal data, payment data, private destinations, or unrelated tenant information appears.
- Do not decode, validate, forward, retain, or reuse sensitive material.
- Stop on unintended delivery evidence, system instability, rate limiting, or scope conflict.
- Record the stop decision and notify the designated recipient through the approved channel.
