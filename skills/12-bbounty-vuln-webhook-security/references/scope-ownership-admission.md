# Scope And Ownership Admission

## Purpose And Preconditions

Use before recording a webhook boundary. Require written authorization, exact in-scope systems, allowed evidence sources, exclusions, a stop contact, and an identified handoff recipient.

## Methodology

Admit only owner-supplied documentation, approved captures, program inventories, or prior authorized artifacts. Record sender and receiver ownership independently, including provider-operated components. Treat an unverified relationship as unverified rather than inferring control from a hostname, brand, or integration name.

## Interpretation And Controls

Authorization to assess one party does not authorize access to its counterpart or provider. Stop on conflicting ownership, ambiguous scope, or evidence suggesting a third party. Do not discover endpoints, inspect accounts, or contact systems to resolve uncertainty.

## Evidence And Handoff

Record the authorization reference, scope decision, artifact locator, collector, timestamp, ownership assessment, and exclusions. Escalate unresolved ownership to the named program contact.
