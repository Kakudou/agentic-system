## Logo and Product Marks

- **[DR]** Keep a protected area around the logo equal to the illustrated lowercase **“n”**; no graphic or text element may enter that area. Source: [Logo](https://zeroheight.com/020822383/p/172a6b), accessed 2026-08-29.
- **[DR]** Use the color logo on light backgrounds or images and the white logo on dark backgrounds or images, always optimizing legibility. The emblem may use its line or solid version when use or legibility requires it. Source: [Logo](https://zeroheight.com/020822383/p/172a6b), accessed 2026-08-29.
- **[DR]** Do not recolor with unsupported/non-palette colors, rotate, distort, alter proportions, or place the logo illegibly on photographs, colored fields, or backgrounds that are too light. Source: [Logo](https://zeroheight.com/020822383/p/172a6b), accessed 2026-08-29.
- **[DR]** Prefer the current official Logo Kit and Product Logo Kit. The current 2025 product kit includes OpenCTI, OpenAEV, and XTM Hub marks. Sources: [Logo](https://zeroheight.com/020822383/p/172a6b), [Resources](https://zeroheight.com/020822383/p/141a98), accessed 2026-08-29.
- **[DR]** “By Filigran” is used when a product logo is integrated into a context different from Filigran. The source leaves consequential edge cases ambiguous; retain that ambiguity and request brand-owner direction. Source: [Logo](https://zeroheight.com/020822383/p/172a6b), accessed 2026-08-29.
- **[IA]** Never redraw, trace, extract, or approximate a logo from screenshots. Record the approved kit filename/version and supplied instructions.
- **[IE]** No official numeric minimum logo size or comprehensive formal co-branding system was found in the reviewed Identity pages listed in the [Official Identity Map](#official-identity-map), accessed 2026-08-29. Do not invent either; the “n” is a supplied visual clear-space reference, not permission to derive an unsupplied millimetre/pixel value or geometric construction.

## Imagery and Iconography

- **[DR]** Prefer realistic, authentic visuals that show people engaged in conversation, exchange, or listening and express a human, open-source, community-and-innovation spirit while retaining seriousness, rigor, and cybersecurity expertise. Source: [Iconography and photography](https://zeroheight.com/020822383/p/39fdbe), accessed 2026-08-29.
- **[DR]** Avoid overly dark visuals, hoodie hackers, generic padlocks, walls of code, and other cybersecurity clichés. This is imagery and cliché-selection guidance, not a universal light-mode rule; a selected dark canvas still requires the approved white-on-dark logo. Source: [Iconography and photography](https://zeroheight.com/020822383/p/39fdbe), accessed 2026-08-29.
