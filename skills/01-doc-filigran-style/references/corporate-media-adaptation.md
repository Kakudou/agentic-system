# Corporate-Media Adaptation

Load this reference for every medium-specific specification, creation-guidance request, or audit. Corporate Identity supplies the brand frame; Product UI Foundation is conditional supporting evidence. Unless a current brand-owner instruction says otherwise, medium choices here are `[IA]` or `[LEC]`, not official media standards.

## Shared Model

- **[IA]** Decide and record the identity-expression branch before layout: `corporate editorial`, `product UI-inspired technical`, or supplied/custom. Inspect supplied visual references and their intended authority before choosing light or dark when references exist; otherwise record `none supplied` and derive the choice from approved instructions, corporate identity, content, audience, medium, and legibility.
- **[IA]** Corporate authority governs marks, exact corporate palette roles, typography roles, imagery, and messaging; it does not imply a light canvas. Corporate-first is not light-first.
- **[IA]** Start with one clear hierarchy: context, dominant claim, evidence/explanation, then action/source/status.
- **[IA]** Use Geologica for display hierarchy and IBM Plex Sans for sustained reading; select medium-readable sizes rather than UI-scale typography.
- **[IA]** Use exact corporate values for corporate palette roles. In the selected product-UI-inspired branch, source-recorded UI values may govern non-brand surfaces and introduce semantic/status/entity colors only when representing those concepts accurately and distinguishably.
- **[IA]** Build hierarchy with type, space, position, authentic imagery, and layered surfaces; use restrained geometry and borders as secondary structure.
- **[IA]** Cards become content panels, badges become metadata/status chips, dialogs/toasts become warning or decision callouts, and tabs become section labels only when the static state is clear.
- **[IA]** Remove hover, focus, pressed, cursor, disclosure, and button cues when no interaction exists. Never imply false clickability.
- **[IA]** Preserve meaning without color alone through labels, shape, pattern, position, or icons.
- **[LEC]** Record exact dimensions, margins/safe areas, grid, density, image crop, chart grammar, export, and recurring motif for the project.

## Presentations

- When a product-UI-inspired technical expression is requested or supported by matching supplied references, load [Product-UI-Inspired Technical Presentation Style](technical-presentation-style.md) and use its conditional branch logic. Do not make dark mode universal.
- **[IA]** Use supplied dimensions/aspect ratio. If `16:9` is chosen without supplied authority, label it `[LEC]`; it is not a Filigran brand rule.
- **[IA]** Give each slide one dominant message or communicative job, with Geologica hierarchy, IBM Plex Sans body, modular panels, and a clear evidence/source/CTA layer.
- **[IA]** Design for viewing distance. Do not copy tiny UI typography, dense dashboard arrangements, or unreadable source notes into slides.
- **[IA]** Essential meaning must be visible without hover, progressive disclosure, or animation.
- **[IA]** Use approved imagery/marks and the documented “n” protected area; optimize logo variant for background legibility.
- **[DR]** Use the exact approved white logo on dark fields and the exact approved color logo on light fields/images; never recolor, redraw, or screenshot-extract it.
- **[IA]** In the technical branch, keep layers and radii restrained, use only two or three elevations, and supplement every status color with labels, shape, pattern, icon, or position.
- **[LEC]** Define masters/archetypes, title-safe zone, footer/source system, maximum density, and section transitions.

Verify full-screen and overview/thumbnail reading; representative large-room/projector legibility where available; minimum type and density budget; chart labels and non-color cues; sources; alternative descriptions where supported; clipping, overlap, fonts, aspect ratio, and representative display export. Use spare vertical space before shrinking type. Keep unavailable checks `UNVERIFIED`.

## Reports and One-Pagers

- **[IA]** Optimize for actual-size reading, scanning, evidence traceability, and predictable digital pagination.
- **[IE]** The inspected official [CTEM Market Survey Report](https://filigran.io/ctem-market-survey-report), accessed 2026-08-29, presents a title/subtitle, immediate CTA, two or three headline statistics, topic sections, and methodology/provenance. These are observations about that artifact, not corporate identity law.
- **[IA]** A three-bullet executive summary is a useful adaptation when content supports it, not a normative Filigran requirement.
- **[IA]** Keep headings with following content and figures with captions/sources where export tooling permits. Keep dates, status, citations, legends, and ownership metadata legible.
- **[IA]** Use panels for summaries, metrics, warnings, or decisions without making them look clickable.
- **[LEC]** Set digital page dimensions, columns, running elements, body measure, page budget, figure treatment, and export profile. Production print remains excluded.
- **[IE]** No official report page size was found in the official Identity corpus enumerated in [Provenance and rights](provenance-and-rights.md#source-register--corporate-identity), reviewed 2026-08-29. This is a bounded reviewed-source finding, not a universal or permanent claim.

Verify at 100% and common zoom levels; reading order; selectable/searchable text where expected; links/bookmarks/tags if required; image alternatives; fonts; clipping; page breaks; format/file size.

## Diagrams and Infographics

- When a product-UI-inspired technical expression is requested or supported by matching supplied references, load [Product-UI-Inspired Technical Presentation Style](technical-presentation-style.md); its UI tokens may guide non-brand surfaces only under that selected branch.
- **[IA]** Use exact corporate values wherever corporate palette roles apply. Under the selected `product UI-inspired technical` branch, source-recorded UI values may govern non-brand surfaces; use status/entity/AI colors only when the represented semantics match and remain distinguishable.
- **[IA]** Define a reading path using labels, grouping, connectors, layers, lanes, and compact geometry. Use labels/icons in addition to color.
- **[IA]** Translate panels/chips/callouts into bounded information groups, classifications, warnings, decisions, or stages while preserving data meaning.
- **[IA]** Label nodes, connectors, states, scales, units, dates, denominators, timeframes, sources, and confidence where relevant.
- **[IA]** Keep evidence separate from interpretation; reduce crossings and preserve directional consistency.
- **[IA]** Avoid compact UI copy, clipped or overlapping labels, excessive layers/radii, and controls that imply interaction. Use spare space before reducing type.
- **[LEC]** Define notation, connector style, legend, detail threshold, aspect ratio, and companion-detail strategy.

Verify at intended size and stated viewing distance, including thumbnail/projector views when relevant; check density, overlap, grayscale/color-deficiency simulation where practical, text alternatives/extraction, topology clarity, source linkage, non-color cues, absence of fake UI, and that decorative treatment does not distort data meaning.

## Social Graphics

- **[IA]** Use one focal claim, one limited support layer, and essential source/date/CTA metadata for feed-speed and small-screen comprehension.
- **[IA]** Adapt approved authentic imagery and logo variants while preserving documented clear space.
- **[IA]** State URLs/handles or genuinely linked text rather than drawing fake buttons.
- **[LEC]** Define platform-specific dimensions, crop-safe areas, text budget, image crop, campaign variants, compression, format, file size, and naming.
- **[IE]** No official social canvas sizes were found in the official Identity corpus enumerated in [Provenance and rights](provenance-and-rights.md#source-register--corporate-identity), reviewed 2026-08-29. This is a bounded reviewed-source finding, not a universal or permanent claim.
- **[IA]** Create platform crops from a common hierarchy rather than stretching one canvas.

Verify realistic mobile-feed size, likely crops/previews, claim comprehension without caption text, alternative-text handoff, logo legibility, compression, and exported dimensions.

## Corporate Assets, Icons, Product Marks, and Loader

- **[DR]** Use current approved Logo/Product Logo/Media kits and corporate type resources via the official Identity pages. Corporate marks from those kits outrank UI package assets.
- **[DR]** Apply the color logo on light fields/images and white logo on dark fields/images; use line/solid emblem variants only as documented and approved for legibility.
- **[IA]** For UI/catalog icons, use `currentColor` behavior only as implementation context; choose final corporate/UI color by the applicable domain and add a text label when meaning is not obvious.
- **[IA]** For static media, use an approved static mark instead of `FiligranLoader`. Never freeze an incomplete loader frame. Skeleton is a generic placeholder, not a branded motif.
- **[LEC]** Record each icon/product-mark/loader decision, source, asset filename/version, color role, label/alternative, rights, and approval.

## Imagery and Copy

- **[DR]** Select realistic, authentic, human visuals showing exchange/listening/community/innovation while retaining seriousness, rigor, and cybersecurity expertise; reject dark hacker, generic padlock, code-wall, and related clichés. Source: [Iconography and photography](https://zeroheight.com/020822383/p/39fdbe).
- **[DR]** Ground narrative in the current [Manifesto](https://zeroheight.com/020822383/p/98eb1a), exact [Core values](https://zeroheight.com/020822383/p/927539), and freshly retrieved [Boilerplate](https://zeroheight.com/020822383/p/29743a). Do not copy the manifesto wholesale or freeze changing company/product statistics.
- **[IA]** Keep copy concise and evidence-led; distinguish claim, support, status, consequence, and source.
- **[LEC]** Set tone, capitalization, localization, abbreviation, number, CTA, and length rules from the deliverable context; do not call these a comprehensive Filigran voice system.

## Audit Ratings

Rate each criterion, never the artifact as one undifferentiated score:

- `PASS`: inspectable evidence meets the cited criterion.
- `FAIL`: inspectable evidence contradicts or misses it.
- `UNVERIFIED`: evidence or inspectability is insufficient.
- `NOT_APPLICABLE`: the criterion does not apply.

Keep separate: sourced rules; adaptation consistency; general quality/accessibility; brand-owner approval. Approval remains `UNVERIFIED` without supplied approval evidence. A polished artifact may be unapproved, and an approved asset may still have accessibility defects.
