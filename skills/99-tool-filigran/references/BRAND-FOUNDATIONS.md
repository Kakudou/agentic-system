# Filigran Brand Foundations

## Evidence levels

This skill distinguishes three kinds of guidance:

- **CANONICAL** — explicitly documented by Filigran UI.
- **OBSERVED** — visible on current official Filigran corporate web properties.
- **ADAPTATION** — recommended translation for media not directly covered by the design system.

Never present an OBSERVED or ADAPTATION rule as an official corporate guideline.

## Brand character

### CANONICAL / product-facing

Filigran UI exists to standardize and unify interfaces across Filigran cybersecurity products. Its stated goals are consistency, efficiency, scalability, customization, accessibility, responsiveness, and reusable components.

The library is React-oriented, Tailwind-friendly but not Tailwind-dependent, and includes a dedicated icon library.

### OBSERVED / corporate-facing

The current corporate site expresses the brand through:

- very clear large headlines;
- short, action-oriented messaging;
- strong white/dark contrast;
- electric blue and turquoise/cyan gradient energy;
- occasional violet/indigo transitions;
- high-quality product UI imagery;
- metrics and proof points;
- customer logos and testimonials;
- a strong ecosystem/integration story;
- restrained cybersecurity symbolism rather than cinematic "hacker" tropes.

The visual tone is polished B2B cybersecurity: technical enough for practitioners, clear enough for executives.

## Design thesis

A Filigran artifact should feel like **clarity applied to complex threat data**.

Design for:

- signal over noise;
- operational confidence;
- interoperability and openness;
- measurable outcomes;
- modern cybersecurity expertise;
- enterprise credibility without enterprise blandness.

## Composition

Prefer a clean grid and generous hierarchy.

For marketing/corporate surfaces:

- strong hero statement;
- concise supporting paragraph;
- clear CTA pair when appropriate;
- proof/evidence soon after the promise;
- modular sections with large breathing room;
- product screenshots or diagrams as proof, not decoration;
- ecosystem/integration density later in the narrative.

For product UI:

- information density may be high;
- keep controls predictable;
- reserve visual emphasis for task priority and state;
- use component-level consistency rather than editorial spectacle.

## Geometry

The brand language is clean and contemporary rather than aggressively angular.

Use:

- tidy cards and containers;
- moderate corner rounding consistent with live components;
- clean separators;
- simple icon containers;
- controlled gradient borders only where the system supports them;
- modern responsive grids.

Avoid importing cyberpunk/HUD geometry into Filigran corporate work unless explicitly requested for a one-off campaign.

## Imagery

Preferred imagery supports product credibility:

- real UI/screenshots;
- diagrams of threat/exposure workflows;
- product/ecosystem illustrations;
- professional editorial photography when relevant;
- abstract gradient forms that support rather than replace content.

Avoid generic stock "hacker in hoodie" imagery.

## Motion

Motion should feel smooth, premium, and informative.

Good uses:

- gradient movement on special actions;
- gentle reveal of platform layers;
- diagram/path activation;
- product walkthrough emphasis;
- carousel or integration movement;
- small hover/focus feedback.

Avoid noisy glitch, rapid shake, or excessive parallax in corporate material.
