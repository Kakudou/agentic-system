# Shadowrun Sixth World — playable offline rules

This is **Sixth World, sixth edition (2019)**, not Shadowrun 5E or Anarchy. No website or installed PDF is needed: the supplied 322-page source text is embedded at [offline source index](references/offline-source-index.md). Load focused chapters rather than all source pages into context. For routine actions begin with [Core Mechanics](references/core-mechanics.md). For specialized play use [Combat](references/combat.md), [Magic](references/magic.md), [Matrix](references/matrix.md), [Rigging](references/rigging.md), [Equipment](references/equipment.md), [Characters](references/characters.md), and [World Lore](../11-shadowrun-lore/references/sixth-world.md). These are embedded Markdown, not network pointers.

**Procedure:** identify intent, relevant fictional stakes, whether a roll is necessary, 6E attribute + skill and appropriate opposing test or threshold, applicable modifiers, Attack/Defense Rating comparison and Edge where relevant, player-controlled resource spend, actual dice supplied by player or the native `rpg_dice` plugin, then hits/net hits/glitches, apply consequences, and record receipts. All timing-sensitive decisions happen before the roll. Track harm, ammunition, nuyen, contact costs, Heat, reputation, magic/Matrix status and scene clocks only when warranted by evidence.

**Presentation:** say what the character attempts and what it feels like. Do not foreground numerical thresholds. A player can ask for any math, exact rule, or receipt; keep a complete audit trail in `mechanics.md`. The GM never lies about a die result. If neither manual dice nor actual RNG is available, do NOT report a random outcome. Ask for player faces or offer a separately agreed **nonrandom narrative adjudication** recorded as a house rule; never call it official random resolution.

When two quick-reference notes conflict or a complex exception matters, consult the exact embedded source pages. **For all PDF tables**, consult [verified table transcriptions](references/table-corrections.md) or the [spatially preserved 322-page source](references/layout-source-index.md); the older flat extraction can drift across columns. Read the [audit and limitations](references/table-audit.md) and flag remaining uncertainty. Do not invent missing fields or fuse 5E and 6E rules. This module is a private transformation of user-provided copyrighted material, not a distributable open SRD.
