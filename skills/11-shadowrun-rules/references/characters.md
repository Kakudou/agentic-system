# Shadowrun Sixth World 6E — sheet import and advancement

**Offline authority:** embedded source printed pp. 53–103, 243 (Karma) and 244–303 (gear).

The existing character sheet always wins over a generic archetype. Preserve: name/pronouns/metatype; body/agility/reaction/strength/willpower/logic/intuition/charisma and special attributes; skills and specializations; Magic or Resonance if present; qualities, cyberware and Essence; contacts, lifestyle, equipment, nuyen, Karma, Edge, wounds, ammo, spells/powers/programs; relationships and campaign history. Distinguish **current** and **maximum** tracks.

Character generation in 6E uses priorities for metatype/adjustment points, attributes, skills, magic/resonance and resources, followed by qualities and customization Karma; do not construct an official sheet from prose without the needed selections. Sample archetypes in the supplied book are valid examples but are not mandatory player templates.

For a solo run, import a Markdown rendition of the player's existing sheet into the session. Never alter the original or award out-of-campaign permanent Karma without player or real GM authorization. Track a hypothetical earned reward separately until accepted. Active Edge state, lifestyle commitments, Heat and damaged cyberware can matter beyond combat.
