# Sixth World 6E — quick mechanical play guide

**Edition:** 2019 sixth edition, not 5th edition or Anarchy. **Offline authority:** [all PDF pages embedded locally](offline-source-index.md); use full chapters for rule exceptions.

## Basic tests

- Roll a pool of six-sided dice, usually **attribute + skill** with applicable modifiers. Every die showing 5 or 6 is a **hit**; count hits, do not add dice together except where a rule expressly says otherwise (for example initiative).
- **Simple test:** hits >= GM-established threshold. **Opposed test:** both parties roll their relevant pools and compare hits, using the action-specific tie rule (do not guess a universal tiebreaker). **Extended test:** multiple rolls accumulated over intervals and against a threshold/limit when specified; time, retries and penalties matter. Teamwork uses a leader and assistants with the specific helping rules.
- **Glitch:** more than half the dice in a rolled pool show 1 (verify the exact book wording and dice-manipulation exceptions in embedded pages). **Critical glitch:** glitch with no hits. A glitch is an additional story complication, not automatic failure if sufficient hits were obtained; check official exceptions.
- **Edge** is a limited tactical resource, not Cypher's permanent stat Edge. Compare relevant Attack Ratings and Defense Ratings for possible Edge, and apply situational Edge as specified; obey gain/spend/cap timing. The player controls optional Edge expenditures; ask before rolling where the chosen boost/action requires it. Track pre- and post-Edge separately. Consult embedded source chapters around printed pages 44–49 for all boosts and actions.
- Resist and mitigate damage with the actual 6E process: armor contributes to Defense Rating; it is **not** rolled as damage soak. Distinguish Physical versus Stun, threshold/effects of wound penalties, damage resistance and overflow. Do not import 5E armor soak.

## Running common scenes

- **Exploration**: approach, observable obstacle, tool/knowledge, threshold or active opposition, consequences/time; many descriptive actions are automatic.
- **Stealth, locks, security**: identify actual security level and mechanism; validate effective kit, skills and access; consider alarms, patrols and time, not generic arbitrary DC.
- **Social**: NPC motives and leverage matter; use relevant Con/Influence/etc opposed tests only when outcome remains uncertain. Social success cannot rewrite an NPC's total personality or instantly grant impossible compliance.
- **Combat**: minor/major actions, initiative, declaration, AR/DR/Edge, attack, defense, damage resistance, outcomes, condition and ammo. See `combat.md`.
- **Matrix**: distinguish real-world Electronics tasks, illegal Cracking actions, devices, hosts, Overwatch Score and IC; see `matrix.md`.
- **Magic**: tradition, type, legal spell/power, opposition, Drain, spirits and astral scope; see `magic.md`.
- **Rigging**: vehicle and drone control, noise/links, vehicle damage; see `rigging.md`.
