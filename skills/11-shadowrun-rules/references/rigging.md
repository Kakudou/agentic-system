# Shadowrun Sixth World 6E — vehicle and drone operations

**Offline authority:** embedded source printed pp. 196–201 and gear pp. 295–303.

- A rigger may operate via ordinary controls, remote control or a control rig; different actions use different pools/limits and interfaces. Verify ownership, link, noise, access, sensor and rig/console configuration.
- Character vehicle/drone sheets should include model, condition, speed/handling, installed sensors/software, mounts, ammo, damage and current control method.
- Handle chase, maneuvering, crash, collision, vehicle combat, drone initiative and remote observation with the actual embedded subsystem rather than arbitrary 'driving DC'.
- Keep vehicle actions and pedestrian actions synchronized in time, and do not retroactively place an unmanned drone somewhere because a scene needs it.
- A solo runner can use a drone as a genuine tool without inventing companion player characters.
