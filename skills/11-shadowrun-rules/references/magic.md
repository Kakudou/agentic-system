# Shadowrun Sixth World 6E — magic desk guide

**Offline authority:** [embedded core](offline-source-index.md), printed pp. 126–169; critters, powers and gear elsewhere.

Magicians, adepts, aspected magicians, mystic adepts, spirits, magical traditions, astral perception/projection, spellcasting, ritual magic, enchanting, summoning/banishing and magical objects are separate but interacting features. Record the character's actual Magic/Resonance, qualities, tradition, spells, reagents, foci and Essence, if applicable. Do not grant a spell or tradition merely because it would be dramatic.

**Casting sequence:** the player describes an intended magical effect; check that their sheet supports the actual spell or ability and its target, visibility/range, area, duration, category and preconditions; determine an appropriate test and defense according to the exact spell; allow pre-roll legal choices; resolve hits/effect; then separately resolve **Drain** and relevant sustaining/visibility consequences. 6E no longer uses the old universal 'Force declaration' model; do not reintroduce it from an earlier edition.

**Astral and spirits:** mind the distinctions between perceiving, projecting, manifesting/materializing, dual-natured targets, astral barriers, spirit services and their etiquette. A remote spirit is not omniscient, and a physical persona is not automatically available in the astral plane.

**At the table:** describe magic through sensory and social consequence, preserving the Sixth World's blend of magic and corporate technology. For exact spell ratings, drain formulas, extended durations, spirit types, adept powers, wards and ritual sequences, load the relevant embedded pages before adjudicating.
