# Shadowrun Sixth World 6E — Matrix desk guide

**Offline authority:** [embedded source](offline-source-index.md), especially printed pp. 170–195.

- The **Matrix** is a pervasive network connecting personas, devices, hosts and services; Augmented Reality, virtual reality, cyberdecks, cyberjacks, commlinks, technomancers and wireless equipment have distinct capabilities and risks.
- Describe what the runner sees in AR/VR first. Ordinary data searches, legitimate use, device control and illegal intrusions have different prerequisites, skills, action types and consequences. Do not let generic 'hacking' bypass access, skills, encryption or host architecture.
- Distinguish **Electronics** from **Cracking** actions, legal access from illegal access, hacking pathways (such as Brute Force and Backdoor Entry), host security, IC, reboots, dumpshock, biofeedback, link locking and damage.
- Track **Overwatch Score** where appropriate; GOD Convergence can occur and is not an arbitrary GM attack. Consult exact tables/action descriptions and their page positions before committing rolls or risks.
- Matrix tests often have defender pools and ratings. Calculate legal attack/defense ratings, relevant Edge and action-specific modifiers, including noise and wireless conditions. Validate abilities and installed programs actually present on the runner's sheet.
- On success record only access actually gained, commands actually executed and any resulting notices, evidence or trace; on failure preserve time, escalation or continuing visibility if the rules imply them.
- A non-decker can use ordinary devices; do not silently grant illegal cyberdeck/technomancer actions. A decker cannot instantly override magical protection just by claiming a hack.
