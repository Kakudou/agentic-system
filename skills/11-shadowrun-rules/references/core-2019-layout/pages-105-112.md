                                                  No one likes being cold. The shakes, chattering

                                               hit by a cold-based attack. Taking damage from
                                               an attack with a Cold damage type inflicts the

                                                  Once you’re hit by an attack that involves fire,
                                               the fire ignites flammable objects, including you.
                                               This effect means the fire burns for several rounds
                                               after the first. Taking damage from an attack with
                                               a Fire damage type inflicts the Burning status.
                                                  Gamemaster note: The damage and duration

                                               a higher threshold to extinguish.
