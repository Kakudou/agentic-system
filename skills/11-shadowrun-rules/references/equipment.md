# Shadowrun Sixth World 6E — gear, nuyen and loadouts

**Offline authority:** embedded source printed pp. 244–303, plus character creation pp. 58–68.

Maintain actual named gear and ratings on a sheet: weapons, ammunition and magazines, armor and modifications, IDs/SINs and licences, medkits, commlinks/cyberdecks, programs, vehicles/drones, magical foci and reagents, cyberware/bioware and Essence, tools and security gear. Track the actual 6E weapon **Attack Rating by range band**, Damage Value, firing mode, capacities, costs, modifications and special effects. Do not substitute 5E gear statistics.

**Transactions:** prior cash, cost/reward, contact availability, favour/credit and any Heat/reputation consequence. Never award or spend money merely because it seems narratively appropriate. Check availability/legality, size and gear restrictions when meaningful. Scavenged gear is not always sellable at listed price.

The original full equipment chapter (including vehicles/drone and weapon lists) is embedded in Markdown. For exact manufacturer stats, consult the specific source file rather than guessing; source tables may have broken columns due to PDF extraction, so explicitly raise an uncertainty if numbers cannot be reliably aligned.
