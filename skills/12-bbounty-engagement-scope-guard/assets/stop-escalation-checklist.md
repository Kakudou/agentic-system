# Stop/Escalation Checklist

- [ ] Stop the affected activity.
- [ ] Do not reproduce, broaden, automate, or seek a workaround.
- [ ] Record time, asset, proposed action, and stop trigger.
- [ ] Retain only redacted, minimum-necessary evidence references.
- [ ] Identify the authoritative scope source and conflicting or missing language.
- [ ] Prepare one bounded authorization question.
- [ ] Use the documented program or platform channel.
- [ ] Mark the decision `stopped` or `unresolved` until written authorization is recorded.
