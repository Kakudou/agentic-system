# Ambiguity And Conflicts

## Purpose

Handle incomplete, contradictory, or changed scope language without expanding authorization by interpretation.

## Preconditions

- The relevant admitted sources and their retrieval metadata.
- A documented proposed activity whose status cannot be determined directly.

## Documentation Process

1. State the exact conflict or missing term, separating quoted source language from interpretation.
2. Apply the most specific current restriction provisionally; record the decision as `unresolved` when sources still do not yield a clear answer.
3. Prepare one bounded question that names the asset, action, rate, and data boundary without attaching sensitive artifacts.
4. Record the approved answer only when it arrives through the program's documented contact or platform channel, with date and source reference.

## Uncertainty Controls

- Never resolve ambiguity using DNS, ownership indicators, past reports, forum advice, or an assumed intent.
- A third party cannot widen program authorization.
- Treat changed or withdrawn scope as a new decision; earlier authorization is not automatically portable.

## Evidence And Handoff

Hand off the conflicting excerpts, source dates, provisional no-action decision, and the exact clarification requested. Keep the issue open until an authoritative answer is documented.
