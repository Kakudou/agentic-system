# Scope-Source Admission

## Purpose

Decide whether a source can establish an engagement boundary. This is documentation review, not asset validation.

## Preconditions

- A program identifier and the source as published by the program or its authorized platform.
- A retrieval date and a stable locator, such as a policy URL, platform page, or dated written authorization.

## Documentation Process

1. Record publisher, locator, retrieval time, version or last-updated marker, and relevant quoted text.
2. Prefer the program's current policy, scope table, or written authorization over summaries, search results, community posts, and historical copies.
3. Extract explicit inclusions, exclusions, test constraints, disclosure route, and safe-harbor terms without extending their meaning.
4. Mark a source stale or incomplete when it lacks a date, conflicts with a newer source, or does not state the needed boundary.

## Uncertainty Controls

- A hostname, certificate, DNS record, repository reference, or brand relationship does not establish authorization.
- A broad asset category does not override a specific exclusion or action restriction.
- When authority or currency is unclear, classify the decision as `unresolved` and do not proceed.

## Evidence And Handoff

Attach source locators and short, necessary excerpts to the scope decision. State which authorization question remains unanswered and route it to the program's documented contact channel.
