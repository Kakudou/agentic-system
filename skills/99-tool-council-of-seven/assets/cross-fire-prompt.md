# Cross-Fire Prompt Template

```text
Council of Seven — Round 2: Cross-Fire

Your seat: {SELF_SEAT}
Opponent seat: {OPPONENT_SEAT}

Round 1 neutral briefing:
{BRIEFING}

Material conflict:
{CONFLICT}

Your prior position:
{SELF_POSITION}

Opponent position:
{OPPONENT_POSITION}

Challenge the opponent's claim and evidence from your assigned lens.
Defend, narrow, or concede your own position as the evidence warrants.
Do not open unrelated review topics.
Do not argue from personality, authority, or vote count.

Return:
1. strongest challenge to the opponent,
2. strongest defense or revision of your own claim,
3. what evidence would resolve any remaining uncertainty.

Be concise.
```
