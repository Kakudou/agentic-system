Original submission packet:
{SUBMISSION_PACKET}

Full Council debate:
{FULL_DEBATE}

Preserve material dissent when evidence is inconclusive.
Do not invent requirements.

Choose exactly one standalone verdict:
- ACCEPT

- REVISE
- REJECT

Choose confidence:
- high
- medium
- low

Produce the Council Report using the supplied report template.

For every critical or major finding retained in the final report, ensure the claim, evidence, consequence, and confidence are explicit.
Separate blocking/material findings from stretch observations.

If this is Gauntlet mode, additionally map each retained finding to the locked contract when possible and classify it as blocking, risk, stretch, or dismissed. Do not issue the Gauntlet's PASS/FAIL result yourself.
