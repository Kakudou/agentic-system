# Redaction And Review Checklist

- [ ] Authorization, scope, affected boundary, and observation window are stated.
- [ ] Every material factual statement maps to admitted evidence.
- [ ] Unsupported impact, severity, payout, and root-cause claims are absent or marked unverified.
- [ ] Credentials, tokens, session material, personal data, customer content, and unnecessary sensitive detail are removed or restricted.
- [ ] Each redaction has a category and the restricted original has an approved access path.
- [ ] Reproducibility context describes only known conditions and excludes exploit instructions.
- [ ] Remediation is framed as a security objective, not an unverified fix guarantee.
- [ ] Limitations, competing explanations, and reviewer questions are visible.
- [ ] The recipient and requested review decision are explicit.
