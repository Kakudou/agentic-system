# Review Handoff

## Purpose And Preconditions

Use when transferring a report draft to an authorized reviewer. Require a named recipient or approved channel, evidence access rules, and the completed claim/evidence matrix.

## Documentation Methodology

Hand off the factual draft, the evidence references a reviewer is permitted to access, redaction decisions, limitations, unresolved questions, and the requested decision. Make the review request explicit: factual accuracy, scope, evidence sufficiency, privacy handling, impact framing, or remediation framing.

## False-Claim And Privacy Controls

Do not represent review as approval, acceptance, validation, severity determination, or submission. Share only the minimum permitted materials. Keep restricted originals outside the draft and identify their approved access path without embedding sensitive content.

## Evidence And Handoff

Use the review handoff template. Record who received the package, what decision is requested, and what remains unverified. A reviewer response is evidence of their decision only, not proof of unsupported technical claims.
