# Evidence Admission

## Purpose And Preconditions

Use before a report draft cites an artifact. Require written authorization, a finding ID, in-scope target boundary, observation window, artifact provenance, and an approved handling location.

## Documentation Methodology

Record an evidence ID, artifact class, observer or system of origin, time window, applicable scope, and whether the report cites an original, redacted derivative, or metadata only. Admit the minimum artifact set that directly supports a stated observation. Preserve links between derivatives and their restricted originals without copying restricted content into the draft.

## False-Claim And Privacy Controls

Do not treat filenames, screenshots without context, summaries, or a reporter's recollection as proof of a technical fact. Do not include credentials, tokens, session identifiers, personal data, customer content, or unrelated system data. Mark missing provenance or incomplete context as a limitation rather than filling gaps.

## Evidence And Handoff

Each admitted item must be traceable from the claim/evidence matrix to the authorized evidence handoff. Send unadmittable artifacts, scope questions, and follow-up needs to the reviewer; do not collect or replay anything.
