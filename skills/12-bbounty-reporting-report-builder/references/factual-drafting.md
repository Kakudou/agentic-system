# Factual Finding Drafting

## Purpose And Preconditions

Use when writing the title, factual summary, and affected boundary. Require admitted evidence and a clear distinction between direct observations and inferences.

## Documentation Methodology

Write the title as the observed condition plus the affected boundary, without a rating or outcome assertion. Summarize: what was observed, where it was observed, when or under which documented conditions, and which evidence supports it. Name the boundary precisely enough for triage, while avoiding unnecessary implementation or operational detail.

## False-Claim And Privacy Controls

Do not state root cause, exploitability, attacker access, affected population, severity, or business consequence as fact unless the evidence directly supports it. Use qualified language for bounded inferences and label unknowns. Omit identifiers and details that exceed the reviewer's need.

## Evidence And Handoff

Give each material sentence a claim ID and evidence ID. Place conflicting observations and unresolved boundary questions in the reviewer questions, not in the factual summary.
