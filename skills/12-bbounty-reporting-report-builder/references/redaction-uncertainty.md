# Redaction And Uncertainty

## Purpose And Preconditions

Use before sharing a draft or evidence reference. Require the evidence handling rules, intended reviewer, and any applicable retention or disclosure constraints.

## Documentation Methodology

Redact only what is necessary and identify the redaction category, such as credential, session material, personal data, customer content, or sensitive infrastructure detail. Retain enough non-sensitive context for a reviewer to understand the claim. State uncertainty directly with its cause: unavailable artifact, incomplete observation, ambiguous scope, or competing explanation.

## False-Claim And Privacy Controls

Never claim that a redacted value has a particular meaning unless an authorized reviewer can verify it from the restricted original. Do not use fabricated placeholders that resemble real secrets. Do not disclose personal data or authentication material merely to make a report more persuasive.

## Evidence And Handoff

List each redacted or withheld artifact in the matrix, its handling status, and who may access the original. Escalate when the redaction prevents adequate review rather than weakening the privacy control.
