Controls:

- Use “supported” only when the cited evidence directly supports the precise wording.
- Use “limited” when evidence supports part of the claim but material conditions remain unknown.
- Use “unverified” for hypotheses, causal explanations, impact, classification, and any claim without independent confirmation.
- Do not include severity, CVSS, CWE, bounty, remediation, or disclosure-status fields.
