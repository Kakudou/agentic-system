# Redaction And Stop Checklist

Complete before sharing a packet.

- [ ] Scope and recipient authorization are confirmed.
- [ ] The packet contains no credentials, tokens, session data, private keys, or authentication material.
- [ ] The packet contains no unnecessary personal, financial, customer, or third-party data.
- [ ] Sensitive values are replaced with category descriptions and restricted evidence references.
- [ ] Screenshots, filenames, URLs, and metadata were reviewed for sensitive content.
- [ ] Original evidence remains in approved access-controlled storage.
- [ ] Facts, hypotheses, and validation questions are visibly separated.

Stop and escalate through the authorized channel if any answer is no, if data exposure may remain active, or if sharing the material could increase access or harm. Do not test, replay, use, or distribute the sensitive material.
