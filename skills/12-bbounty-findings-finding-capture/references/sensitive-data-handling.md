# Sensitive-Data Handling

## Purpose

Minimize exposure while preserving enough provenance for an authorized reviewer to locate and assess evidence safely.

## Preconditions

- The program's disclosure channel and evidence-storage rules are known, or the material is held pending clarification.
- The recorder can restrict access to any retained original evidence.

## Documentation Method

Before writing the packet, inspect the planned text, filenames, screenshots, and metadata for protected information. Replace sensitive values with a description of the data category and a restricted evidence reference. Record what was redacted, why, and who may access the original. Retain original evidence only in the program-approved private location.

## False-Claim And Privacy Controls

- Do not copy credentials, tokens, session data, personal data, financial data, private keys, or unrelated third-party information into the packet.
- Do not attempt to verify, use, replay, or expand sensitive material.
- Stop distribution and escalate through the authorized channel if exposure could create ongoing harm or access.

## Evidence And Handoff

Attach the completed stop checklist. State whether originals exist, where they are access-controlled, and which reviewer is authorized to request them.
