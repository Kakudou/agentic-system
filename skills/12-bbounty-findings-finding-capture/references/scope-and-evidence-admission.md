# Scope And Evidence Admission

## Purpose

Decide whether existing material may be documented as a finding candidate. This is an admission check, not a testing instruction or authorization substitute.

## Preconditions

- A current program authorization and scope source are available.
- The evidence already exists and its origin is known.

## Documentation Method

Record the program or authorization reference, in-scope asset identifier, observation time, collector or source, and each artifact's stable reference. Preserve original artifacts where the approved storage permits; record an integrity value only when one was actually supplied or calculated during collection. Note scope exclusions and constraints that affect review.

## False-Claim And Privacy Controls

- Do not infer authorization from asset ownership, branding, or a public URL.
- Do not admit material with an unknown source, altered provenance, or unclear scope.
- Do not copy raw protected content merely to prove admission; reference the approved evidence location.
- Stop and route to the program contact when scope or safe handling is ambiguous.

## Evidence And Handoff

The worksheet must identify the authorization and every admitted artifact. Mark non-admitted material as excluded, not as supporting evidence. Hand off unresolved admission questions with the packet.
