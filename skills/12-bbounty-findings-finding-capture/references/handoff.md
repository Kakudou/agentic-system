# Handoff

## Purpose

Transfer a bounded finding-capture packet to the authorized program reviewer without making a report decision, severity determination, or public disclosure.

## Preconditions

- The worksheet, confidence matrix, and redaction checklist are complete.
- Evidence access and recipient authorization have been confirmed.

## Documentation Method

Use the handoff template. Include the scope reference, factual observation summary, evidence inventory, confidence labels, redaction status, and explicit validation questions. State the secure evidence location rather than attaching material outside the approved channel.

## False-Claim And Privacy Controls

- Describe the packet as a finding candidate until the receiving process accepts it.
- Do not state severity, exploitability, remediation, bounty eligibility, or publication readiness.
- Do not send sensitive originals through an unapproved channel or to an unauthorized recipient.

## Evidence And Handoff

Record recipient, channel, time, packet version, and any access restrictions. If receipt or reviewer authorization cannot be established, retain the packet in approved private storage and escalate the delivery question.
