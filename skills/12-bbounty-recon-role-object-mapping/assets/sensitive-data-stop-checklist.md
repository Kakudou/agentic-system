# Sensitive-Data And Stop Checklist

Stop and seek direction if any item applies:

- The next step needs an unapproved account, tenant, object, role change, or state-changing action.
- Evidence exposes credentials, tokens, session material, personal data, financial data, private content, or unrelated customer records.
- The target, route, feature, or data category is outside scope or scope is unclear.
- The observation would require modifying a request, identifier, header, account context, or permission.
- Rate limiting, instability, a warning banner, or program rules require stopping.

Before handoff: redact to the minimum necessary, preserve source locations and timestamps, separate observations from inferences, and document the stop condition.
