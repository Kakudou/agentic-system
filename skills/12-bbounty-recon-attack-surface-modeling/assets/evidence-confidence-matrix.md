## Confidence Rule

- High: directly traceable evidence with no material unresolved conflict.
- Medium: traceable evidence with limited directness, recency, or corroboration.
- Low: ambiguous, single-source, indirect, stale, or attribution-limited evidence.

Confidence describes the claim only. It does not indicate impact, severity, or a vulnerability.
