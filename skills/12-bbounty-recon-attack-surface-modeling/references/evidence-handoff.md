# Evidence And Handoff

## Purpose

Package the model so an authorized recipient can review its scope, evidence, uncertainty, and required follow-up without treating it as a finding.

## Preconditions

- Scope admission, model records, confidence decisions, and conflicts are complete.
- A recipient and approved handling channel are known.

## Method

1. State the authorization reference, modeling period, included assets, exclusions, and stop decisions.
2. Deliver the worksheet and evidence/confidence matrix with stable evidence references.
3. Summarize material boundaries, unknowns, conflicts, and review indicators separately from observations.
4. List concrete validation questions, owner, and requested decision. State that no vulnerability conclusion is made.
5. Retain evidence only according to the program’s handling and retention rules.

## Interpretation

Handoff transfers a review package, not ownership, proof, or permission for additional activity. Recipients decide whether follow-up is authorized.

## False-Positive And Attribution Controls

- Make source provenance and confidence visible beside each material claim.
- Label analyst interpretation separately from observed evidence.
- Do not omit contradictory or out-of-date evidence merely to simplify the summary.

## Privacy And Scope Limits

Use the approved channel and minimum necessary disclosure. Redact secrets, personal data, tokens, and sensitive operational details; include secure references where approved.

## Evidence And Handoff

Complete the [recon handoff template](../assets/recon-handoff-template.md), attach the two matrices, and document delivery restrictions or unresolved handling questions.
