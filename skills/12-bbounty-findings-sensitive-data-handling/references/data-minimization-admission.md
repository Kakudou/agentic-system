# Data-Minimization Admission

## Purpose and Preconditions

Decide whether an already-observed artifact, derivative, or metadata is necessary to support one stated finding fact. Require written authorization, current scope, a finding ID, an approved recipient, and a known reporting channel. If any are absent, do not admit the artifact.

## Documentation Method

Record the claim supported, the minimum artifact class needed, source and observation window, excluded fields or views, admission decision, and reviewer. Prefer metadata or a narrow description over content. Do not record values, reconstruct content, or collect a replacement artifact.

## Privacy, Legal, and Scope Controls

Limit processing to the engagement purpose and authorized audience. Treat uncertainty as sensitive. Do not admit material merely because it might be useful later. Stop if the artifact includes personal data, credentials, session material, private communications, regulated data, or out-of-scope content that is not necessary and explicitly authorized.

## Evidence and Handoff

Place the admission rationale, classification, and exclusions in the manifest. Hand off only the admitted form and its limitations. A refusal or metadata-only decision is valid evidence of privacy-preserving handling.
