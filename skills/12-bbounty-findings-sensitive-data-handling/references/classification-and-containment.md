# Classification and Immediate Containment

## Purpose and Preconditions

Classify an already-observed artifact before it is copied into reporting material. Require a scope reference and authorized handler. Use `public`, `internal`, `confidential`, or `restricted`; choose the more protective class when uncertain.

## Documentation Method

Record artifact ID, class, category at a high level, scope status, handler, and handling decision. Categories may be described as personal data, authentication material, private content, internal operational data, or report-safe metadata. Do not include the sensitive value or a value-derived fingerprint.

## Privacy, Legal, and Scope Controls

Immediately stop further viewing, copying, sharing, testing, or processing when restricted material appears or classification is uncertain. Keep the original within the existing approved boundary. Notify only the authorized program contact or incident path specified by the engagement. Do not contact affected individuals, attempt to validate access, or expand scope.

## Evidence and Handoff

Record the stop time, reason, authorized notification reference, and whether any derivative is prohibited. Handoff is limited to classification, minimal metadata, and an explicit request for direction. Resume only after documented authorization.
