# Retention and Access Boundaries

## Purpose and Preconditions

Set the handling boundary before retaining or sharing admitted evidence. Require the engagement's approved store or channel, authorized recipients, retention rule, disposal owner, and any legal or program constraints. This guidance does not select, create, or configure storage.

## Documentation Method

Record the artifact ID, classification, approved audience, retention trigger and end condition, disposal owner, and access decision. Record exceptions and unresolved policy questions without copying the restricted material.

## Privacy, Legal, and Scope Controls

Grant access only to authorized recipients with a demonstrated need to know. Do not send restricted evidence through an unapproved channel, retain it beyond the approved period, or broaden access for convenience. If the boundary cannot be verified, stop and request direction from the authorized program contact.

## Evidence and Handoff

Include the retention and access decision in the manifest and handoff. The recipient must be able to see what they are authorized to receive, what they are not receiving, and who owns disposition. Do not claim deletion, access revocation, or retention compliance without confirmation from the responsible system or owner.
