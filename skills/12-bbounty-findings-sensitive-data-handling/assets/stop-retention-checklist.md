# Stop and Retention Checklist

- [ ] Written authorization and current scope are confirmed.
- [ ] The minimum necessary artifact form is identified.
- [ ] Classification is documented or handling has stopped for uncertainty.
- [ ] No sensitive value was copied into notes, messages, or the report.
- [ ] Restricted or uncertain content triggered immediate containment and authorized notification.
- [ ] Recipient and reporting channel are approved for the classification.
- [ ] Retention trigger, end condition, and disposal owner are documented.
- [ ] Handoff states limitations and any authorization needed for follow-up.
