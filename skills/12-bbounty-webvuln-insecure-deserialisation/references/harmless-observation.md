# Harmless Format And Handling Observation

## Purpose And Preconditions

Use to collect passive, authorized evidence without sending, modifying, decoding, or replaying input.

## Inert Bounded Methodology

Review existing documentation, redacted logs, approved captures, source, or owner-run test records. Note declared format, parser identity, validation order, stable response class, and correlation locator. Do not retain content values.

## Observations And Interpretation

A documented parser or stable handling event can establish a review lead. It cannot establish unsafe type activation, code execution, or data exposure. Unexpected behavior is a stop signal, not an invitation to probe.

## False-Positive Controls

Confirm that the record belongs to the intended route and environment. Exclude middleware errors, cached responses, and unrelated telemetry.

## Evidence And Remediation

Record timestamp, route, component, and redacted locator. Handoff unexpected handling to the owner for controlled investigation.
