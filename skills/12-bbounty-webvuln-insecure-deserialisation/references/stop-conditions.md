# Strict Stop Conditions

## Purpose And Preconditions

Use before and throughout every assessment. Written authorization and an owner escalation path are mandatory.

## Inert Bounded Methodology

Stop and preserve only existing evidence if scope is unclear, the next action would modify or submit serialized content, production behavior changes unexpectedly, sensitive material appears, or any execution, external interaction, data/file access, state change, or availability risk is indicated.

## Observations And Interpretation

A stop is not negative evidence. It means the remaining question requires an owner-controlled environment or a revised written authorization.

## False-Positive Controls

Do not treat error pages, type labels, or dependency indicators as permission to continue. Confirm anomalies with the owner before resuming any review.

## Evidence And Remediation

Record the timestamp, boundary, observed condition, and notification path. Handoff for controlled triage and remediation review.
