# Inclusion and Exclusion Preservation

## Purpose and Preconditions

Document the permissions and restrictions expressed by admitted sources without changing their boundary. Require a cited inclusion or restriction statement for every matrix entry.

## Documentation Methodology

Record inclusions, exclusions, conditional permissions, prohibited actions, environment restrictions, time limits, rate limits, and reporting requirements in separate fields. Quote the source language verbatim, then add a short neutral restatement only when it preserves the same meaning. Link each restriction to every affected identifier only when the source makes that relationship explicit.

Keep broad patterns and narrow exceptions as independent records. A later consumer can see the original statements and decide only with authorized clarification.

## Uncertainty and Scope Controls

An exclusion, prohibition, or condition is never discarded because an inclusion seems broader. Do not assume exceptions, precedence, ownership, or applicability across assets. Conflicting or overlapping language is unresolved until the authorized owner clarifies it. No target expansion, testing, resolution, or accessibility check belongs in this process.

## Evidence and Handoff

Handoff must identify the exact source citation for each inclusion and restriction, list potential overlaps without resolving them, and state the conservative operational posture: restrictions remain in force until clarified.

## Authoritative Sources

- The currently admitted rules of engagement and scope policy.
- Written clarifications issued by the authorized program owner.
- ISO/IEC 29147:2018 for coordinated disclosure terminology where applicable.
