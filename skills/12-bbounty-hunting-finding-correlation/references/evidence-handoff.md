# Evidence And Handoff

## Purpose And Preconditions

Prepare a reviewable, minimally sensitive package for an authorized report owner. Complete correlation and privacy checks first; this guide does not submit reports or contact third parties.

## Evidence-Led Method

1. Use the handoff template to identify scope, assessment purpose, and decision owner.
2. List admitted and excluded findings by ID with immutable evidence locators.
3. Describe relationships in bounded language: observed similarity, supported pattern, duplicate candidate, multi-aspect candidate, or unresolved.
4. Attach confidence, conflicts, assumptions, redactions, and follow-up evidence needs to every relationship.
5. Verify recipients, handling rules, and redactions before transfer.

## Interpretation And Uncertainty

A handoff is an assessment for review, not a vulnerability report, root-cause determination, severity decision, or authorization to retest. The report owner decides whether and how to consolidate material under the applicable program rules.

## Controls

- Apply data minimization: include references and excerpts only when needed for review.
- Remove secrets, personal data, access tokens, and out-of-scope architecture details.
- Do not use speculative chain, impact, or cause language.
- Stop transfer if recipient authorization, encryption requirements, or disclosure boundaries are unknown.

## Evidence And Handoff

The completed package must contain source IDs and locators, relationship assessments, confidence and conflict records, redaction notes, scope constraints, and a named reviewer or queue. Retain no new copies beyond the authorized handling process.
