# Confidence And Conflict Controls

## Purpose And Preconditions

Make correlation uncertainty visible and prevent a convenient grouping from becoming an unsupported conclusion. Apply this guide to every proposed relationship among admitted findings.

## Evidence-Led Method

1. Rate confidence in the relationship, not the underlying finding: high, medium, low, or unresolved.
2. Base the rating on evidence provenance, directness, consistency, condition equivalence, and known gaps.
3. Record every material conflict and classify it as resolved with cited evidence, unresolved, or out of scope.
4. Define the next permitted evidence needed to change the classification; do not prescribe active verification in this skill.
5. Revisit labels when new authorized evidence arrives, preserving the prior assessment and why it changed.

## Interpretation And Uncertainty

High confidence is not certainty and does not establish root cause, exploitability, severity, or remediation priority. An unresolved conflict is a valid result. Absence of contradictory evidence is not confirmation.

## Controls

- Separate facts, inferences, and assumptions in every record.
- Require at least one disconfirming check in the matrix before a causal label.
- Avoid confidence inflation from repeated copies of the same source or tool output.
- Stop and escalate if handling evidence would exceed authorization, privacy limits, or reporting policy.

## Evidence And Handoff

Hand off the relationship label, confidence rationale, evidence IDs, conflicts, assumptions, decision owner, and conditions that would invalidate the assessment.
