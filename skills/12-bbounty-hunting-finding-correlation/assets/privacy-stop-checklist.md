# Privacy And Stop Checklist

- [ ] Each item is within written authorization and declared scope.
- [ ] Evidence has a stable ID and locator; interpretations are labeled separately.
- [ ] Personal data, secrets, tokens, and customer content are removed or replaced with authorized references.
- [ ] No relationship is based only on labels, hashes, endpoint names, or repeated scanner output.
- [ ] Material conflicts and uncertainty are recorded rather than merged away.
- [ ] The intended recipient and permitted handling channel are confirmed.
- [ ] Stop and escalate if scope, provenance, privacy, recipient authorization, or disclosure rules are unclear.
