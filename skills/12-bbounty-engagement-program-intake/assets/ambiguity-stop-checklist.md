# Ambiguity And Stop Checklist

- [ ] Primary source and access time recorded.
- [ ] Scope and exclusions have direct evidence references.
- [ ] Explicit authorization is distinguished from silence.
- [ ] Prohibited actions, systems, accounts, and data rules are captured.
- [ ] Safe-harbor conditions and exceptions are captured.
- [ ] Reporting, confidentiality, disclosure, duplicate, and timing terms are captured.
- [ ] Conflicts, incomplete terms, and undefined material boundaries are listed as questions.
- [ ] Every low-confidence material item has a clarification route or stop condition.
- [ ] No downstream activity is implied by this intake packet.
