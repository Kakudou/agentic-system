# Safe Harbor And Disclosure

## Purpose

Capture the program's stated protections and disclosure obligations without overstating their legal or operational effect.

## Preconditions

- Current program policy and any linked safe-harbor or legal terms are available.
- Scope and prohibited-action entries have been extracted.

## Documentation Methodology

1. Quote the complete safe-harbor statement and every condition or exception that qualifies it.
2. Record the reporting destination, confidentiality terms, coordination expectations, and public-disclosure rules separately.
3. Capture defined timelines, embargoes, acknowledgement expectations, and program rights to change terms.
4. Identify whether terms apply to the program operator, platform, affiliates, or a different party.

## Interpretation And Uncertainty Controls

- Safe harbor is conditional program language, not a blanket waiver or authorization beyond stated scope.
- A disclosure timeline is not a promise of triage, remediation, payment, or immunity.
- If legal wording, jurisdiction, or third-party coverage is unclear, preserve the ambiguity and seek clarification.
- Never infer permission to access, retain, share, or publish data from a disclosure clause.

## Evidence And Handoff

Deliver quoted terms, conditions, timing fields, and open legal or operational questions. Stop when required reporting, confidentiality, or disclosure conditions cannot be determined.

## Concise Sources

- Program-owned policy and legal terms are primary.
- ISO/IEC 29147:2018, Vulnerability disclosure: <https://www.iso.org/standard/72311.html>
