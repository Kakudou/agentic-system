# Scope And Rules Extraction

## Purpose

Produce a literal, reviewable scope and rule inventory without enlarging the program's stated authorization.

## Preconditions

- Source provenance has been captured.
- The current document is identified as authoritative or its authority is recorded as uncertain.

## Documentation Methodology

1. Copy each asset entry as written and assign an asset type only when the source supports it.
2. Record inclusions, exclusions, environment qualifiers, account requirements, and stated ownership separately.
3. Split prose rules into one normalized statement per rule, preserving a verbatim excerpt and locator.
4. Preserve qualifiers such as `only`, `except`, dates, regions, versions, and third-party limitations.

## Interpretation And Uncertainty Controls

- A wildcard, range, brand name, or product family is not a mandate to enumerate or expand targets.
- Treat examples, reward tables, and marketing copy as non-authorizing unless the program explicitly makes them operative rules.
- Mark incomplete tables, dynamic content, conflicting documents, and undefined terms as unresolved.
- Do not convert silence into in-scope status.

## Evidence And Handoff

For every extracted entry, provide source ID, locator, verbatim text, normalized meaning, and confidence. Send unresolved scope boundaries to the program's designated clarification channel or stop the engagement.

## Concise Sources

- Program-owned scope and policy pages are primary.
- Platform program briefs are secondary only when linked or incorporated by the program.
- CISA, "Stakeholder-Specific Vulnerability Categorization," for coordinated vulnerability disclosure context: <https://www.cisa.gov/resources-tools/resources/stakeholder-specific-vulnerability-categorization>
