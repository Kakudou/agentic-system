# Ambiguity And Validation

## Purpose

Prevent unsupported interpretations from becoming engagement authorization.

## Preconditions

- Source, scope, restrictions, and disclosure entries have evidence references.
- A program clarification or authorized review channel is known.

## Documentation Methodology

1. Score each material entry: `high` for explicit, current, authoritative wording; `medium` for clear but qualified or indirectly incorporated wording; `low` for incomplete, conflicting, stale, or inferred wording.
2. List ambiguity as a question, not a conclusion, and cite the conflicting or incomplete text.
3. Identify the operational consequence: proceed only within explicit terms, request clarification, or stop.
4. Validate completeness by checking every packet field against source evidence and every source against the register.

## Interpretation And Uncertainty Controls

- Never resolve ambiguity in favor of broader scope, greater access, or more intrusive activity.
- A downstream reviewer's risk appetite cannot replace program authorization.
- Confidence measures evidence quality, not legal validity or operational safety.
- Do not use later documents to silently rewrite the evidence for an earlier intake.

## Evidence And Handoff

Complete the [ambiguity and stop checklist](../assets/ambiguity-stop-checklist.md) and [confidence matrix](../assets/scope-rule-confidence-matrix.md). A low-confidence material boundary is a stop or escalation condition.
