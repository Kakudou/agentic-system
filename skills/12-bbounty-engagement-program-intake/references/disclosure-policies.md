# Disclosure Interpretation

## Purpose

Normalize reporting, duplicate handling, confidentiality, and timing terms for downstream planning.

## Preconditions

- The reporting and disclosure policy has been captured with source locators.
- Any platform terms that govern the named program are identified.

## Documentation Methodology

1. Record report channel, required fields, language, encryption, and acknowledgement terms exactly as stated.
2. Separate duplicate policy, reward eligibility, publication policy, remediation timeline, and researcher obligations.
3. Capture calendar references with their stated timezone or mark timezone unknown.
4. Note whether a term is a requirement, target, discretionary practice, or undefined.

## Interpretation And Uncertainty Controls

- Do not equate first report, accepted report, resolved report, and rewarded report.
- Do not translate aspirational service targets into binding deadlines.
- Preserve undefined terms such as "reasonable" or "promptly" rather than assigning a duration.
- When program and platform terms conflict, flag the conflict; do not select one unilaterally.

## Evidence And Handoff

Supply a timeline table with source locator and confidence. Escalate conflicting, expired, or incomplete disclosure directions before a reportable event.
