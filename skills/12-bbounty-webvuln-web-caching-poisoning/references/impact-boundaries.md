# Impact Boundaries

## Purpose And Preconditions

Ensure cache-key validation remains a controlled configuration assessment. A known owner contact and stop authority are required.

## Safe Controlled Methodology

Use only an inert fixture marker and one controlled account. State possible consequences only as conditional engineering risk based on confirmed configuration behavior. Do not deliver a response to another user, test reachability, or assess user impact.

## Observations And Interpretation

A controlled cache-key gap proves a need for remediation review, not exposure, audience size, or severity. Those require separate owner-led risk assessment.

## False-Positive Controls

Verify shared-cache eligibility, key scope, TTL, origin variance, and invalidation behavior before describing risk. Do not infer cross-user impact from a single controlled session.

## Cleanup And Stop Conditions

Stop and alert the owner if any marker leaves the fixture context, data is not controlled, or invalidation cannot be confirmed.

## Evidence And Remediation

Record the boundary decision and escalation. Prioritize non-shared caching for personalized content and complete key coverage for approved public variants.
