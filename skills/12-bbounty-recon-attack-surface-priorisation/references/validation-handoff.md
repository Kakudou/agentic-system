# Validation And Handoff

## Purpose

Ensure an authorized reviewer can audit the surface review without relying on unstated judgments.

## Preconditions

- The worksheet is complete enough to trace each entry to its authorization and source evidence.
- Sensitive material has been minimized or redacted according to the engagement rules.

## Evidence-Led Methodology

For every entry, compare the written indicator, confidence label, rationale, and limitations with the cited evidence. Check that the asset remains in scope, that the stated context matches the source, and that the review order does not imply an unrecorded security claim. Use the [recon handoff template](../assets/recon-handoff-template.md) to package facts, uncertainty, excluded material, and requested decisions.

## Interpretation

Validation confirms traceability and faithful representation of the evidence. It does not validate a vulnerability, authorize further work, or establish that the inventory is complete.

## Bias And False-Positive Controls

- Require a source reference for each material statement and rationale.
- Flag unsupported certainty, stale records, conflicting ownership information, and entries whose order cannot be explained.
- Keep reviewer corrections as annotations; do not silently rewrite source facts.

## Privacy And Scope Limits

Share only with authorized recipients and only the minimum redacted evidence needed to review the handoff. Stop for misdirected recipients, sensitive-data exposure, scope ambiguity, or a request for activity not covered by authorization.

## Evidence And Handoff

Deliver the completed handoff, source references, confidence and limitations, review order rationale, stop decisions, and a clear request for the required decision.
