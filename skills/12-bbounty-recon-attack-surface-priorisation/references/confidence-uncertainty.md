# Confidence And Uncertainty

## Purpose

Make the support and limits of each surface statement visible to the receiving reviewer.

## Preconditions

- Every statement is linked to at least one admissible source.
- Source dates, environments, and collection contexts are available when known.

## Evidence-Led Methodology

Use `direct` for an explicit statement or observation in one attributable approved record. Use `corroborated` when independent approved records agree after their environment and date are compared. Use `inconclusive` when evidence is incomplete, conflicting, stale, or lacks ownership context. Record the competing explanation and the specific missing evidence in each inconclusive entry.

## Interpretation

Confidence measures support for the recorded observation only. It does not measure likelihood of a security issue, impact, exploitability, business value, or completeness.

## Bias And False-Positive Controls

- Do not count copied inventories, generated reports, or records derived from the same source as independent corroboration.
- Prefer a dated primary record over an undated secondary summary.
- Treat absence from a record as unknown unless the record explicitly claims completeness.

## Privacy And Scope Limits

Resolve uncertainty only through an approved owner and authorized follow-up. Do not generate new traffic, use alternate identities, access protected material, or expand collection to improve confidence.

## Evidence And Handoff

Provide the confidence label, supporting references, collection context, alternatives, limitations, and the least additional authorized evidence that could resolve the question.
