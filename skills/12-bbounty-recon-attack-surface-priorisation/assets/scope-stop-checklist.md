# Scope And Stop Checklist

- [ ] Written authorization covers each reviewed asset, evidence source, time window, recipient, and handling rule.
- [ ] Each evidence record is attributable, dated when available, and linked to its collection context.
- [ ] Sensitive data, credentials, tokens, and unnecessary personal data are excluded or redacted.
- [ ] Surface indicators are factual descriptions, not security claims.
- [ ] Confidence, alternatives, and limitations are recorded for each material entry.
- [ ] Review order is explained by objective relevance, evidence quality, ownership clarity, or blocking uncertainty only.
- [ ] No new discovery, traffic, testing, authentication, enumeration, or automation is being performed.
- [ ] Stop for scope ambiguity, out-of-scope material, sensitive exposure, conflicting authorization, or a request to take further action.
- [ ] Escalate the stop reason and requested decision to the recorded contact.
