# Living Worldbuilding

`/10-rpg-world {location-or-setting-development}`

Start from the chosen game and campaign's established canon. Record each world fact as `published`, `established-at-table`, `private-prepared`, `in-world-rumor`, or `proposed` and name its source/fictional time when needed. A setting's description may be incomplete: introduce believable, compatible original locations and factions, but never misrepresent them as published lore. A rules engine (especially Cypher) does not imply a default world.

For a new scene, prepare only its useful sensory texture, practical geography, social norms, key interests, faction pressures, and immediate opportunities. Time and player actions can advance offscreen clocks; changes must be causally motivated and committed when they actually occur. Keep NPC knowledge and player knowledge bounded. A prepared disaster or twist is a possibility until triggered by confirmed events.

A world is not a plot. Give the player room to act, refuse, explore, negotiate or leave. Feed public perceptions to scene narration; private plans to restricted GM preparation. Update durable descriptions only from confirmed developments and actual supported writes.
