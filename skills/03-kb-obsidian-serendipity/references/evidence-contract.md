# Evidence Contract

Serendipity permits synthesis, not fabrication.

## Direct Claims

A `direct` claim is explicitly supported by one or more selected zettels.

Preserve the source's:

- modality;
- certainty;
- scope;
- exceptions;
- technical literals.

## Synthesis

A `synthesis` is a conclusion that reasonably follows from combining selected zettels.

It may be new prose that appears in no individual note.

A synthesis must:

- identify a real shared mechanism, relationship, contrast, or consequence;
- remain compatible with all supporting notes;
- not silently strengthen uncertain claims;
- be explainable from the selected evidence.

## Hypothesis

Use `hypothesis` when a connection is plausible but the current graph does not establish it.

Hypotheses are allowed because they can be useful thinking prompts.

They must never be written as settled fact.

## Unsupported Gap

When the selected notes do not support a needed claim:

- state the gap;
- stop the claim there.

Do not use general model knowledge to patch the vault unless the user explicitly requests outside
context or research.

## Traceability

Every material factual or synthetic conclusion should be attributable to one or more selected
zettels.

The user-facing response does not need citation clutter after every sentence, but the supporting
zettel set must be explicit enough that the reasoning can be audited.
