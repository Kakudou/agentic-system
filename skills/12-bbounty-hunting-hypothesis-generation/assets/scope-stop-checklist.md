# Scope And Stop Checklist

## Before Synthesis

- [ ] Written authorization reference is present.
- [ ] Asset is explicitly in scope.
- [ ] Exclusions, time window, and account/data restrictions are known.
- [ ] Each observation has source, context, and time.
- [ ] Handoff recipient and escalation contact are known.

## Stop And Escalate

- [ ] Scope, authorization, or evidence provenance is ambiguous.
- [ ] Unexpected sensitive data, credentials, or personal data appears.
- [ ] Service degradation or an authorization anomaly is observed.
- [ ] A third party or out-of-scope asset may be affected.
- [ ] Resolving uncertainty would require new interaction or expanded scope.

Stop work, preserve only necessary context, and notify the named recipient. Do not convert a stop signal into a finding.
