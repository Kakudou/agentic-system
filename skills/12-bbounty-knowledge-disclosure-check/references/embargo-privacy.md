# Privacy And Embargo Controls

## Purpose And Preconditions

Use before retaining, sharing, or discussing a disclosure-status record. Require handling rules, any supplied embargo terms, a named authorized recipient, and an approved destination.

## Documentation Methodology

Classify each record as cleared, redacted, embargoed, restricted, or stopped. Retain only source references and facts necessary for the status statement. Replace protected material with a reasoned redaction marker and record the embargo owner, review date, and release condition when supplied.

## Uncertainty And Privacy Controls

Never include credentials, tokens, private keys, session material, raw customer data, private reports, reporter identity, or unpublished technical details. Do not guess an embargo expiry or authorization. Stop and escalate if material cannot be safely minimized, the recipient is not authorized, or handling terms conflict.

## Evidence And Handoff

Complete the [embargo/stop checklist](../assets/embargo-stop-checklist.md). Transfer restricted evidence only by approved reference and channel; the handoff does not authorize publication, further access, or testing.
