# Approved Source And Scope Admission

## Purpose And Preconditions

Use before recording a disclosure-status claim. Require written authorization, a named program or engagement scope, a supplied source reference, a named recipient, and applicable retention and handling rules.

## Documentation Methodology

For each source, record its owner or publisher, locator, supplied date or version, public-access status, and the exact status fact it supports. Admit only sources that are both in scope and attributable. Label source currency as known only when the supplied material establishes it; otherwise record it as unknown.

## Uncertainty And Privacy Controls

Do not treat a source's title, a similar target name, or an unverified screenshot as proof of program ownership or current policy. Do not include private-report content, credentials, personal data, or access instructions. Stop and escalate when authorization, provenance, scope, or handling rights are unclear.

## Evidence And Handoff

Attach the admission decision and excluded-source rationale to the static record. An admission confirms only that a source may be documented, not that its claims are complete, current, or applicable.
