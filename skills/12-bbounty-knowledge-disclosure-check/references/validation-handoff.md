# Validation And Knowledge Handoff

## Purpose And Preconditions

Use to review a completed disclosure-status record and transfer it to an authorized recipient. Require admitted sources, a completed privacy and embargo review, an approved destination, and no unresolved stop condition.

## Documentation Methodology

Check each factual statement against an admitted source. Confirm the record distinguishes observation from inference; labels missing evidence as unknown; includes date context, limitations, confidence rationale, attribution limits, and handling status; and contains no retrieval instructions or operational detail. Mark it `reviewed`, `needs-clarification`, or `stopped`.

## Uncertainty And Privacy Controls

Review does not verify a vulnerability, determine a duplicate, authorize submission or disclosure, or establish reward eligibility. Do not transfer protected source contents where a redacted reference is sufficient. Stop for unsupported claims, unknown recipient authority, or unresolved embargo restrictions.

## Evidence And Handoff

Transfer the minimum necessary summary using the [knowledge handoff template](../assets/knowledge-handoff-template.md). Include reviewer role, review date, disposition, source references, limitations, handling restrictions, recipient, and questions requiring separately authorized work.
