# Public Disclosure-Status Record

## Purpose And Preconditions

Use to document status facts already present in admitted, supplied material. Require an admitted source, source date or an explicit unknown date, and a bounded subject label.

## Documentation Methodology

Record only the source's observable wording about publication, acknowledgment, embargo, or lack of public documentation. Distinguish `publicly_disclosed`, `publicly_unconfirmed`, `not_publicly_documented`, and `unknown`. `not_publicly_documented` means the admitted material contains no public record for the stated subject; it does not establish nonexistence or novelty.

## Uncertainty And Privacy Controls

Do not convert a missing source, inaccessible page, or incomplete excerpt into an undisclosed finding. Preserve dates and access limitations. Omit report identifiers, technical details, and personal information unless authorized and necessary for attribution. Respect the strictest supplied embargo or handling rule.

## Evidence And Handoff

Use the [static disclosure-status record](../assets/static-disclosure-status-record.md) to retain source references, exact observed facts, and limitations. Pass only a redacted status summary and unresolved questions to the named recipient.
