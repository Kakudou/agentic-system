# Embargo/Stop Checklist

- [ ] Authorization, scope, source provenance, and recipient authority are documented.
- [ ] Public status is stated only from admitted supplied material; missing evidence is `unknown`.
- [ ] Embargo owner, release condition, and review date are recorded when supplied.
- [ ] Private reports, credentials, personal data, and unpublished technical details are excluded or redacted.
- [ ] Similarity observations do not claim duplicate status, identity, priority, impact, or reward eligibility.
- [ ] The destination and channel meet handling restrictions.
- [ ] Stop and escalate if any authorization, provenance, privacy, embargo, or recipient restriction is unresolved.
