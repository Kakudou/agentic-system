# News Selection

## Topic Sources

### Cybersecurity

Discovery communities:

- `r/cybersecurity`
- `r/netwatch`

Useful underlying evidence often includes:

- vendor/security advisories;
- CERT/CSIRT notices;
- official incident statements;
- reputable security journalism;
- primary vulnerability/project notices.

### France

Discovery communities:

- `r/france`
- `r/Paris`

Useful underlying evidence often includes:

- official French institutions;
- local authorities;
- public operators;
- established French/international reporting;
- primary statements from the involved organization.

## Freshness

Prefer stories from the last 24–48 hours.

Always distinguish:

- when the story was published;
- when the event actually happened.

A newly reposted old event is not fresh news.

## Filtering

Exclude:

- advertisements/sponsored posts;
- self-promotion without independent relevance;
- duplicate reposts;
- pure memes;
- headlines whose underlying source does not support the claim;
- stale items presented as current.

Political news is not automatically excluded from the France topic, but it must meet the same
evidence/freshness bar and remain a factual brief rather than partisan framing.

## Reddit Signal

Votes/comments are discovery signals only.

Do not use engagement as factual verification.

## News Summary

Keep only what the evidence establishes.

Do not import speculation from Reddit comments into the news summary.
