# Program Intake Handoff

## Purpose

Transfer bounded, source-backed discovery evidence through an authorized intake channel without implying authorization or recommending testing.

## Preconditions

- Completed discovery record, source/confidence matrix, and eligibility/stop checklist.
- An authorized intake recipient or review channel is documented.

## Documentation Method

1. Identify the program using its official name and source identity.
2. Transfer source records, verbatim claim excerpts, confidence and recency status, and all conflicts unchanged.
3. Separate documented authorization, restrictions, and exclusions from unknown or conditional items.
4. State the last observed status and the time it was observed.
5. Include a required revalidation instruction for current authorization and rules before downstream work.

## Uncertainty And Attribution Controls

- Discovery does not approve a program, grant access, or clear a target for testing.
- Do not omit stop conditions or convert an unresolved item into a caveated approval.
- Keep private credentials, target data, researcher identity documents, and report content out of the reusable handoff record.

## Evidence And Handoff

Use the handoff template. Record acknowledgement or review status, require validation of the current official rules, and preserve unresolved items for escalation.
