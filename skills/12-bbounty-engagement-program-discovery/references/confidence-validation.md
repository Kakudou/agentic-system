# Confidence And Validation Controls

## Purpose

Make source age, conflicts, and unknowns visible so discovery does not convert stale or ambiguous public material into authorization.

## Preconditions

- At least one documented source and claim entry.
- Recorded access time for each consulted source.

## Documentation Method

1. Assign each material claim a confidence of `high`, `medium`, or `low` based on source authority, directness, completeness, and recency evidence.
2. Record the exact evidence supporting the assessment rather than a weighted rating.
3. Compare sources only for the same claim. Preserve contradictory excerpts and identify the conflict.
4. Re-check live program status and material rules immediately before handoff when feasible; record the recheck time or its absence.

## Uncertainty And Attribution Controls

- High confidence requires a direct official source with a clear locator and no known conflict; it is not a guarantee of current authorization.
- Medium confidence means a material limitation exists, such as no revision signal or an indirect official source.
- Low confidence, conflicting, inaccessible, or undated material is a stop/escalation condition for authorization-dependent work.
- Never fill a gap using historical knowledge, platform assumptions, or a source's general terms.

## Evidence And Handoff

Provide the completed source/confidence matrix, conflict log, recency status, recheck time, and owner for each unresolved question. Intake accepts only documented claims and must revalidate material authorization.
