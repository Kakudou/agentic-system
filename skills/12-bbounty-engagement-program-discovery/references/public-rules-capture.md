# Public Rules And Scope Evidence

## Purpose

Capture public program rules as bounded evidence for downstream intake without expanding assets, actions, or permissions.

## Preconditions

- An official, provenance-recorded program source.
- Authorization screening has not produced a stop condition.

## Documentation Method

1. Capture each in-scope asset, excluded asset, action restriction, data restriction, and reporting instruction exactly as written.
2. Preserve qualifiers such as environment, ownership, account type, asset class, date window, and required coordination.
3. Record scope, exclusions, authorization, safe-harbor conditions, disclosure, and reporting terms as separate claim entries with their own locators.
4. Record linked rules documents separately; do not assume that an overview page includes all operative terms.

## Uncertainty And Attribution Controls

- Do not expand wildcards, infer subdomains, resolve ownership, or treat related brands as in scope.
- Do not normalize an ambiguous asset into a testable target.
- Note differences between platform rules and program-owner rules without selecting a winner.
- Mark unpublished, dynamic, inaccessible, or contradictory terms as unresolved.

## Evidence And Handoff

Deliver excerpt-backed rule entries and a clear partition: documented scope, documented exclusions, documented restrictions, and unresolved items. The receiving intake owner must validate the current rules before authorization-dependent work.
