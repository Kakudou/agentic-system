# Eligibility And Authorization Screening

## Purpose

Determine whether public material documents a bounded path to proceed to intake, while preserving the distinction between eligibility, safe harbor, and authorization.

## Preconditions

- A provenance-recorded public source.
- A named prospective program and downstream intake owner.

## Documentation Method

1. Record program status exactly as displayed, including paused, invitation-only, or region-limited status.
2. Extract stated participant eligibility, account, identity, age, location, employee/contractor, and conflict-of-interest requirements.
3. Extract explicit authorization language separately from safe harbor and reporting terms.
4. Record conditions on accounts, rate limits, production systems, data handling, social engineering, and third parties.
5. Mark the record `stop` unless the public material clearly identifies the authorized participant class and testing boundary.

## Uncertainty And Attribution Controls

- Eligibility to join is not authorization to test every asset or method.
- Safe harbor is limited to its written conditions; never generalize it.
- Silence, a platform listing, or a public brand page is not authorization.
- Conflicting sources, missing eligibility criteria, or required authenticated terms require escalation rather than interpretation.

## Evidence And Handoff

Provide verbatim authorization and eligibility excerpts, source locators, applicable conditions, and an explicit `proceed to intake` or `stop/escalate` status. Do not hand off an inferred permission.
