# Program Source Provenance

## Purpose

Establish whether a public document can support a program-discovery claim and preserve enough context for intake to verify it.

## Preconditions

- A public URL or document supplied by the requester or reached through an official publisher link.
- The ability to record the access time and the displayed document identity.

## Documentation Method

1. Prefer a program owner or named platform's official program page, policy, or rules document.
2. Record title, canonical URL or document identifier, publisher, access time, and visible revision, effective date, or last-updated signal.
3. Preserve exact excerpts and stable locators for each material claim. Record redirects and linked policy documents as separate sources.
4. Identify the relationship between a platform page and the program owner without assuming either supersedes the other.

## Uncertainty And Attribution Controls

- Attribute every claim to one source and locator; do not merge inconsistent documents.
- An unauthenticated public page does not prove access to private scope or eligibility.
- Missing revision evidence lowers recency confidence; it does not make the material current.
- Stop and flag unofficial reposts, stale copies, broken links, or uncertain publisher identity.

## Evidence And Handoff

Provide a source entry with identity, publisher relationship, access time, revision signal, excerpt, locator, and observed limitations. Intake must re-check material sources before action.
