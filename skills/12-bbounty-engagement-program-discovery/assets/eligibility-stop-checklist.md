# Eligibility And Stop Checklist

- [ ] Official source identity and access time are recorded.
- [ ] Current program status is documented.
- [ ] Participant eligibility requirements are captured or marked unknown.
- [ ] Explicit authorization is captured separately from safe harbor.
- [ ] In-scope assets and exclusions are excerpt-backed.
- [ ] Action, account, data, and third-party restrictions are excerpt-backed.
- [ ] Reporting and disclosure terms are recorded.
- [ ] Conflicts, missing terms, and stale evidence are listed.
- [ ] A named intake owner and escalation route are recorded.
- [ ] No private access, test activity, or scope expansion occurred during discovery.

## Stop Conditions

Stop and escalate if any material authorization, eligibility, scope, exclusion, restriction, or current-status claim is missing, contradictory, inaccessible, or low confidence.
