# Correlation And Handoff

## Purpose

Convert passive observations into a reviewable handoff without converting a lead into an unsupported ownership, reachability, exposure, or vulnerability assertion.

## Preconditions

- Every retained observation has a source locator, retrieval time, and scope mapping.
- Sensitive data has been excluded or minimized according to the stop checklist.
- The recipient and reporting channel are established.

## Passive Authorized Method

Group observations by explicit claim, not by source volume. Compare independent source types, dates, and identity evidence. Use the worksheet and matrix to record contradictions, stale records, and confidence. Do not conduct active validation or recommend it as though authorization already exists.

## Interpretation And Controls

- Repeated copies from one provider or syndicated datasets are one evidence lineage, not independent corroboration.
- Separate `source reliability`, `identity attribution`, and `claim confidence`.
- State what the evidence cannot establish, especially current operation, ownership, scope, and security impact.

## Privacy And Scope Limits

Share only the minimum evidence required by the authorized recipient. Redact sensitive values, personal data, and irrelevant third-party material. Use the program's sensitive-report channel if available.

## Evidence And Handoff

Complete the [source-to-claim worksheet](../assets/source-to-claim-evidence-worksheet.md), [attribution/confidence matrix](../assets/attribution-confidence-matrix.md), and [recon handoff template](../assets/recon-handoff-template.md). Escalate ambiguous ownership, sensitive exposure, and authorization gaps rather than pursuing them.
