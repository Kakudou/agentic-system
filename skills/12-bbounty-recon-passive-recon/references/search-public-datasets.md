# Search And Public Dataset Observations

## Purpose

Capture a small, reproducible set of public-index or third-party dataset observations relevant to an in-scope identity. These sources describe their own crawl or collection, not an authoritative view of the target.

## Preconditions

- Confirm source terms and engagement rules permit the review.
- Define the claim sought and the minimum data needed to support or reject it.
- Use sources that expose already-collected public observations, not tools that initiate a new target interaction.

## Passive Authorized Method

Manually review relevant result records and their timestamps. Record the source URL, result locator, observation date, and exact limited claim. Do not use dork catalogs, API query recipes, bulk exports, collection automation, or searches designed to surface credentials or sensitive files. Do not open, request, or validate target URLs surfaced by a result.

## Interpretation And Controls

- Search indexes can be stale, incomplete, personalized, cached, or affected by third-party content.
- Internet-wide datasets may misattribute shared hosting, CDN, cloud, or recycled IP observations.
- A result is evidence of an indexer's observation, not current exposure, target control, or vulnerability. Require independent identity evidence and date context.

## Privacy And Scope Limits

Exclude personal data, credentials, tokens, and unrelated third-party records. Capture result metadata rather than content. Stop and report through the authorized process if sensitive material is encountered.

## Evidence And Handoff

Record provider, source record URL, retrieval date, provider observation date, exact claim, scope mapping, and limitations. Handoff dataset-only claims as leads requiring separately authorized validation.
