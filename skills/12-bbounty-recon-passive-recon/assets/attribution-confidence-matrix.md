# Attribution And Confidence Matrix

Apply independently for each claim. Do not average labels or treat multiple copies of one dataset as corroboration.

| Dimension | Low | Medium | High |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Source reliability | Undated, unclear provenance, or stale | Reputable source with limits or age | Primary/public record with clear date and provenance |
| Identity attribution | Name similarity or inferred association | One documented relationship | Independent authoritative relationship evidence |
| Claim confidence | Single ambiguous observation | Consistent, but incomplete, evidence | Independent dated sources support the limited claim |
| Currentness | No usable date or historical only | Dated but aging observation | Recent, explicitly dated observation |

Use `unverified` when scope or ownership is unclear. No confidence level establishes reachability, authorization, exposure, or vulnerability.
