# Passive Data Handling And Stop Checklist

Before retaining an observation:

- Confirm the identity and source review are within written authorization.
- Record metadata and minimal excerpts, not full archives or bulk datasets.
- Exclude credentials, tokens, private keys, personal data, and irrelevant third-party data.
- Label source date, retrieval time, and attribution limitations.

Stop and hand off when:

- Scope, ownership, or reporting authority is unclear.
- A next step would resolve DNS, request a target URL, authenticate, scan, enumerate, or otherwise contact target infrastructure.
- Sensitive data appears. Do not reproduce it; preserve only the minimum safe locator needed by the authorized report channel.
- A source demands credentials, automation, bulk collection, or terms inconsistent with the engagement.

Record the stop event, reason, source locator, and authorized recipient. Do not attempt remediation or verification.
