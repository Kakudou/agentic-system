# Logo / Geometric Mark Usage

## Source

The mark in `assets/` is supplied by the user. Treat its geometry as authoritative.

Available variants:

- `logo-original.png`
- `logo-black-transparent.png`
- `logo-white-transparent.png`
- `logo-blue-transparent.png`
- `logo-green-transparent.png`
- `logo-orange-transparent.png`

Use the transparent variants whenever possible.

## Preferred variants

### On dark surfaces

Prefer:

1. White mark for maximum legibility.
2. TARDIS blue mark when the surrounding surface provides enough separation.
3. Green mark for an energized/live brand moment.

Orange is an accent variant, not the default brand mark.

### On light surfaces

Prefer the black or blue mark.

## Clear space

Keep open space around the mark. A useful starting point is roughly one quarter of the mark's outer width on each side, adjusted for composition.

Do not crowd it with body copy, controls, dense technical rules, or another logo.

## Minimum size

Keep the internal negative space clearly visible.

Suggested practical floors:

- Digital UI: about 24 px high for a simple icon use; larger when used as a primary brand mark.
- Print: about 8 mm high, subject to output quality.

If the mark begins to close up visually, increase its size rather than simplifying its geometry.

## Allowed treatments

- Flat black, white, blue, green, or controlled orange.
- Subtle blue/green outer glow on dark media.
- Cropping only when clearly intentional and the full mark appears elsewhere.
- A temporary glitch ghost produced by the top artifact layer.
- Masked reveal during motion.

## Forbidden treatments

- Stretching or changing aspect ratio.
- Redrawing the geometry casually.
- Adding soft cartoon shadows.
- Filling it with a busy texture that destroys silhouette.
- Permanently separating its pieces as a “glitch.”
- Using many gradient colors inside the base mark.
- Heavy bevel / 3D chrome unless the user explicitly requests a separate dimensional identity study.

## Relationship to the top artifact layer

The logo belongs to the clean brand layer. The glitch overlay belongs above it.

If animated, duplicate ghosts may briefly offset in blue/green/orange, but the primary mark should remain stationary or quickly resolve to a crisp state.

## Favicon / tiny icon behavior

At very small sizes:

- use the plain silhouette.
- remove glow.
- remove glitch from the base icon.
- if an animated favicon or equivalent is supported and desired, apply only a rare single-pixel/channel pulse.

## Brand hierarchy

The mark should not compete with the artifact's actual title or purpose. It can be:

- a top-left anchor.
- a corner stamp.
- a loading/lock-on glyph.
- a watermark.
- a transition element.
- a document/footer identifier.

Choose one dominant role per artifact.
