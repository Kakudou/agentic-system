# Canon and Character Boundary

Research answers:

> What appears to be true in the relevant external source/canon?

It does not answer:

> What must the character say?

## External Fact vs Persona Knowledge

A true lore fact may still be outside the character's knowledge.

Example:

```text
The setting's sourcebook establishes the king's assassin.
```

If the character is an ordinary citizen who cannot plausibly know that secret, mark:

```text
character_knowledge_fit: unlikely
```

Ōshō should not casually leak it through dialogue.

## Research Does Not Rewrite Canon

A research packet cannot change:

- locked persona identity;
- user-defined alternate universe;
- accepted conversation continuity;
- user-specified canon overrides.

If the user explicitly runs an AU where a canon event differs, public canon is background reference,
not an automatic correction.

## Spoilers and Hidden Knowledge

When the roleplay depends on secrets:

- distinguish world truth from character-accessible truth;
- identify spoiler/secret status when obvious;
- do not force hidden lore into character dialogue.

## Atmosphere

Research may support:

- geography;
- architecture;
- clothing;
- language;
- technology;
- customs;
- historical texture.

Atmospheric detail should be evidence-grounded when accuracy matters, but not every decorative line
requires research.

## No Voice Contamination

The packet is neutral.

Do not write:

```text
"Heh. Corpo bastards built the tower in 2072."
```

Write:

```text
fact: "The tower was completed in 2072."
```

Voice belongs to dialogue.
