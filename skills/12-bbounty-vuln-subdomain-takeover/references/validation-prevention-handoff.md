# Validation, Prevention, And Handoff

## Purpose And Preconditions

Use after a bounded concern is supported by admitted evidence. Require a named remediation owner and a complete evidence package. This guide permits no external validation beyond explicitly approved, low-impact observation and no claim, registration, account creation, content placement, or exploitation.

## Methodology

State the observed relationship, ownership confidence, lifecycle concern, alternative explanations, and impact conditions that the owner must verify internally. Recommend that the owner either remove an obsolete delegation, restore and govern the intended service, or place DNS and service deprovisioning under a coordinated change process. Ask the owner to validate account state through their own authorized control plane.

## Interpretation And Controls

Report an unverified dangling-delegation concern rather than a takeover. Do not assign severity from a provider signature or missing response alone. Limit remediation guidance to the in-scope source name and avoid naming or contacting unrelated tenants.

## Evidence And Handoff

Provide authorization reference, scope decision, raw evidence references, timestamps, observation/inference separation, confidence, limitations, and stop decisions. Use the remediation template and retain evidence only in the approved location.
