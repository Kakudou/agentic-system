# Scope And Ownership Evidence Admission

## Purpose And Preconditions

Use before assessing any dangling-delegation concern. Require written authorization, exact allowed names, permitted evidence sources and observation methods, exclusions, time and rate limits, handling rules, and a stop contact.

## Methodology

Match the observed source name to the approved asset boundary. Attach the authorization reference, source, collection time, collector, and integrity-preserving evidence reference. Admit only facts tied to that source name; provider domains, alias targets, and historical related names do not inherit scope.

## Interpretation And Controls

A public DNS record, link, certificate, or passive-data result does not establish authorization or organizational ownership. Stop for wildcard ambiguity, conflicting ownership evidence, sensitive data, unclear permitted methods, or any transition toward a third-party system. Request a written boundary clarification instead of probing.

## Evidence And Handoff

Record the admission decision, exclusions, ambiguity, and stop rationale in the checklist. Send unanswered scope or ownership questions to the program contact before further work.
