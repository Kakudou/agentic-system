# Uncertainty And Handoff

## Purpose And Preconditions

Transfer a bounded packet for authorized review while preventing observations, interpretations, and unresolved questions from being represented as confirmed findings. Require completed evidence admission, record, matrix, and redaction review.

## Documentation Methodology

Use confidence labels for each material statement. List supporting evidence IDs, missing information, contradictory evidence, material withheld, and the smallest reviewer decision needed. Record the authorized recipient, channel, time, version, access restrictions, and requested review outcome.

## False-Claim And Privacy Controls

- Describe the packet as documentation for review, not as a confirmed vulnerability.
- Do not assert root cause, exploitability, severity, bounty eligibility, remediation, or publication readiness without the responsible process's decision.
- Do not route sensitive originals through an unapproved channel or to an unauthorized recipient.

## Evidence And Handoff

Use the [reproduction handoff template](../assets/reproduction-handoff-template.md). If authorization, receipt, or access control is uncertain, retain the packet in approved private storage and escalate the delivery question.
