# Redaction And State-Change Controls

## Purpose And Preconditions

Protect people, systems, and evidence while documenting a finding. Require an approved private evidence location and recipient channel, or stop pending clarification. This guide authorizes no testing, replay, access, or state change.

## Documentation Methodology

Review proposed text, images, filenames, metadata, and evidence references before sharing. Replace protected values with a data-category description and a restricted evidence reference. Record the redaction reason, original-evidence location, access restriction, and any state-change concern.

## False-Claim And Privacy Controls

- Never include credentials, tokens, session data, private keys, personal, financial, customer, or unrelated third-party data.
- Do not verify, use, replay, distribute, or expand sensitive material.
- Stop distribution and escalate when a record could expose active access, alter a system, or increase harm.

## Evidence And Handoff

Attach the completed [redaction/stop checklist](../assets/redaction-stop-checklist.md). State which authorized reviewer can access originals and which materials were withheld.
