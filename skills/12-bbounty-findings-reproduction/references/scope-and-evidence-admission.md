# Scope And Evidence Admission

## Purpose And Preconditions

Determine whether existing material may support a reproduction-documentation packet. Require a current authorization, an explicit in-scope asset decision, and evidence with known origin and custody. This is not testing guidance or a substitute for program authorization.

## Documentation Methodology

Record the authorization reference, exact approved asset identifier, relevant scope restrictions, evidence identifier, source or collector, capture time, and access-controlled location. Preserve observed wording and distinguish supplied facts from recorder interpretation. Mark excluded or uncertain material as non-admitted.

## False-Claim And Privacy Controls

- Do not infer scope from branding, ownership, or a public address.
- Do not treat unverifiable, altered, or context-free material as evidence.
- Reference protected originals in approved storage rather than copying them into the packet.
- Stop and escalate through the authorized channel if authorization, provenance, recipient access, or scope is unclear.

## Evidence And Handoff

Link every admitted statement to an evidence ID and record the constraints that affect independent review. Handoff unresolved admission questions as blockers, not as assumptions.
