# Redaction And Stop Checklist

Complete before sharing a packet.

- [ ] Scope, authorization, and recipient channel are confirmed.
- [ ] Every included statement has an admitted evidence reference or is labeled uncertain.
- [ ] No credentials, tokens, session data, private keys, or authentication material are included.
- [ ] No unnecessary personal, financial, customer, or third-party data are included.
- [ ] Screenshots, filenames, identifiers, URLs, and metadata were reviewed for protected information.
- [ ] Sensitive details are replaced by restricted evidence references and access conditions.
- [ ] No command, payload, replay sequence, automation, account example, or state-changing guidance is included.
- [ ] Original evidence remains in approved access-controlled storage.

Stop and escalate through the authorized channel if any answer is no, if disclosure could preserve or increase access, if state may change, or if the scope or evidence boundary is uncertain.
