# Privacy And Stop Checklist

- [ ] The record contains no credentials, tokens, session data, private keys, or authentication artifacts.
- [ ] Personal data and contact details are omitted or minimized.
- [ ] Exploit detail is reduced to the minimum needed for the stated review purpose.
- [ ] Each status claim has attributable evidence or is marked `unknown`.
- [ ] Reporter requests and program responses are separately classified.
- [ ] Original platform wording and timestamps are preserved where available.
- [ ] Scope and admission references are present.
- [ ] No platform access, polling, submission, message, or escalation is requested by this record.

Stop and hand off if scope is unclear, data cannot be safely redacted, evidence conflicts materially, a secret is requested, or the next step would contact a third party.
