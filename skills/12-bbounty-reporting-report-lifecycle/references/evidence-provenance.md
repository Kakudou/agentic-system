# Evidence And Provenance

## Purpose

Preserve a reviewable chain from lifecycle claims to their supporting material while separating reporter-originated requests from program-originated responses.

## Preconditions

- Evidence is supplied or explicitly authorized for local documentation.
- The source, capture time, and permitted sensitivity level are known or can be marked unknown.

## Documentation Method

Classify each evidence item as `reporter-request`, `program-response`, `platform-status`, or `review-note`. Do not convert a reporter statement into a program response. Record a stable locator, capture timestamp, custodian, relevant excerpt or digest, and redaction treatment. Quote only the minimum needed to support the record. A lifecycle interpretation must link to one or more evidence IDs.

## No-Message/No-Submission Boundary

Do not contact any party, obtain new evidence from a platform, upload attachments, or transform evidence into a submission.

## Evidence And Handoff

The review packet lists evidence IDs by claim and calls out unavailable originals, conflicting accounts, and redacted portions. Reviewers decide whether evidence supports a status interpretation.

## Sources

- NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 2, *Computer Security Incident Handling Guide*
