# Review Handoff

## Purpose

Package lifecycle documentation for an authorized human reviewer without making an outcome decision.

## Preconditions

- Lifecycle record is complete to the extent evidenced.
- Privacy checklist result and unresolved uncertainties are recorded.

## Documentation Method

Complete `../assets/review-handoff-template.md`. State the proposed review question, current interpreted status, confidence, observations since the prior review, supporting and conflicting evidence IDs, privacy constraints, and explicit decision owner. Keep the handoff factual and bounded.

## No-Message/No-Submission Boundary

Do not send the handoff to a platform, program, or third party. Do not mark the report accepted, paid, closed, or escalated based on the handoff.

## Evidence And Handoff

Deliver only through the user-authorized review channel. A reviewer may accept, reject, request clarification, or retain uncertainty; document that decision as a new review note with its source.

## Sources
