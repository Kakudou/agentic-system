# Privacy And Uncertainty Controls

## Purpose

Keep lifecycle records useful without retaining secrets, unnecessary personal data, exploit-enabling detail, or unsupported conclusions.

## Preconditions

- The material has been classified for sensitivity before inclusion.
- The reviewer can identify the evidence supporting each non-unknown field.

## Documentation Method

Use `../assets/privacy-stop-checklist.md` for every new observation. Prefer identifiers, hashes, redacted excerpts, and source locators over credentials, session material, personal contact details, or full proof-of-concept content. Label conclusions `confirmed`, `supported`, `uncertain`, or `unknown` according to the matrix. Describe uncertainty and its cause in the record.

## No-Message/No-Submission Boundary

Do not request clarification from a platform, disclose material, or make a risk decision. Stop and hand off when unauthorized disclosure, scope ambiguity, coercive language, or a request for credentials is present.

## Evidence And Handoff

Document the checklist result, redactions, omissions, and stop reason. A stop is a review condition, not a closure or escalation instruction.
