# Publication Authorization And Embargo Admission

## Purpose

Decide whether preparation of a public writeup may proceed and establish its allowed scope. Authorization to report a finding is not automatically authorization to disclose it publicly.

## Preconditions

- A recorded authorization source from the program owner or another authorized party.
- The proposed audience, timing, and disclosure scope are known.
- Any embargo, remediation, legal, or contractual constraint is available for review.

## Documentation Method

Record the authority, date, permitted subject matter, permitted attribution, embargo end or release condition, and any required review. Compare every planned section against that record. Treat silence, informal expectation, and an unverified status as insufficient. If disclosure terms change, stop and obtain updated authorization.

## Privacy And False-Claim Controls

Do not infer permission from a resolved status, bounty payment, public program page, or prior disclosures. Do not state that a vulnerability is fixed, public, or authorized unless the supporting record says so. Keep private report content and correspondence out of the draft unless expressly admitted.

## Evidence And Handoff

Attach a citation or stable reference to the authorization record, note the embargo decision, and list excluded material. If any field is missing or contradictory, mark the preparation `blocked` and hand off the question without drafting public detail.
