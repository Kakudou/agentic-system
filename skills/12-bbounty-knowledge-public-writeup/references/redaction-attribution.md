# Redaction, Attribution, And Uncertainty

## Purpose

Prevent harmful disclosure, protect people and organizations, and represent credit and uncertainty fairly.

## Preconditions

- A proposed draft and associated public-admissible evidence references exist.
- Authorization defines permitted naming and attribution.

## Documentation Method

Review text, metadata, images, diagrams, filenames, links, and captions for identifiers, secrets, personal data, internal details, and hidden contextual clues. Replace target-specific detail with approved generalization. Attribute organizations, reporters, researchers, and sources only as authorized. Label uncertainty directly where evidence is incomplete.

## Privacy And False-Claim Controls

Remove tokens, credentials, account data, private URLs, internal hostnames, report identifiers, contact details, timestamps that create risk, and unapproved names. Do not imply blame, remediation completion, exploitability, or ownership from circumstantial evidence. A redaction that cannot be independently checked is an open review item, not a completed control.

## Evidence And Handoff

Record each redaction category, its reason, and whether a reviewer verified it. Record allowed and disallowed attribution. Escalate potentially identifying or safety-relevant material for authorized review instead of deciding to disclose it.
