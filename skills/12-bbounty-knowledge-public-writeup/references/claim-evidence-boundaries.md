# Claim And Evidence Boundaries

## Purpose

Ensure each public statement is attributable, proportionate to its evidence, and clear about uncertainty.

## Preconditions

- Evidence has provenance, collection context, and an admissibility decision for public use.
- Claims are split into atomic statements.

## Documentation Method

For each claim, record the evidence reference, observation date, scope, confidence, and permitted wording in the claim/evidence matrix. Classify statements as observed fact, bounded inference, or unresolved question. Remove claims that lack public-admissible support rather than relying on private evidence or memory.

## Privacy And False-Claim Controls

Do not convert a hypothesis into a fact, extrapolate impact beyond verified conditions, or attribute actions or intent without evidence. Do not expose raw artifacts merely to strengthen a claim. Use qualified language when evidence is partial, and preserve uncertainty in the handoff.

## Evidence And Handoff

Give reviewers the matrix, not sensitive source artifacts by default. Identify every omitted or disputed claim and the reason. A reviewer must be able to trace each retained public statement to an admissible evidence reference.
