# Review And Publication-Ready Handoff

## Purpose

Package a public-writeup draft for an authorized reviewer without publishing, scheduling, or representing approval.

## Preconditions

- Authorization and embargo admission is confirmed.
- Claim/evidence and redaction reviews are complete or clearly marked with blockers.
- A reviewer or review route is identified.

## Documentation Method

Provide the draft, outline, claim/evidence matrix, checklist status, permitted attribution, exclusions, and open questions using the handoff template. State the proposed audience and release condition. Ask reviewers to approve, revise, defer, or block the material, without treating silence as approval.

## Privacy And False-Claim Controls

Do not include private source artifacts, credentials, personal data, or unapproved correspondence in the handoff by default. Do not label a draft `publication-ready` when authorization, embargo, claim support, or redaction status is unresolved.

## Evidence And Handoff

The handoff records who must review which decision, the evidence references they need, and the remaining blockers. Its terminal state is `ready_for_review`, `blocked`, or `returned_for_revision`; it never records publication.
