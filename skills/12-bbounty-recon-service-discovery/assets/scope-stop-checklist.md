# Scope and Stop Checklist

- [ ] Current authorization or policy reference recorded
- [ ] Asset identifier explicitly in scope
- [ ] Permitted service class recorded
- [ ] Allowed observation sources recorded
- [ ] Time, rate, and retention limits recorded
- [ ] No active scan, probe, authentication, enumeration, or protocol interaction planned
- [ ] Ownership boundary checked for tenant, vendor, and third-party exclusions
- [ ] Stop contact and escalation channel recorded

Stop and hand off if authorization is missing, scope or ownership is ambiguous, sensitive data appears, or further validation would require interaction.
