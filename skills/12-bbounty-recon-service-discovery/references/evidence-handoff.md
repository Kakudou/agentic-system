# Evidence and Handoff

## Purpose and Preconditions

Prepare a reviewable reconnaissance handoff that lets the next authorized owner assess the observation without repeating or expanding activity. Confirm the recipient and permitted evidence channel.

## Authorized Bounded Method

Use the supplied handoff template. Include only the minimum source excerpts or stable references needed to reproduce the reasoning, plus timestamps, scope decision, confidence, and limitations. Redact credentials, tokens, personal data, and unnecessary operational detail.

## Interpretation

A handoff documents an observation, not a confirmed vulnerability or authorization for follow-on testing. Clearly distinguish source fact, analyst interpretation, and requested next decision.

## False-Positive Controls

- Link every assertion to a source or mark it as an inference.
- Include competing explanations and exclusions.
- Do not include inferred versions, banners, or reachability claims without authorized evidence.
- Ensure the recipient can see the scope limitation and stop condition.

## Rate and Scope Limits

Do not collect additional material to make a handoff look complete. Stay within retention requirements and share only through the approved program channel.

## Evidence and Handoff

Attach the completed worksheet, matrix entries used, policy reference, and a concise decision request: validate ownership, confirm scope, authorize a separate activity, or close as insufficient evidence.
