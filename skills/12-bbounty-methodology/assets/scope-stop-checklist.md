# Scope And Stop Checklist

- [ ] Authorization source is current and attributable.
- [ ] Exact asset and activity are explicitly permitted.
- [ ] Applicable rules, exclusions, and safety restrictions are recorded.
- [ ] Reporting contact and sensitive-data handling expectations are known.
- [ ] Scope is rechecked after any target, method, role, or impact change.
- [ ] Work is paused for ambiguity, ownership doubt, unintended exposure, instability, or risk of harm.
- [ ] Only minimal evidence needed for authorized disclosure is retained.
