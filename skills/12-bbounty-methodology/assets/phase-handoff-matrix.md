# Phase And Handoff Matrix

| From | To | Required handoff | Stop when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Engagement | Scope admission | Authorization source, target, restrictions | Authorization is absent, expired, or contradictory |
| Scope admission | Recon | Allowed asset, rules citation, stop triggers | Asset is excluded or ownership is unresolved |
| Recon | Hunting | Admitted asset, observation, bounded hypothesis | Hypothesis or method exceeds authorization |
| Hunting | Finding | Observation, minimal evidence, impact boundary | Harm, instability, or sensitive exposure occurs |
| Finding | Reporting | Validation decision, minimized evidence, disclosure route | Facts, scope, or evidence remain unresolved |
