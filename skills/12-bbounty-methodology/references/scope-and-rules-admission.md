# Scope And Rules Admission

## Purpose

Convert the governing authorization into a clear, auditable decision for a target or activity.

## Preconditions

- Admitted engagement mode and current authorization evidence.
- Candidate asset or activity to assess.

## Process

1. Identify the exact asset, owner relationship, and applicable scope/rules version.
2. Classify it as explicitly allowed, explicitly excluded, or unresolved.
3. Record operational restrictions, sensitive-data rules, rate/impact boundaries, and the disclosure channel.
4. Admit only explicitly allowed activity; send unresolved or conflicting cases for clarification.

## Limits

- Discovery does not expand scope.
- Do not infer wildcard coverage, ownership, or permission from naming, DNS, branding, or prior practice.

## Evidence And Handoff

Provide the admission decision, scope citation, restrictions, and stop triggers to recon or hunting. An excluded or unresolved item is a stop, not a research backlog.
