# Uncertainty And Ethics

## Purpose

Provide fail-closed decisions when authorization, ownership, safety, or evidence is uncertain.

## Preconditions

- A decision cannot be supported by current engagement evidence.

## Process

1. Label the uncertainty: authorization, scope, ownership, safety, evidence, or disclosure.
2. Pause the affected activity and preserve only minimal decision evidence.
3. Request clarification through the authorized channel or choose a lower-risk, clearly permitted path.
4. Resume only after the uncertainty is resolved and admission is updated.

## Limits

- Never test to resolve whether testing is authorized.
- Stop immediately for unintended sensitive-data access, service instability, or credible risk of harm.

## Evidence And Handoff

Record the uncertainty, paused activity, decision owner, clarification request, and resolution or stop outcome. Do not pass unresolved work to a testing skill.
