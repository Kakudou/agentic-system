# Finding-To-Report Handoff

## Purpose

Turn a safely validated observation into a clear coordinated-disclosure report handoff.

## Preconditions

- Finding has passed the applicable validation decision.
- Evidence is minimized, protected, and sufficient to support reproduction and impact assessment.

## Process

1. Distinguish observed facts from assumptions and state the affected admitted asset.
2. Describe security impact conservatively and record conditions required for reproduction.
3. Redact or minimize sensitive material; use the authorized reporting channel.
4. Transfer the evidence package to the reporting sub-skill for composition and quality review.

## Limits

- Do not claim severity, exploitability, data access, or chaining beyond evidence.
- Do not publish, retain, or redistribute sensitive material outside approved handling rules.

## Evidence And Handoff

Provide scope reference, validation result, minimal reproduction evidence, impact rationale, redaction record, and disclosure route. Stop and notify the authorized contact when exposure requires immediate handling.
