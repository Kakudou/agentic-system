# Evidence Admission

## Purpose

Decide whether supplied material may support a validation packet. Admission permits documentation only; it does not authorize collection, testing, disclosure, or a finding conclusion.

## Preconditions

- Written authorization and program constraints.
- A source record with locator, observation time, collection context, and handling classification where available.

## Bounded Documentation Method

1. Record the authorization reference and the exact asset, date, account, and activity limits it establishes.
2. Assign each submitted item an evidence ID and retain its original locator or approved secure-storage reference.
3. Admit only material whose supplied target association and collection context fit those limits. Record excluded items and the reason without copying sensitive content.
4. Mark missing provenance, ownership, timing, or integrity information as unknown.

## Interpretation And Uncertainty

Admitted means eligible to support a documented observation, not that the observation is accurate, current, in scope, or safe to disclose. Indirect or stale material can support a question for the recipient but not a definitive claim.

## False-Positive And Privacy Controls

- Do not infer ownership from a name, provider, historical reference, or branding similarity.
- Do not treat a screenshot, log excerpt, or tool label as self-authenticating.
- Keep credentials, tokens, personal data, private content, and full request bodies out of the packet unless the recipient's approved channel requires them.

## Evidence And Handoff

Record the admission decision, authorization reference, evidence IDs, excluded material, and unresolved scope questions. Route missing authorization or unsafe handling needs to the authorized program contact.
