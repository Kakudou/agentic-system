## Confidence Rule

- High: direct, traceable evidence with no material unresolved conflict.
- Medium: traceable evidence with a limited gap in directness, recency, or corroboration.
- Low: single-source, indirect, stale, ambiguous, or attribution-limited evidence.

Confidence applies only to the stated claim. It does not establish current behavior, impact, severity, exploitability, or bounty eligibility.
