# Redaction And Stop Checklist

## Stop And Escalate If

- Authorization, asset ownership, scope, recipient, or handling channel is missing or unclear.
- The next action would require target interaction, a replay, altered input, account change, or access beyond the supplied evidence.
- Evidence includes credentials, tokens, session material, personal data, private content, financial data, or unrelated third-party records.
- The claim depends on assumed access, assumed impact, a scanner label, or an unverified duplicate or known-behavior conclusion.
- The material indicates active harm or cannot be safely minimized.

## Before Handoff

- Replace sensitive values with minimal descriptors and approved evidence references.
- Preserve source locators, timestamps, scope basis, and conflicting observations.
- Label observations, inferences, hypotheses, and unknowns separately.
- State the impact boundary and avoid severity, exploit, or remediation assertions.
- Confirm the recipient and channel are authorized for the retained detail.
