# Uncertainty and Validation

## Purpose and Preconditions

Use whenever evidence is incomplete, indirect, environment-specific, or awaiting reviewer confirmation. It does not authorize more testing.

## Documentation Method

Label claims as observed, supported interpretation, or unverified hypothesis. Assign confidence from the quality and repeatability of the admitted evidence, list assumptions, and specify the smallest safe validation needed from the program owner.

## Interpretation

High confidence requires direct, reproducible evidence within the documented boundary. Moderate confidence requires relevant but incomplete support. Low confidence is a lead for review, not an impact assertion. Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence, but it is also not proof of impact.

## False-Claim and Privacy Controls

Do not convert uncertainty into probability, severity, or business consequence. Do not request or retain sensitive material to eliminate uncertainty when a program-side review can validate it.

## Evidence and Handoff

Record the classification, limitation, and requested validation in the [dimension and claim confidence matrix](../assets/dimension-claim-confidence-matrix.md).
