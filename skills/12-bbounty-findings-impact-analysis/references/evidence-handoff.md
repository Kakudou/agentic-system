# Evidence Handoff

## Purpose and Preconditions

Use after the impact record is complete and a permitted recipient or submission channel is known.

## Documentation Method

Package the finding identifier, scope, claims, evidence locators, redactions, confidence, limitations, and requested reviewer action. Keep original evidence separate from the report when program policy requires it; preserve locator consistency.

## Interpretation

The handoff communicates bounded evidence, not a severity decision or confirmation of broader harm. A reviewer can reproduce the documented observation or evaluate the stated validation gap without unnecessary attack detail.

## False-Claim and Privacy Controls

Share only through the authorized channel. Remove secrets, personal data, session material, and unnecessary internal details. Do not state remediation, ownership, customer impact, or breach status as facts unless the program has supplied evidence.

## Evidence and Handoff

Use the [impact handoff template](../assets/impact-handoff-template.md). Retain only policy-permitted evidence and record any withheld material and reason.
