# Policy And Stop Checklist

- [ ] Written authorization covers the finding, evidence sources, recipient, and current handling rules.
- [ ] Each material record is attributable, in scope, and linked to collection context and time.
- [ ] Sensitive values, credentials, tokens, and unnecessary personal data are excluded or redacted.
- [ ] Observed impact, inference, prerequisites, and uncertainty are documented separately.
- [ ] Each material claim has evidence references and alternatives or limitations.
- [ ] A platform policy is mapped only when its exact supplied version and relevant sections are available.
- [ ] No severity, eligibility, acceptance, remediation, or reward claim is presented as a fact without an authorized decision.
- [ ] Stop for scope ambiguity, unsafe handling, missing policy, conflicting evidence, or a request for further testing.
- [ ] Record the stop reason, evidence retained, and decision required from the authorized contact.
