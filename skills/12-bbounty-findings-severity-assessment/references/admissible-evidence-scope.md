# Admissible Evidence And Scope

## Purpose

Determine whether existing records may support an assessment and when review must stop.

## Preconditions

- Written authorization identifies in-scope assets, engagement window, handling rules, and escalation contact.
- Each proposed record has a source, locator, collection context, and time when available.

## Documentation Method

For every admitted record, record the authorization reference, scope match, source locator, observation time, collector or origin, and redaction status. Exclude or quarantine material that lacks provenance, falls outside scope, or cannot be safely retained.

## Bias And False-Claim Controls

- Admissibility does not establish ownership, current behavior, exploitability, or impact.
- Keep source facts, analyst interpretation, and requested follow-up separate.
- Stop rather than broaden scope for ambiguous ownership, third-party material, credentials, personal data, or conflicting instructions.

## Evidence And Handoff

Provide the admitted and excluded evidence lists, exclusion reasons, scope gaps, handling restrictions, and the decision required to resume.
