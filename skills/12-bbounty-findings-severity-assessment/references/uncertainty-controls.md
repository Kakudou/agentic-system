# Likelihood And Uncertainty Controls

## Purpose

Separate observed impact from the evidence supporting reach, prerequisites, repeatability, and reliability.

## Preconditions

- An admitted evidence set and bounded impact statements exist.
- Any claim about preconditions or reach identifies its supporting observation.

## Documentation Method

For each claim, label it as observed, bounded inference, or unsupported. Record supporting evidence, contradictory evidence, prerequisites, alternatives, and confidence. Describe uncertainty directly; do not convert it into a favorable or unfavorable likelihood estimate.

## Bias And False-Claim Controls

- A successful observation does not prove repeatability, scale, or access by other actors.
- A missing observation does not prove absence; state the collection limitation.
- Do not use confidence as a severity multiplier or use a likelihood label to conceal an assumption.

## Evidence And Handoff

Hand off the confidence and claim matrix, unresolved prerequisites, alternative explanations, and any approval needed for separate validation.
