# Platform-Policy Interpretation

## Purpose

Apply an assessment framework or platform policy only when the current, supplied policy defines its applicability.

## Preconditions

- The exact policy or framework version is supplied by the program, recipient, or authorized record.
- Scope, eligibility conditions, and relevant definitions can be cited from that supplied material.

## Documentation Method

Quote or reference the relevant policy section, then map each policy condition to evidence, an explicit unknown, or a non-applicable condition. Record framework selections and policy interpretation separately. If no policy is supplied, state `not supplied` and hand off the evidence without a policy conclusion.

## Bias And False-Claim Controls

- Do not infer a program policy from its name, historical practice, community discussion, or another platform.
- Do not treat CVSS, OWASP guidance, or a vulnerability class as a platform decision.
- Do not claim report acceptance, severity, safe harbor, duplicate status, or payout.

## Evidence And Handoff

Provide the policy version and section references, condition-to-evidence mapping, unresolved definitions, and the recipient authorized to decide the outcome.

## Sources

- FIRST, [CVSS](https://www.first.org/cvss/)
- OWASP, [Risk Rating Methodology](https://owasp.org/www-community/OWASP_Risk_Rating_Methodology)
- Program-supplied policy: authoritative only when provided for the current assessment.
