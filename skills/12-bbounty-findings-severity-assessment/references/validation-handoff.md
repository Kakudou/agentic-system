# Claim Review And Handoff

## Purpose

Produce a minimal, evidence-linked packet for an authorized reviewer without overstating what the evidence proves.

## Preconditions

- Scope and evidence admission are complete or explicitly blocked.
- Impact, uncertainty, and any supplied-policy mapping are documented.
- A recipient and safe channel are known.

## Documentation Method

Review each material statement against its cited evidence. Ensure observations, inferences, unknowns, and policy interpretation are visibly separate. Retain references instead of copying sensitive data, and record a handoff, hold, or close decision with its reason.

## Bias And False-Claim Controls

- Require evidence references for every material impact or policy statement.
- Preserve contradictory records and alternative explanations.
- Do not represent reviewer acceptance, severity, remediation priority, or reward as an assessment result.

## Evidence And Handoff

Use the handoff template to provide scope status, evidence references, impact boundaries, claim confidence, policy status, redactions, stop events, and a clear requested decision.
