# Sensitive Data Stop Checklist

- [ ] Stop further requests and do not inspect or download content.
- [ ] Do not copy, store, replay, validate, or share the material.
- [ ] Record only time, asset, authorized method, and material category.
- [ ] Redact all identifiers from any permitted evidence.
- [ ] Notify the authorized reporting or incident channel.
- [ ] Request owner-side classification and containment.
- [ ] Resume only with explicit written authorization and handling safeguards.
