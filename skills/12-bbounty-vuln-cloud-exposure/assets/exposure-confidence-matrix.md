# Exposure Confidence Matrix

| Confidence | Use when | Required evidence | Handoff language |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Confirmed | Owner validates an unintended public boundary. | Scope, attribution, redacted observation, owner validation. | Confirmed unintended public exposure. |
| Supported | Attribution and observation are strong, but owner validation is pending. | Scope, attribution, redacted observation, alternatives considered. | Potentially inconsistent public boundary; owner validation requested. |
| Indeterminate | The response or ownership has credible alternative explanations. | Minimal observation and unresolved limitations. | Observation is insufficient to establish exposure. |
| Stop / escalate | Sensitive material appeared or scope is unclear. | Redacted stop event and escalation reference. | Assessment stopped; owner review required. |

Do not infer impact from names, status codes, or provider context alone.
