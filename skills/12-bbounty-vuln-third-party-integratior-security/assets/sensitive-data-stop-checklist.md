# Sensitive-Data and Stop Checklist

Stop and escalate if any item applies:

- Authorization, ownership, vendor relationship, or permitted method is unclear.
- Credentials, tokens, keys, session material, or authentication artifacts appear.
- Personal, financial, health, customer, or other sensitive data is encountered outside an approved redacted fixture.
- An observation would send traffic, alter state, cross a tenant boundary, or affect availability.
- Evidence cannot be safely redacted or stored in an approved location.
- A vendor, legal, privacy, or incident-response boundary is implicated.

Record only the minimum approved metadata: time, authorized scope reference, high-level condition, stop reason, evidence location or non-retention decision, and notified owner.
