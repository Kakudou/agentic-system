# Validation and Prevention

## Purpose

Corroborate non-invasive observations and turn demonstrated conditions into owner-actionable preventive remediation.

## Preconditions

- An authorized, redacted observation with its scope and limitations.
- A second approved evidence source or an owner able to confirm the condition.

## Method

Validate by comparing documentation with owner-supplied configuration, approved logs, or a repeated normal-use observation. Do not escalate to traffic generation, credential use, or boundary crossing. Describe the condition, affected boundary, evidence quality, and uncertainty. Recommend controls such as ownership assignment, data minimization, least privilege, destination restrictions, independent validation, logging, retention limits, and tested incident contacts.

## Interpretation and Scope Controls

Corroboration establishes confidence in an observation, not exploitability. Do not assign impact from hypothetical attack paths. Keep vendor responsibilities distinct from application responsibilities, and request written owner confirmation where control ownership is disputed.

## Evidence and Handoff

Provide the redacted evidence references, false-positive controls, limitations, owner, vendor escalation route, and requested due date. Retest only under a new explicit authorization that names the permitted method.
