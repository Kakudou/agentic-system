# Sensitive-Data And Stop Checklist

Stop capture and do not retain the value when any of these appear:

- Passwords, secrets, API keys, bearer tokens, cookies, authorization codes, assertions, or state values.
- Recovery links, recovery codes, MFA seeds, QR codes, one-time passcodes, or push prompts.
- Personal data, another user's content, privileged content, tenant data, or a third-party identity-provider session.
- An unlisted host, ambiguous scope, rate-limit warning, unexpected side effect, or access beyond the supplied account.

Before handoff:

- Remove values, identifiers, query strings, full headers, and unapproved screenshots.
- Record only the category, redacted location, UTC time, scope status, and permitted evidence reference.
- Notify the approved contact when a stop condition or unexpected access occurred.
- State what was not collected and that no account-access or bypass action was attempted.
