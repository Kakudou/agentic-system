## Controls

- [ ] Evidence copies are not counted as independent corroboration.
- [ ] Observation confidence is separate from cause and impact confidence.
- [ ] Negative and disconfirming evidence is retained.
- [ ] Missing provenance, scope, or context is recorded as a limit.
- [ ] No numerical score substitutes for evidence quality.
