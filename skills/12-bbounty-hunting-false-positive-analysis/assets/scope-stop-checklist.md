# Scope And Stop Checklist

## Before Validation

- [ ] Written authorization reference is present.
- [ ] Asset, tenant, and account boundary are explicitly in scope.
- [ ] Exclusions, time window, data handling, and disclosure rules are known.
- [ ] The finding claim and original evidence references are available.
- [ ] An authorized triage or escalation recipient is named.

## Stop And Escalate

- [ ] Scope, authorization, provenance, or evidence handling is ambiguous.
- [ ] Unexpected credentials, personal data, or other sensitive data appears.
- [ ] Service degradation, third-party exposure, or an authorization anomaly is indicated.
- [ ] Resolving uncertainty would require new interaction, broader access, or scope expansion.
- [ ] A known-issue record is restricted or cannot be safely compared.

Stop review, retain only necessary evidence references, and notify the named recipient. Do not convert a stop signal into confirmation.
