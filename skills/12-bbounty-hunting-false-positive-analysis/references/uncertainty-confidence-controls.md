# Uncertainty And Confidence Controls

## Purpose

Express the strength and limits of a validation decision without formulas, unsupported precision, or confidence inflation.

## Preconditions

- Evidence has been admitted and separated from interpretations.
- Alternative explanations and material evidence gaps are recorded.

## Bounded Methodology

1. Rate observation confidence separately from causal and impact-inference confidence as low, medium, or high.
2. Base each rating on provenance, contextual completeness, recency, corroboration, and fit to the stated claim.
3. Reduce confidence for stale, indirect, ambiguous, single-source, or conflicting evidence.
4. State what additional authorized evidence could change the decision rather than deriving a numerical score.

## Observation Interpretation

High confidence means the narrow stated claim is well supported, not that every consequence is known. Multiple copies of a single event do not increase corroboration. Conflicting evidence should be described, not averaged away.

## False-Positive, Bias, And Scope Controls

- Tool severity, common vulnerability labels, and analyst familiarity are not evidence-quality measures.
- Do not use a score threshold to override missing provenance or scope authorization.
- Preserve negative and disconfirming evidence alongside supportive material.

## Evidence And Handoff

Use the matrix to show claim level, evidence basis, alternatives, confidence, and decision limit. Route medium- or low-confidence material claims to review rather than presenting them as confirmed.
