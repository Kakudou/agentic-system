# Evidence And Handoff

## Purpose

Produce a bounded, reviewable validation packet for an authorized triage or reporting owner.

## Preconditions

- Scope-admission decision and evidence inventory are complete.
- The validation decision, limitations, and recipient are known.

## Bounded Methodology

1. State the finding claim and decision in one narrow sentence.
2. List evidence references, baseline comparison, known-issue review, and causal assessment separately.
3. Preserve uncertainties, alternative explanations, scope restrictions, and any evidence that would change the decision.
4. Identify the authorized recipient and next decision needed. Do not include operational instructions, credentials, sensitive data beyond need, or claims not supported by the packet.

## Observation Interpretation

A handoff communicates an evidence-bound decision, not a guarantee of acceptance, severity, root cause, or remediation. `Needs-review` is a valid terminal validation outcome when the record cannot safely support a stronger conclusion.

## False-Positive, Bias, And Scope Controls

- Retain disconfirming evidence and rejected explanations.
- Do not omit material limitations to make a report appear stronger.
- Share only with parties authorized by the engagement and program rules.

## Evidence And Handoff

Complete the handoff template and attach immutable references or approved redactions. The recipient owns any further validation, triage, remediation, or disclosure decision.
