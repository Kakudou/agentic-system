# Uncertainty And Validation

## Purpose And Preconditions

Use before a historical record is handed off. Require admitted sources, provenance and access labels, relevance assessment, stated limitations, and privacy review. This is documentation review, not live-target validation.

## Documentation Methodology

Separate historical facts from interpretations and recommendations. Check that each factual statement has a source reference, dates are retained, contradictions are visible, and confidence reflects evidence quality, relevance, completeness, and recency. Use low confidence for weak, stale, partial, or conflicting support; do not average away a material unknown.

## Privacy And False-Claim Controls

Do not upgrade confidence because a claim is plausible, frequently repeated, or similar to another finding. Do not call a historical record current, reproducible, exploitable, applicable, unique, or validated without direct authorized evidence for that conclusion. Recheck redactions before review circulation.

## Evidence And Handoff

Record the review disposition as documented, reviewed, needs-clarification, or stopped, with outstanding questions and the accountable reviewer. A recipient may retain, revise, or reject the record; handoff creates no new factual finding.
