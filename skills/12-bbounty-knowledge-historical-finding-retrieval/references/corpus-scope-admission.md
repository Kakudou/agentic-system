# Authorized Corpus And Scope Admission

## Purpose And Preconditions

Use before documenting any historical material. Require written authorization, a defined corpus or supplied item set, allowed purpose, scope boundaries, retention period, handling rules, and an escalation contact.

## Documentation Methodology

Record the corpus or item identifier, owner, authorization reference, source type, collection or publication date when available, allowed use, and admission decision. Admit each item independently: similarity of program, organization, technology, or topic does not extend authorization to another source.

## Privacy And False-Claim Controls

Do not admit material whose origin, access right, scope, or retention right is unclear. Exclude credentials, personal data, customer content, private program material, and operational details not needed for the historical statement. Admission proves only permission to document the source, not that its claims are true or current.

## Evidence And Handoff

Mark every item admitted, excluded, restricted, or awaiting clarification with a reason. Hand scope or authorization questions to the corpus owner or program contact. Do not complete a historical record from excluded or unresolved material.
