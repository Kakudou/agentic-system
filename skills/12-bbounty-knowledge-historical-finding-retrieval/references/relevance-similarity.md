# Relevance Criteria And Similarity Controls

## Purpose And Preconditions

Use after corpus admission to decide whether a historical observation is useful context for a stated review question. Require cited historical facts and enough declared context to compare scope, time, environment, and claim type.

## Documentation Methodology

State the question and evaluate relevance by: same authorized scope or relationship; comparable environment or control boundary; comparable observation type; recency; and material differences. Record each criterion separately in the relevance/confidence matrix. Label relevance supported, conditional, unsupported, or unknown rather than collapsing it into a numeric conclusion.

## Privacy And False-Claim Controls

Do not infer production equivalence, ownership, implementation details, vulnerability presence, impact, or duplicate status from a name, technology label, report category, or superficial similarity. Do not expose target-specific identifiers merely to improve matching. A historical analogy is context, not confirmation.

## Evidence And Handoff

Link every relevance conclusion to its source references and list disconfirming facts, missing context, and date limitations. Escalate material similarity judgments when the comparison could affect disclosure, remediation, prioritization, or authorization.
