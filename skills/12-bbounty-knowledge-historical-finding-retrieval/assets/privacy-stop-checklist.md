# Privacy And Stop Checklist

Complete before retaining or handing off a historical knowledge record.

- [ ] Authorization, corpus scope, allowed purpose, and retention right are documented.
- [ ] Each source has provenance and access status; unresolved sources are excluded or marked stopped.
- [ ] The record contains no credentials, tokens, keys, session material, personal data, customer content, or unnecessary target identifiers.
- [ ] Restricted details are redacted and their handling limitation is recorded.
- [ ] Each factual statement has a source reference; interpretations and unknowns are labeled.
- [ ] Similarity has not been represented as current applicability, exploitability, impact, novelty, or validation.
- [ ] Recipient authorization and approved destination are confirmed.

Stop and escalate for uncertain scope, source rights, classification, recipient authorization, conflicting provenance, or material that cannot be safely minimized.
