# Sources and Filtering

## Discovery Communities

Use these as discovery pools:

- `r/todayilearned`
- `r/InterestingFacts`
- `r/datahoarder`
- `r/technology`

Choose one community first.

Only try another when the selected community yields no usable candidate.

Do not scrape all four on every invocation.

## Freshness

Prefer items surfaced in roughly the last 48 hours.

The fact itself may be historical; freshness refers to the discovery item, not when the underlying
event happened.

## Reddit Is Discovery, Not Proof

A Reddit post title is not sufficient evidence for a factual claim.

When the post links to an underlying source:

1. inspect that source when accessible;
2. confirm the core claim;
3. paraphrase conservatively.

When the post is a self-post or the underlying source is unavailable, independently verify the claim
from a credible source when practical.

Skip claims that cannot be grounded without guesswork.

## Exclude

Skip:

- politics/ideology;
- war, death, tragedy, disaster, health panic;
- medical advice;
- sensational claims with weak sourcing;
- memes/jokes;
- obvious promotional content;
- duplicate facts within the invocation;
- claims whose interesting part disappears once accurately qualified.

## Signal

Votes/comments may help reject obvious noise, but they are not truth metrics.

A lower-ranked well-sourced fact is preferable to a viral unsupported claim.
