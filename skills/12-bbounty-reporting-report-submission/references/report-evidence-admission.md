# Report and Evidence Admission

## Purpose

Decide whether an existing report and its evidence references may enter submission preparation. This is a documentation gate, not a test, evidence-collection, or submission process.

## Preconditions

- Written reporting authorization and current scope reference.
- Stable finding ID, quality approval or named reviewer decision, and approved submitter.
- Evidence provenance, handling classification, and permitted recipient boundary.

## Documentation Process

1. Record the report version or immutable identifier, quality decision, finding ID, and source locator.
2. Admit an evidence item only when it supports a named report claim, has provenance, and is permitted for the intended recipient.
3. Record evidence by identifier and minimum necessary description. Reference, rather than duplicate, restricted content.
4. Mark missing provenance, scope mismatch, unsupported claims, or recipient uncertainty as blockers. Stop rather than repair the evidence or collect more.

## Uncertainty and Privacy Controls

- Do not infer authorization, claim support, or recipient access.
- Do not copy credentials, tokens, session material, personal data, customer content, or out-of-scope material into the preparation package.
- Escalate uncertain classifications to the named reviewer; retain only permitted metadata until resolved.

## Evidence and Handoff

Provide the admission decision, excluded items, claim-to-evidence references, handling restrictions, and blockers to the next review stage.
