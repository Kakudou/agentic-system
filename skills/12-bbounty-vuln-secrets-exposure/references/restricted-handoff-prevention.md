# Restricted Handoff, Prevention, And Remediation

## Purpose And Preconditions

Transfer a minimized report to the authorized recipient and state prevention-oriented recommendations. Require a designated restricted channel and a recipient accountable for remediation.

## Documentation Methodology

Provide the authorization reference, redacted locator, classification, uncertainty labels, and stop/minimization record. Recommend owner-led containment, removal from exposed surfaces, rotation or revocation where applicable, access review, and safeguards against recurrence. Recommendations are not confirmation that any action is necessary or completed.

## Privacy And Scope Controls

Do not include the material, reproduction steps, raw artifacts, or claims of validity. Do not distribute outside the approved channel or track remediation in public systems. Do not perform remediation unless separately authorized.

## Evidence And Handoff

Use the [restricted remediation and handoff template](../assets/restricted-remediation-handoff-template.md). Record recipient, time, and acknowledgement status; if acknowledgement is unavailable, mark the handoff pending without retransmitting sensitive detail.
