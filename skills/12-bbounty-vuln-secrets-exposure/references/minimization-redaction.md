# Immediate Stop, Minimization, And Redaction

## Purpose And Preconditions

Prevent further disclosure when sensitive-looking material is encountered. This applies immediately on observation and does not require a determination of authenticity.

## Documentation Methodology

Stop inspection, record only a redacted locator and time, and note the containment action taken. Use a coarse boundary description such as the affected component or access surface; omit values and unnecessary surrounding context.

## Privacy And Scope Controls

Do not copy, quote, screenshot, download, cache, paste, attach, or retain the material. Do not place sensitive content in issue trackers, source control, local notes, prompts, or telemetry. Retain only the minimum redacted report required by the authorized process.

## Evidence And Handoff

Use the [immediate-stop and retention checklist](../assets/immediate-stop-retention-checklist.md). If a restricted recipient is unavailable, report only that handoff is blocked; do not widen distribution.
