# Evidence And Source Admission

## Purpose And Preconditions

Establish whether an observation can be documented without expanding access or exposing sensitive material. Require explicit authorization, an in-scope source, and a designated restricted recipient.

## Documentation Methodology

Record the authorization reference, a redacted locator, observation time, source type, and the smallest description of the exposure boundary. Describe what was observable without copying values, payloads, headers, or adjacent sensitive content.

## Privacy And Scope Controls

Do not browse beyond the reported location, retrieve related material, preserve raw responses, or reproduce the observation in tickets, chat, logs, or screenshots. Stop if the source is out of scope or the disclosure route is absent.

## Evidence And Handoff

Mark the record restricted. Transfer only the redacted locator and boundary description to the named recipient. State whether the source is directly observed, reporter-supplied, or unverified.
