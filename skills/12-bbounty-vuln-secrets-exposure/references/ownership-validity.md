# Ownership And Validity Uncertainty

## Purpose And Preconditions

Prevent unsupported claims about who owns material or whether it works. Use whenever an exposure report could affect remediation priority, notification, or third parties.

## Documentation Methodology

Record ownership as `unknown`, `reported`, or `owner-confirmed`, with the source of that status. Record validity as `not assessed` unless an authorized owner supplies an independent confirmation. Separate observed facts from assumptions.

## Privacy And Scope Controls

Do not contact inferred owners, external providers, or third parties without authorization. Do not attempt to establish validity, scope, privilege, or attribution by using the material. Treat uncertainty as a reason for restricted escalation, not further investigation.

## Evidence And Handoff

Include the uncertainty labels and supporting authorization or owner-confirmation reference. Ask the authorized recipient to determine ownership, rotation, revocation, and impact through their internal process.
