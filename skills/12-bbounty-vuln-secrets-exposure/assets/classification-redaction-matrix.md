# Classification And Redaction Matrix

| Classification | Record | Redaction | Handling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Suspected | Minimal contextual observation | Omit material and nearby content | Restricted triage |
| Potentially sensitive | Boundary and reason for concern | Omit material, identifiers, and raw artifacts | Immediate restricted handoff |
| Confirmed exposure | Owner confirmation reference only | Omit all material and confirmation payload | Restricted remediation owner |
| Out of scope | Scope reason and redacted locator | Omit material | Stop and notify authorized coordinator |

Validity remains not assessed unless independently confirmed by the authorized owner.
