# Immediate-Stop And Retention Checklist

- [ ] Stop inspection and do not expand the source boundary.
- [ ] Do not use, copy, quote, screenshot, download, attach, or transmit the material.
- [ ] Record only authorization, redacted locator, time, and minimal boundary.
- [ ] Mark ownership and validity uncertainty explicitly.
- [ ] Send the minimized record through the designated restricted channel.
- [ ] Retain only the redacted report required by the authorized process.
- [ ] Mark the case blocked if an authorized recipient or channel is unavailable.
