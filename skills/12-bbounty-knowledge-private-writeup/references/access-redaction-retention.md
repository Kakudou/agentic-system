# Access, Redaction, and Retention

## Purpose and Preconditions

Apply minimum-necessary handling to private writeup material. Require a classification, approved recipient/channel, access boundary, and retention or disposal rule before handoff.

## Documentation Methodology

Record whether each item is a restricted original, reviewed redacted derivative, or metadata only. State what was withheld, why the retained form is necessary, who may access it, and when it must be reviewed or disposed of.

## Privacy and False-Claim Controls

Redaction must be manual, reviewable, and sufficient to prevent disclosure or procedural reconstruction. Do not place credentials, session material, personal data, private content, or unapproved identifiers in the narrative. If redaction cannot safely preserve the supported fact, use metadata only or stop.

## Evidence and Handoff

Attach classification, redaction decision, reviewer, access restriction, and retention disposition to the handoff. Recipient access does not authorize redistribution or additional testing.
