# Evidence and Scope Admission

## Purpose and Preconditions

Decide whether already-observed material may support a private writeup. Require written authorization, current in-scope target or system boundary, finding ID, provenance, approved audience, and retention rule. If any is unknown, stop and record only permitted handling metadata.

## Documentation Methodology

For each candidate artifact, record its identifier, source, observation window, admitted form, supported claim, exclusions, and admission decision. Prefer a reference or metadata over content. Do not collect, reopen, replay, or replace an artifact.

## Privacy and False-Claim Controls

Do not admit material because it could be useful later. Treat uncertain ownership, sensitive content, and out-of-scope material as restricted. Admission does not establish correctness, impact, exploitability, or permission for follow-up work.

## Evidence and Handoff

Place the admission decision and limitations in the claim/evidence matrix. A stopped or metadata-only decision is a valid handoff outcome.
