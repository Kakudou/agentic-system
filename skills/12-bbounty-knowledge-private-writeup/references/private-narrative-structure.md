# Private Narrative Structure

## Purpose and Preconditions

Create a clear private record from admitted evidence. Begin only after scope admission, classification, and audience restrictions are known.

## Documentation Methodology

Use this order: administrative context; bounded observation; evidence references; factual interpretation; limitations and alternatives; handling restrictions; requested decision. Write observations in past tense and identify their source. Keep claims atomic and distinguish direct observation from reviewer-facing analysis.

## Privacy and False-Claim Controls

Use neutral descriptions rather than sensitive values or procedural detail. Do not imply repetition, impact, access, attribution, or remediation success that the evidence does not establish. Mark unknowns and competing explanations explicitly.

## Evidence and Handoff

Every factual statement should map to an artifact ID or be labeled interpretation. Deliver the narrative with its matrix and handling boundary, not as a standalone assertion.
