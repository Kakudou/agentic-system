# Claim and Evidence Boundaries

## Purpose and Preconditions

Prevent a private writeup from converting incomplete evidence into a finding claim. Require admitted artifact references and a defined observation boundary.

## Documentation Methodology

For each claim, record its type as `observed`, `corroborated`, or `inconclusive`; the exact supporting artifact IDs; relevant context; and limitations. Keep facts separate from interpretation, requested review, and unverified consequences.

## Privacy and False-Claim Controls

An artifact does not alone prove severity, exploitability, ownership, or impact. Do not fill gaps with common patterns, assumptions, or likely outcomes. Remove or generalize sensitive detail that is not necessary for the claim.

## Evidence and Handoff

Use the claim/evidence matrix as the review surface. Escalate mismatched claims, missing provenance, or unsupported conclusions rather than repairing them through inference.
