# Validation and Knowledge Handoff

## Purpose and Preconditions

Prepare a reviewable private package without turning documentation into validation, publication, or authorization to act. Require completed claim mapping, handling decisions, and an approved recipient/channel.

## Documentation Methodology

Review each claim against its cited evidence and label the package `reviewed`, `draft`, or `stopped`. Include scope, provenance, classification, limitations, and the exact decision or authorization requested. Preserve useful general lessons only when they remain non-sensitive and do not reveal target-specific details.

## Privacy and False-Claim Controls

Do not represent reviewer receipt as acceptance, remediation, confirmation, or permission for further work. Do not transfer restricted originals into a general knowledge base. Stop when recipient access, retention, or factual support is unresolved.

## Evidence and Handoff

Use the restricted handoff template. Hand off the smallest permitted package, the matrix, and explicit follow-up boundary. Retain only according to the stated policy.
