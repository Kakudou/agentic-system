# Claim And Uncertainty Review

## Purpose And Preconditions

Use to assess material statements in an authorized draft against admitted evidence. A claim must have an ID or a clear draft location and linked evidence references.

## Documentation Methodology

Classify each statement as supported observation, bounded inference, unsupported, or withheld. State the boundary of a supported observation, the assumption behind an inference, and the reason for uncertainty, such as incomplete context, ambiguous scope, unavailable evidence, or a competing explanation.

## False-Claim And Privacy Controls

Do not turn an observed condition into a claim of access, data exposure, business harm, or exploitability without direct support. Do not replace uncertainty with a probability, severity label, or confident wording. Keep restricted details out of the draft.

## Evidence And Handoff

Record the evidence IDs and limitation for every material claim. Hand unsupported claims back as revisions or escalate them for authorized evidence review; never silently upgrade their status.
