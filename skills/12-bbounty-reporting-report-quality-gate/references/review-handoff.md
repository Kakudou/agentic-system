# Review Handoff

## Purpose And Preconditions

Use to transfer the review package to an authorized recipient. Require a named recipient or approved channel, completed claim/evidence mapping, handling restrictions, and a stated decision request.

## Documentation Methodology

Hand off the draft identifier, permitted evidence references, claim statuses, redaction decisions, limitations, revision items, and requested decision. Use one of three outcomes: ready for authorized review, revision required, or stopped for authorization or safety.

## False-Claim And Privacy Controls

Review is not approval, acceptance, severity determination, remediation verification, or submission. Share only permitted material and keep restricted originals outside the handoff package.

## Evidence And Handoff

Use the review handoff template. Record the recipient, access constraints, outcome, and unanswered questions. A recipient response documents their decision only; it does not prove unsupported technical claims.
