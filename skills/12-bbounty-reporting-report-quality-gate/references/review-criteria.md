# Review Criteria

## Purpose And Preconditions

Use for a supplied, authorized report draft with an identified finding and reviewer. It establishes factual-review criteria, not a score, rating, submission rule, or acceptance decision.

## Documentation Methodology

Review whether the title, affected boundary, observation, evidence references, limitations, impact framing, and requested remediation are understandable and distinguish facts from interpretation. Record missing or ambiguous content as a revision item, citing the affected claim or section.

## False-Claim And Privacy Controls

Do not infer facts from a polished narrative, template field, or finding label. Do not add operational detail, secrets, personal data, or unreviewed evidence to make a draft appear complete.

## Evidence And Handoff

Link each review item to a claim ID, draft location, or evidence ID. Hand off only the outcome and bounded revisions; unresolved technical questions remain unresolved.
