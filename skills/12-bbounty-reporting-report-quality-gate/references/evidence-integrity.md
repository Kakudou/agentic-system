# Evidence Integrity And Provenance

## Purpose And Preconditions

Use when a report cites supplied artifacts. Require authorization, scope, an evidence identifier, origin or custodian, observation time or window when known, and handling restrictions.

## Documentation Methodology

For each cited artifact, record its ID, class, provenance, relation to the claim, permitted access path, and whether it is original, redacted, or metadata only. Admit the minimum material needed to support the stated observation.

## False-Claim And Privacy Controls

Filenames, summaries, and context-free screenshots do not establish technical facts. Do not copy credentials, tokens, session data, personal data, customer content, or unrelated system information into the review package. Mark incomplete provenance as a limitation.

## Evidence And Handoff

Ensure the claim/evidence matrix points to authorized artifacts. Escalate missing provenance, restricted access that prevents review, or a need for new evidence; do not collect, alter, or replay artifacts.
