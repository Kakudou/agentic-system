# Remediation Review

## Purpose And Preconditions

Use to review whether the draft separates observed conditions, potential impact, and corrective framing. Require an authorized factual draft and claim-to-evidence mapping; this reference does not authorize reproduction, verification, or implementation testing.

## Documentation Methodology

Describe the observed condition first. State impact only at its documented confidence boundary. Review proposed remediation as a risk-reduction objective tied to the condition, noting assumptions, owners, or validation needs without declaring that a change will resolve the issue.

## False-Claim And Privacy Controls

Do not present a remediation suggestion as a confirmed fix, a guarantee of impact, or evidence that exploitation occurred. Avoid payloads, commands, credentials, or procedural detail that creates unnecessary exploitation guidance.

## Evidence And Handoff

Link condition and impact statements to evidence IDs; mark remediation as advisory unless an authorized owner confirms it. Hand unresolved feasibility, ownership, and validation questions to the appropriate reviewer.
