# Redaction And Stop Controls

## Purpose And Preconditions

Use before distributing a draft, worksheet, or evidence reference. Require the intended recipient, evidence handling rules, and applicable privacy or retention constraints.

## Documentation Methodology

Redact only the minimum necessary content and label the category: credential, session material, personal data, customer content, or sensitive infrastructure detail. Preserve enough non-sensitive context to understand the claim. State uncertainty and its cause directly.

## False-Claim And Privacy Controls

Never claim a redacted value has a particular meaning unless an authorized reviewer can verify the restricted original. Do not fabricate realistic-looking placeholders. Stop and escalate for exposed secrets, personal data, unclear authorization, unsafe scope, or redaction that prevents meaningful review.

## Evidence And Handoff

Record withheld material, redaction category, approved access path, and escalation recipient. A stop outcome is a safety handoff, not a rejection or technical conclusion.
