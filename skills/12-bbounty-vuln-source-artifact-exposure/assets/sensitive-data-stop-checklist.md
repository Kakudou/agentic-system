# Sensitive-Data Stop Checklist

- Stop further viewing immediately.
- Do not reload, download, copy, decode, validate, authenticate, or share the material.
- Do not retain screenshots, request logs, browser exports, or payload excerpts containing it.
- Record only the observation time, redacted locator, broad data category, and stop action.
- Notify the designated restricted recipient using the approved channel.
- Request handling instructions; do not continue without explicit authorization.
- Keep reusable references and general tracking systems free of sensitive content.

Possible categories include credentials, tokens, private keys, session material, personal data, private source, customer data, and internal operational details.
