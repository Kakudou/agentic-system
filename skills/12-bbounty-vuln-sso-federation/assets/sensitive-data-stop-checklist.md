# Sensitive-Data and Stop Checklist

- [ ] Scope includes the relying party, IdP, environment, and designated test account.
- [ ] Evidence excludes or redacts cookies, codes, tokens, assertions, passwords, subject values, email addresses, and group membership unless explicitly required and protected.
- [ ] Stop contact and secure evidence destination are recorded.
- [ ] Stop immediately for third-party data, unexpected privilege, tenant crossover, real-user access, unlisted provider, or ambiguous ownership.
- [ ] Do not alter account links, identity attributes, authentication artifacts, or production state.
- [ ] Record the stop reason, evidence already retained, and requested controlled next step.
