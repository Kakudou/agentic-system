# Validation And Ethics Controls

## Purpose

Verify that a review order is traceable, authorized, and safe to hand off for planning.

## Preconditions

An admitted-hypothesis list, review-order rationale, and current program rules exist.

## Evidence-Led Method

Check each item for an in-scope asset, linked observations, stated assumptions, an uncertainty note, and a stop condition. A second reviewer should inspect exclusions, ties, conflicts, and items relying on a single source when feasible.

## Interpretation And Controls

Validation confirms documentation quality and authorization alignment, not whether a vulnerability exists. Reject unsupported certainty, severity, payout, or exploit claims. Correct ordering only when the evidence record supports the change.

## Scope And Privacy

Do not plan denial-of-service, social engineering, persistence, data extraction, or other sensitive activity unless the written program authorization explicitly permits it and the planning owner accepts the constraints.

## Evidence And Handoff

Record validation status, reviewer, unresolved concerns, and the exact scope constraints that the planning owner must preserve.
