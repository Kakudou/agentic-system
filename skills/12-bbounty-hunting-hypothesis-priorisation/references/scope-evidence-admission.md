# Scope And Evidence Admission

## Purpose

Set the minimum conditions for a hypothesis to enter review.

## Preconditions

Current written program rules, an identifiable asset or feature, and sourceable observations are available.

## Evidence-Led Method

Admit a hypothesis only when its target is explicitly in scope, its proposed review is allowed, and each supporting observation has a source and locator. Record hypotheses as excluded when scope disallows them; record them as deferred when a needed fact or permission is absent.

## Interpretation And Controls

Scope silence is not permission. Treat stale scope statements, redirects, shared infrastructure, and third-party services as unresolved until the program clarifies them.

## Scope And Privacy

Respect program rules, rate limits, account restrictions, data boundaries, and all applicable law. Do not access, retain, or disclose data beyond authorization.

## Evidence And Handoff

Provide the scope citation, evidence references, admission decision, and open questions in the planning handoff.
