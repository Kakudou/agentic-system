# Scope And Stop Checklist

- [ ] Target and proposed activity are explicitly authorized.
- [ ] Evidence is traceable and minimally retained.
- [ ] Account, rate, data, and third-party restrictions are known.
- [ ] No planned review requires unapproved state change, load, or sensitive-data access.
- [ ] Unknown material facts are marked `defer`.
- [ ] A stop condition and escalation contact are recorded.
- [ ] Conflicts and bias concerns are disclosed.
